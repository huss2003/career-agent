Smile Lounge | Narre Warren, Melbourne, Australia | Remote | Project-based, minimum 10 hours a week guaranteed

**You don’t just chat with ChatGPT or Claude. You’ve built things with them: automations that run while you sleep, small web apps people actually use, workflows that turned a two-hour job into five minutes. You want a role where that’s the whole job.**

Smile Lounge is a premium boutique dental practice in Melbourne with a team of 15+.

Our owner already builds with AI: we run an AI phone-training app for our receptionists, AI consult-prep and assessment tools for clinicians, an AI scribe for clinical notes, and data pipelines for practice analytics.

We’ve reached the point where we need someone to take this over: build the next tools, keep the current ones running, and be the person our team comes to when they need something automated.

**What you’ll own**

- **Build**: take a task from our team (“we copy this from X to Y every morning”) and turn it into a working automation or tool.
- **Maintain**: own the web apps and automations we’ve already built. Fix, upgrade, monitor and improve them.
- **Support**: be the team’s go-to person for workflows. Help staff build, troubleshoot and improve their own AI workflows.
- **Connect**: integrate our systems, including our practice software (CareStack, via API), our CRM (GoHighLevel), call analytics and spreadsheets.
- Document: every build gets a short write-up or video walkthrough so it keeps running when you’re on leave.

**The kind of projects waiting for you**

- A live KPI dashboard built on the CareStack API, replacing reports we don’t trust.
- Automated follow-up and reminder workflows in our CRM.
- Upgrading and maintaining our AI phone-training and consult tools.
- Turning recurring admin (reports, data clean-up, call-list preparation) into one-click or zero-click jobs.

**You’re who we want if you**

- Use AI tools at an advanced level: ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini or similar. You use projects, custom GPTs and agents, you know how to get reliable output, and you know where these tools fail.
- Have built real automations and can show us (Make, n8n, Zapier, GoHighLevel workflows, Apps Script, Python or similar).
- Understand how the pieces fit: APIs, webhooks, JSON, databases. When something breaks at 7am, you can find out why.
- Explain technology in plain English to a receptionist or a dentist without making them feel slow.
- Figure things out yourself, then document how you did it.

**Moves you to the top of the pile**

- You’ve built and deployed web apps or software using Claude Code, OpenAI Codex, Cursor or similar AI coding tools, and people use them to work faster.
- You’ve used AI agents that take actions across apps (e.g. ChatGPT agent, Claude with connected tools) to complete real work.
- Git/GitHub and deployment experience (Vercel, Netlify, Supabase, Firebase or similar).
- Healthcare experience, or experience handling sensitive data.

**Working with patient data**

You’ll work around private patient information, so you’ll design with privacy in mind: approved tools and accounts only, least-access permissions, and nothing sensitive pasted into personal AI accounts. Changes to live systems are tested and agreed with us before go-live. If that’s already how you work, you’ll fit right in.

**This probably isn’t for you if**

- Your AI experience is mainly writing content or social media posts.
- You need a detailed specification before you start any task.
- You’d rather build alone and don’t enjoy helping people use what you build.

We watch every walkthrough before we read any CVs. Applications without a walkthrough video won’t be reviewed.

- **Record a 3–5 minute screen-share walkthrough (Loom or similar) of something you built:** the problem, how it works, the tools you used, and the result (time saved, errors reduced, etc.). Blur or remove any client data.
- Email your CV, walkthrough link and any portfolio, GitHub or live-app links to **apply.bds@gmail.com** with the subject line **“Builder — \[Your Name]”**.
