We're looking for a senior full-stack developer for a 3 to 6 month contract.

### About the role

- This is a 3 to 6 month contract role, remote or hybrid from our Sydney studio, open to candidates based in Australia and overseas, with the possibility of extension depending on project needs and mutual fit.
- You'll be building Rally, our own proposal platform. The product is already in active development, with a React + TypeScript frontend and a NestJS + TypeScript API, and we need someone who can hit the ground running across both codebases.
- This isn't just a heads-down coding role. We want someone who works closely with our designers, brings strong product and design sensibility, and can help us build something that feels genuinely best-in-class. You should care deeply about craft: clean interfaces, thoughtful interactions, and polished details.
- We also want someone who's comfortable leveraging AI tools (we already use Cursor) to accelerate development, explore new features rapidly, and help the team adopt smarter workflows. You'll be expected to simplify our deployment processes, introduce best practices, and leave the team in a stronger position than where you found it.

### Working with our design team

- Our team includes senior product designers who are strong in design and product thinking but are looking to deepen their collaboration with engineering. A key part of this role is bridging the gap between design and development: helping our designers understand technical possibilities and constraints, and building a shared language so they can contribute more effectively to the product build process.
- You'll be helping to shape how our design and engineering functions work together long after this engagement ends.

### What you’ll be working with

- Frontend (rally-ui): React 18, TypeScript, Vite, Tailwind CSS v4, Radix UI Themes, React Aria Components, TanStack React Query, Formik + Yup, React Router, Playwright (testing)
- Backend (rally-api): NestJS, TypeScript, TypeORM, PostgreSQL, AWS (S3, SES, SQS, SNS), Stripe, OpenAI, Passport + JWT auth, CASL (authorization), Swagger, AdminJS, Jest (testing)
- Infrastructure and tooling: Docker, GitHub Actions, SonarQube, Sentry, Cursor AI, Husky + lint-staged

### What you will do

The daily life of a Senior Full-Stack Developer at UntilNow looks something like this:

- Build and ship features across both the frontend and backend, working comfortably in React and NestJS.
- Work agent first. Use Claude Code, Cursor and similar coding agents to plan, build, test and refactor, while owning the review, architecture and quality of everything that ships.
- Help shape Rally's own AI features: prompt design, structured outputs, guardrails, evals, and keeping an eye on cost and latency.
- Collaborate directly with our senior product designers to translate designs into high-fidelity, production-ready UI, and push back constructively when needed.
- Upskill our design team on how to collaborate more effectively with engineering, from design handoff and component structure to using AI tools to prototype ideas themselves.
- Simplify and improve our deployment pipeline, CI/CD workflows, and developer experience, including the guardrails (tests, linting, review) that make agent-written code safe to merge.
- Introduce and teach best practices around code architecture, testing, performance, and maintainability.
- Take ownership of technical decisions and help shape the direction of the product.
- Write clean, well-structured, documented code that the team (and their agents) can build on after the engagement ends.
