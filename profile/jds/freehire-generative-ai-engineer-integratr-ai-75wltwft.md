**Company Description** Integratr.ai builds AI assistants and automation workflows for tuition and enrichment centers, turning WhatsApp inquiries into booked trials and seamlessly managing student, class, and payment records in one place. The platform is designed for centers running weekly classes with recurring fees, helping them automate administrative busywork without expanding headcount. Its WhatsApp AI assistant responds to parents using center-specific data, escalates to staff when needed, and runs the same pipelines in production as in testing. A separate center AI assistant enables staff to ask plain-language questions, view metrics, and perform actions such as creating students or recording payments, all with strict permissions and audit trails. Automation workflows further streamline fee generation, reminders, payment matching, and receipts, allowing centers to keep the entire family lifecycle in a single, organized system.

**Role Description** The Generative AI Engineer will design, build, and optimize AI assistants and automation workflows that power Integratr.ai’s tuition and enrichment center platform. This full-time remote role involves developing and integrating generative AI models, crafting retrieval and orchestration pipelines, and ensuring robust performance in production environments. Day-to-day work includes implementing conversational AI features for WhatsApp and internal assistants, integrating with data sources and payment systems, and building workflows to automate fee generation, reminders, and other administrative tasks. The engineer will collaborate closely with product and operations teams to translate business requirements into technical solutions, monitor AI usage and costs, and continuously improve reliability, safety, and user experience. The role also includes maintaining clear documentation, contributing to architectural decisions, and participating in code reviews and experiments to refine AI capabilities.
