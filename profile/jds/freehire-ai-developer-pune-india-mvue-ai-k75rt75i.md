**About Us:**

At Mvue, we believe the future of work belongs to intelligent systems that empower people—not replace them. We are building an Agentic AI platform that deploys autonomous digital employees to take on the operational burden so human teams can focus on the work that truly moves organizations forward. Our agents streamline critical workflows across healthcare, tech and enterprise operations—from patient intake and eligibility to billing, AR collections, and cash management—bringing unmatched accuracy, consistency, and scale. Driven by a mission to redefine what teams can achieve, we're creating a world where operations run smarter, people are supported by AI that works as hard as they do, and organizations can grow without limits.

**Position Overview:**

We're looking for a seasoned Senior Python & GenAI Developer to design and deliver robust Python applications and advanced Agentic AI workflows. You'll lead technical initiatives, mentor junior developers, and work closely with cross-functional teams to align engineering output with business goals.

**What You'll Do:**

- Build and maintain scalable Python applications and services.
- Develop production-ready Agentic AI workflows and multi‑model RAG pipelines (e.g., LangGraph), including MCP-based low-latency tool integrations.
- Lead technical design, code reviews, and best-practice implementation.
- Collaborate with teams to define requirements and ensure solution alignment with business needs.
- Troubleshoot, optimize, and test applications for reliability and performance.
- Maintain clear documentation and drive continuous improvement.
- Stay updated on modern Python, AI, and cloud technologies.

On Your First Day, We'll Expect You to Have:

- 3–7 years of Python development with solid OOP skills.
- Experience with Django/Flask/FastAPI and building RESTful APIs.
- Strong grasp of system architecture and software design patterns.
- Hands-on experience with SQL databases (PostgreSQL/MySQL); NoSQL exposure is a plus.
- Familiarity with AWS/Azure, Docker, and Python data libraries (Pandas, NumPy, Scikit-learn).
- Strong debugging, analytical, and problem‑solving abilities.
- Ability to integrate diverse data sources, learn quickly, and communicate effectively.
