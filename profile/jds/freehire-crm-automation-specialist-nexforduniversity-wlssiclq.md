**CRM & Automation Specialist** · Remote · Full-time contractor · Reports to the Marketing & Automation Lead

**About Nexford**

Nexford University is an accredited, fully online university headquartered in Washington, DC, with learners in more than 100 countries. We offer career-focused business and technology degrees, built with employers around the skills they actually hire for, at a fraction of the cost of a traditional degree. Following our Series A, we’re growing fast, including expansion into the US and Canada. We’re AI-native: our admissions, sales and marketing teams run on automation and AI agents. The bottleneck is no longer spotting what to improve. It’s shipping the improvements fast enough, and that’s where you come in.

**The role**

You turn briefs into working automations across our revenue engine. You build in HubSpot, connect systems through Make, and use Claude to write small scripts and tools. You’ll start mostly with the Sales team, then expand into Marketing. You won’t write strategy or copy. You’ll make things work, check them, document them, and move on to the next one.

**What you’ll do**

- Build and improve HubSpot workflows
- Build email sequences from a provided copy brief, and set cadence, enrollment and suppression rules
- Support our AI voice assistants: update prompts and maintain the workflows that connect them to HubSpot and other services
- Automate operational calculations such as sales commissions, with careful testing
- Build data-cleaning workflows so reports are accurate, including data pulled from several sources
- Use Claude / Claude Code to build small internal tools and scripts
- Document every build so anyone on the team can maintain it
- Spot broken or missing automations and propose fixes

**What you bring**

- 2–4 years in marketing ops, sales ops, RevOps or automation roles
- Hands-on CRM experience (HubSpot preferred; Salesforce or similar transfers)
- Hands-on middleware experience: Make, Zapier or n8n
- Working knowledge of APIs, webhooks and JSON
- At least one small app or service you built with an AI coding agent for a real business use case
- A habit of testing before you switch anything on, and asking before you assume

**Nice to have**

- HubSpot or Make certification
- Exposure to GitHub, Vercel, Supabase or AWS
- Experience with AI voice agents or LLM prompting
- Background in education, contact centers or B2C sales funnels
