## **Job Purpose**

We are looking for a passionate and motivated \** Machine Learning Engineer\** to join our AI Engineering team.  
You will work on building intelligent AI solutions across Machine Learning, Computer Vision, Deep Learning, and Generative AI.  
This role provides an excellent opportunity to work on real-world enterprise AI applications, including Large Language Models (LLMs), Agentic AI systems, Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG),  
computer vision models, and scalable ML solutions deployed on cloud platforms.

You will collaborate with experienced AI engineers, software developers, and business stakeholders to design, develop, deploy, and improve AI-powered products.

## **Key Responsibilities**

Develop, train, evaluate, and optimize Machine Learning, Computer Vision and Deep Learning models for real-world business problems.  
\* Work on Generative AI applications using Large Language Models (LLMs), Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG), AI Agents, and advanced prompt engineering techniques.  
\* Develop AI workflows using modern frameworks and orchestration tools for enterprise applications.  
\* Explore, clean, preprocess, and analyze structured and unstructured datasets to improve model performance.  
\* Perform feature engineering, data validation, and model evaluation using appropriate performance metrics.  
\* Conduct experiments, compare different algorithms, and recommend the most suitable approaches.  
\* Deploy ML and AI models on Microsoft Azure and Databricks environments.  
\* Develop and integrate REST APIs for AI services and model inference.  
\* Collaborate with software engineers and product teams to integrate AI capabilities into production applications.  
\* Monitor deployed models and continuously improve model performance and reliability.  
\* Document experiments, model architectures, and technical implementations.  
\* Stay updated with the latest advancements in Machine Learning, Generative AI, and emerging AI technologies.
