**Shopify and AI Developer – Full-Time Remote**

AlphaPixels Media is a Canadian digital marketing agency serving clients across North America. We build complete growth systems for our clients: e-commerce stores, CRM pipelines, AI tools, and marketing automation. We are hiring a technical all-rounder who lives at the intersection of web development, automation, and AI, and who takes pride in clean, finished work.

**The role**

You will be the technical backbone of our client delivery. Your first project is a complete Shopify store build for an established North American business, followed by ongoing store builds, CRM setups, automation pipelines, and AI tool deployments across our client base. You take a brief and deliver working systems.

**What you will do**

- Build Shopify stores from scratch: theme setup, customization, custom sections (Liquid, HTML, CSS, JavaScript), payments, shipping, taxes, multi-currency
- Build conversion-focused product pages and landing pages
- Implement 301 redirect maps for site migrations without losing SEO rankings
- Set up and manage CRM systems: pipelines, calendars, forms, SMS and email sequences, workflow automations (GoHighLevel experience is a strong advantage)
- Build automations with Make (Integromat), Zapier, webhooks, and REST APIs connecting stores, CRMs, sheets, and ad platforms
- Deploy and configure AI tools: website chatbots, AI receptionists and voice agents, and content pipelines using OpenAI, Claude, or similar APIs
- Install tracking (Meta pixel, GA4, conversion APIs) and third-party widgets
- Maintain, troubleshoot, and improve everything you build

**Must have**

- 3+ years hands-on web development with live Shopify stores you personally built
- Strong Liquid, HTML, CSS, JavaScript
- Real experience building automations with Make, Zapier, or direct API integrations
- Working knowledge of AI APIs and prompt-based tools (chatbots, LLM integrations)
- Comfortable using AI coding tools (Claude Code, Cursor, or similar) in your daily workflow
- At least one full store migration or replatforming experience
- Strong written English and reliable daily communication
- Self-managed: you take a brief and return finished, tested work

**Strong advantage**

- GoHighLevel (sub-accounts, workflows, snapshots)
- Voice AI platforms (Vapi, Retell, ElevenLabs or similar)
- Shopify Markets / multi-currency
- Basic SEO (redirects, page speed, structured data)
- Figma to web workflow

**Hours**

Flexible, with a minimum 3-hour overlap with Canadian Central Time mornings (approximately 6:30 PM to 9:30 PM IST).
