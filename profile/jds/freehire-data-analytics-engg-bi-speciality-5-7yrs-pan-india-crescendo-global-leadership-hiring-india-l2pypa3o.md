**ROLE SUMMARY**

We are seeking a proactive Data Analytics Engineer with a BI specialty to design, build, and evolve high-impact dashboards for Visa business stakeholders. This role is dashboard-first: you will turn raw data into clear, intuitive, decision-ready experiences, applying strong UX/UI judgment and knowing the best way to represent each metric (chart choice, hierarchy, interactivity, and context). You will use AI tools (e.g., Claude, Copilot) as a daily accelerator to prototype dashboards in hours rather than days and to make fast, safe changes to the underlying SQL and data pipelines. You will work across a legacy Hadoop/Hive environment and the target AWS/Databricks platform, partnering directly with business users from requirement to adoption.

**KEY RESPONSIBILITIES**

- **Dashboard Design & Development:** Build, enhance, and maintain production Tableau dashboards (and other BI tools as needed) that are fast, intuitive, and aligned to business KPIs.
- **Metric Visualization & UX:** Choose the right visual for each metric (trend, comparison, distribution, variance, funnel, KPI card), apply sound layout, color, and accessibility principles, and design for the viewer’s decision, not the data.
- **AI-Accelerated Prototyping:** Use AI tools to generate wireframes, mock datasets, calculated fields, and working prototypes quickly; iterate with stakeholders in short feedback cycles before hardening for production.
- **SQL & Pipeline Changes:** Use AI-assisted workflows to write, tune, and review SQL and PySpark; efficiently modify upstream tables, views, and pipelines to support new metrics, with proper testing and validation.
- **Semantic & Data Modeling:** Design curated, reusable data models and certified data sources (star schemas, aggregates, extracts) that give users consistent, trusted metric definitions.
- **Performance & Reliability:** Diagnose and fix slow dashboards (extract optimization, query tuning, calculation and LOD refactoring, data-source design) and ensure data freshness and accuracy.
- **Stakeholder Partnership:** Gather requirements, translate business questions into metrics, run demos and UAT, and drive dashboard adoption through training, documentation, and usage feedback.
- **Platform & Migration Support:** Support the migration of reporting workloads from Hadoop/Hive to AWS and Databricks (Delta Lake, Unity Catalog), maintaining continuity for downstream dashboards.
- **Governance & Quality:** Apply data-quality checks, version control, access controls, and dashboard standards (naming, style guides, templates) in line with Visa’s security and compliance requirements.

**REQUIRED TECHNICAL SKILLS**

- **BI & Visualization:** Expert-level Tableau (Desktop and Server): calculated fields, LOD expressions, parameters, actions, dashboard performance tuning, and publishing/permissions. Equivalent depth in Power BI or Looker is acceptable.
- **Dashboard UX/UI & Data Storytelling:** Demonstrated portfolio of dashboards showing strong layout, visual hierarchy, chart selection, and accessibility; able to justify design choices for each metric.
- **SQL:** Advanced SQL (window functions, CTEs, complex joins, query tuning) across Hive/Spark SQL and cloud warehouses.
- **AI-Assisted Development:** Proven, hands-on use of LLM tools (Anthropic Claude, GitHub Copilot, or similar) to prototype dashboards, generate and refactor SQL/Python, and accelerate analysis, with disciplined validation of AI output.
- **Data Engineering Fundamentals:** Python (PySpark) for data transformation; working knowledge of Apache Spark and distributed concepts (partitioning, shuffling, caching, broadcast joins).
- **Cloud & Platform:** Experience working with Hadoop/Hive environments and experience on AWS (S3, IAM, Glue) and Databricks (Delta Lake, SQL warehouses).
- **Data Modeling:** Dimensional modeling and building BI-ready datasets, aggregates, and extracts.
- **Engineering Practices:** Git/version control, testing and data validation, and workflow orchestration awareness (Airflow, Control-M).

**PREFERRED / NICE-TO-HAVE SKILLS**

- Building AI-assisted analytics features: natural-language querying, LLM-generated insights or summaries, or lightweight AI agents (LangChain, Mosaic AI).
