**About Flexxd**

How people live has changed. Hybrid work, moving between neighborhoods, travel, wanting variety. Flexibility is the default for everything else they pay for, but gym access still means one location and a long contract.

Based in NYC, Flexxd is a flexible fitness access platform that connects fitness enthusiasts with premium gyms through a simple, pay-as-you-go model, eliminating long-term contracts and subscriptions. The platform enables users to discover and book nearby gyms, filtering by price, activities, equipment, and amenities to match their individual fitness goals. Flexxd’s mission is to empower people to manage their own fitness journey and maintain an active lifestyle wherever they are, whether traveling, trying new gyms, or seeking variety.

And people are loving it! We launched last year and live in NY & NJ with a rapidly growing partner network in the biggest fitness market in the US.
