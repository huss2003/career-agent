Required Skills: - Proficiency in Python and experience with AI frameworks. - Strong understanding of generative AI concepts and technologies. - Experience with large language models (LLMs) and related tools. - Familiarity with container orchestration using Kubernetes. - Knowledge of SQL and database management. - Experience with CI/CD practices and tools. - Familiarity with cloud platforms such as AWS. - Strong problem-solving skills and ability to work in a collaborative environment.
