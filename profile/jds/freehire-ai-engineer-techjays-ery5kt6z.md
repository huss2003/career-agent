About Techjays

Techjays is an AI Reimagination Company focused on building intelligent, scalable, and impactful software solutions. We help businesses solve complex challenges through AI, automation, and modern software engineering.

We are looking for an AI Engineer who is passionate about building AI-powered applications and integrating modern AI technologies into real-world products.

Key Responsibilities

- Design, develop, and deploy AI-powered applications using Python and modern AI frameworks.
- Build and implement LLM-based applications, including Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG) systems.
- Work with LLM APIs such as OpenAI, Gemini, and Anthropic.
- Develop AI agents and workflows using frameworks such as LangChain and LangGraph.
- Integrate vector databases and implement efficient embedding and semantic search solutions.
- Develop scalable backend services and REST APIs.
- Work with databases, data processing pipelines, and cloud-based services.
- Collaborate with frontend and backend teams to integrate AI capabilities into applications.
- Evaluate AI model performance, improve response accuracy, and optimize application performance.
- Debug, test, and maintain production-grade AI applications.

Required Skills

- 2–4 years of experience in software development, AI engineering, or a related field.
- Strong programming knowledge in Python.
- Hands-on experience with LLMs and Generative AI applications.
- Understanding of RAG architecture, embeddings, and semantic search.
- Experience with frameworks such as LangChain, LangGraph, or LlamaIndex.
- Knowledge of vector databases such as Pinecone, FAISS, ChromaDB, or Qdrant.
