**YOUR MISSION**

**About the Organisation & Role:**

At PUMA, technology plays a critical role in enabling exceptional consumer, digital, and business experiences across global markets. Our engineering teams build and operate the platforms, products, and services that power PUMA's digital ecosystem, supporting consumers, business users, and operational teams worldwide.

As part of the Engineering organization, you will contribute to the development and evolution of modern digital platforms and applications that support key business capabilities across eCommerce, consumer engagement, retail, merchandising, supply chain, data, and enterprise domains. The organization embraces cloud-native architecture, engineering excellence, automation, AI-assisted development, and a product-centric mindset to deliver scalable and impactful solutions.

As a Software Engineer (Full Stack), you will build and maintain modern web applications and backend services that power business-critical and consumer-facing experiences. You will work closely with Product Managers, Designers, Architects, Data Engineers, and fellow engineers to deliver intuitive, scalable, secure, and high-performing solutions while contributing to engineering best practices across the organization.

**What you will do:**

- Design, build, and maintain modern web applications and supporting backend services that power business-critical and consumer-facing experiences.
- Drive the technical design and delivery of complex frontend features and end-to-end user journeys.
- Develop scalable, reusable, and maintainable UI architectures, components, and shared libraries using React and TypeScript.
- Collaborate with backend, platform, data, and architecture teams to deliver integrated and scalable solutions.
- Build and enhance backend APIs and microservices required to support frontend experiences and business workflows.
- Optimize application performance, accessibility, reliability, security, and overall user experience.
- Contribute to architecture discussions, technical design reviews, and engineering best practices.
- Troubleshoot complex production issues and drive improvements in observability and operational excellence.
- Partner closely with product managers, designers, and engineering teams to deliver impactful business outcomes.
- Mentor junior engineers through code reviews, technical guidance, and knowledge sharing.
- Leverage automation, AI-assisted engineering tools, and modern development practices to improve development productivity and quality.
- Contribute to continuous improvement of engineering standards, development processes, and platform capabilities.

**YOUR TALENT**

- 5-7 years of experience designing, building, and operating modern web applications and full-stack software solutions.
- Strong expertise in React, TypeScript, JavaScript, and modern frontend engineering practices.
- Experience building scalable, responsive, and high-performing web applications with a strong focus on user experience and accessibility.
- Strong understanding of component-based architecture, state management, frontend design patterns, and design-system-driven development.
- Hands-on experience integrating frontend applications with REST APIs, microservices, and backend services.
- Good understanding of backend development using Java, Spring Boot, API design, and microservices architecture.
- Experience working with relational and NoSQL databases, caching technologies, and cloud-native applications on Azure, AWS, or GCP.
- Exposure to containerization and orchestration technologies such as Docker and Kubernetes.
- Experience building AI-powered and conversational user experiences using frameworks such as Google Conversational Commerce Kit (CCK), Vercel AI SDK, CopilotKit, LangChain/LangGraph, OpenAI SDK, or similar agentic application frameworks.
- Familiarity with agentic workflows, tool calling, streaming interactions, conversational experiences, and Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG) patterns within modern web applications.
- Familiarity with search technologies such as Elasticsearch, OpenSearch, Azure AI Search, or similar platforms.
- Strong understanding of application performance optimization, observability, security, and operational excellence.
- Experience owning medium-to-large features and contributing to technical design discussions and engineering best practices.
