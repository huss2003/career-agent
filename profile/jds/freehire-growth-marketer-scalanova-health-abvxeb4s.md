**About Scalanova Health**

Scalanova Health builds and operates AI-native Global Capability Centers (GCCs) and specialized engineering pods for biotechnology, medical device, and healthcare technology platforms across North America and Europe. Governed by clinical and scientific practitioners, we engineer high-performance solutions across Drug Discovery, MedTech Innovation, Revenue Operations, and Agentic AI.

**The Opportunity**

We are looking for an execution-focused B2B Growth & Inbound Marketing Specialist who brings solid experience in B2B MarTech SaaS, marketing automation products, or digital health platforms.

In this role, you will own our inbound conversion engine: building high-converting WordPress web assets, setting up automated lead capture workflows, and leveraging modern generative AI tools (Gemini, Claude, ChatGPT) to produce sharp B2B messaging. Because our core engagements are high-value enterprise GCC setups and engineering pods, your PPC and growth skills will also power client growth pods and new service incubation.

**Your Primary Responsibilities:**

- Build & Manage Web Assets: Design, publish, and iterate conversion-focused landing pages in WordPress (Elementor) showcasing our GCC models and clinical pillars. Maintain fast page speeds and clean UX.
- Lead Routing & Speed-to-Lead Workflows: Integrate web forms with HubSpot/CRM, configure instant notifications, and embed automated meeting schedulers on key conversion paths such as our 90-Day Growth Gateway Roadmap.
- MarTech Architecture: Utilize your knowledge of marketing automation and software products to optimize visitor journeys and inbound attribution.
- Strategic Growth & PPC Execution: Utilize Google Ads and performance marketing expertise for client growth pods, product incubation, and targeted enterprise search intent.
- AI-Assisted Content Production: Leverage Gemini, Claude, and ChatGPT to rapidly draft landing page copy, value propositions, and thought-leadership assets for executive polish.
- Attribution & Tag Hygiene: Configure GTM events, GA4 conversion tracking, and Search Console monitoring to ensure full funnel visibility.
