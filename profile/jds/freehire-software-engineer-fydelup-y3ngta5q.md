**About Fydelup**

Fydelup is a French software company that helps businesses bring their past customers back through WhatsApp and email campaigns. We are a small, fully remote team, and we build AI agents and automations that run in production every day.

**The role**

As a Software Engineer at Fydelup, you will build our product and the AI agents and automations behind it. Day to day, you will:

- Design and ship back-end features: APIs, integrations, data pipelines
- Build AI agents and workflow automations on top of LLM APIs
- Write reliable, tested code and review your teammates' code
- Fix production issues and improve our tools and documentation
- Work closely with product and design to turn business needs into working software

**What we are looking for**

- A degree in Computer Science or a related field, or equivalent experience
- 2+ years of professional software development
- Solid back-end skills: at least one modern server framework (Node.js, Python...) and REST/JSON APIs
- Good knowledge of SQL or NoSQL databases and Git
- Experience with LLM APIs, AI agents or workflow automation is a strong plus
- Good written English; French is a plus

**What we offer**

- Salary: €70,000 to €80,000 per year, full-time, 100% remote from India
- Real ownership: you ship features that customers use every day
- A small team where your work has direct impact
