Specright is the first purpose-built, patented platform for Specification Management. Whether it's packaging, raw materials, formulas, products, or machines, Specright helps companies digitize, map, and take action across their supply chain to reduce costs, increase profitability, and drive sustainability. Specright serves customers across industries, including packaging, food and beverage, consumer packaged goods, pharmaceutical, retail, industrials, and more.

**Role Overview:**

Specright is looking for a Customer Success AI Engineer to be the first hire for the next stage of the Customer Success org’s AI strategy to launch a Digital CSM motion. The AI Engineer will design, build and own the Digital CSM AI agents that help support both core products and next generation AI-native products at scale. As a member of the Customer Success team, this role will work closely with Customer Success Managers, Customer Success Account Managers, and Customer Success Engineers to capture “human in the loop” activities that can be integrated into the Digital CSM concept. This agent will improve customer outcomes by supporting the full customer journey lifecycle through onboarding, education, adoption, value delivery risk detection and renewals. This role will own the Digital CSM program, ensuring the agentic solutions align to updates to Specright's Product and customer journey. You will be the founding AI Engineer for a new function at Specright, this person will help set the bar for how we build and run AI in Customer Success. This role reports to the Director of Customer Success.

### **Key Responsibilities:**

- Partner with Customer Success stakeholders to map their workflows, find friction points and prioritize where AI agents can improve customer outcomes.
- Architect the agent lifecycle to connect systems together (Salesforce, Claude, Specright products and Customer Success reporting) into customer facing interfaces.
- Maintain each agent, and ensure agents are properly tested/trained in sandbox
- Set SMART KPIs for agent performance to limit failure to launch outcomes and maximize accuracy.
- Establish guardrails to ensure agents function at a high level of reliability, consistency and quality.
- Align agents to the current versioning of Specright's product, ensuring its knowledge base accurately reflects the sandbox and production environment builds of each Product/Feature.
- Create agents that leverage Customer Success best practices, strategy, problem solving and structure through strategic playbooks.
- Solid engineering fundamentals. Testing, debugging, security basics, performance optimization, and reliability are second nature to you.
- Create and deliver reporting to senior Specright leadership on business impact of agent projects.
- Influence Sales and Customer Success leadership on quarterly AI priorities and workstreams at Specright.

**Preferred Experience:**

- 4+ years in an AI engineering, software engineering, or similar role.
- Must have experience working with LLMs and deploying agents into production. You've previously owned an agentic feature or product through deployment and operation.
- Ideally has a functional working knowledge of AI coding in languages such as TypeScript/Node.js and/or Python, with experience in modern web stacks and startup-pace delivery.
- Deep familiarity with agentic coding best practices. You understand task decomposition, agent delegation, prompt hygiene, iterative loops, and how to review and steer agentic outputs effectively.
- Advanced knowledge of Salesforce and associated technologies.
- Comfort working in a fast-changing, high-growth environment where priorities can change frequently - taking ownership to help build the function through these changes is a must.
- Strong written and verbal communication skills, including explaining technical topics to non-technical audiences.
