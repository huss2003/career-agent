**About the Role:**

You will be the engineer who turns our platform into live customer deployments. All of our customers a resort, a golf course, a restaurant group need the platform shaped around how they actually operate: agents configured, integrations wired up, and a steady stream of smaller tools, automations, and utilities built on top of our OS to close the gap between what the platform does out of the box and what the customer needs on day one. That is your job. You will work closely with our delivery and customer success team in Sunnyvale, taking signed customers from kickoff to a fully deployed, working system. This is an early-career role with unusual scope: you will ship real software for real customers every week and see the direct impact of your work on live properties.

**What You'll Do:**

\- Deploy our platform for new customers: configure AI agents, call flows, knowledge bases, and SOPs to match each property's operations.

\- Build smaller tools, scripts, and custom configurations on top of Dextr OS data loaders, integration glue, dashboards, automations whatever a deployment needs to go live.

\- Wire up integrations with customer systems (PMS, POS, telephony, guest messaging, CRMs) using APIs, webhooks, and low-code tooling.

\- Write and refine prompts for customer-facing AI agents, and test agent conversations end-to-end before go-live.

\- Debug deployment issues across the stack from a misbehaving prompt to a failing API call and fix them fast.

\- Turn repeated deployment work into reusable tooling and templates, so every customer after this one goes live faster.

\- Collaborate daily with the US-based delivery team and feed deployment learnings back to the core platform engineering team in Bangalore.
