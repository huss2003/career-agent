**ROLE OVERVIEW**

We are looking for an AI Automation Engineer with strong full-stack development experience to design, develop, and implement AI-powered applications, intelligent workflows, and automation solutions for clients.

This role combines software engineering, AI integration, and business process automation. The ideal candidate can develop end-to-end solutions across frontend and backend environments and engage directly with clients to understand requirements, present technical solutions, and support implementation. Strong communication skills and excellent English proficiency are essential.

**KEY RESPONSIBILITIES**

- Design, develop, and deploy AI-powered applications, automation tools, and intelligent workflows.
- Build and maintain full-stack applications, including frontend interfaces, backend services, APIs, and database integrations.
- Integrate AI models, large language models (LLMs), and third-party services into applications and business processes.
- Develop workflow automations using platforms and frameworks such as n8n, Make, Zapier, or equivalent tools.
- Implement API integrations, webhooks, and system-to-system connectivity to streamline business operations.
- Develop, test, troubleshoot, and optimize automation pipelines and application functionality.
- Engage directly with clients to gather requirements, understand business processes, and translate operational needs into technical solutions.
- Participate in client meetings, provide technical updates, demonstrate solutions, and communicate implementation progress.
- Identify opportunities to improve existing systems and processes through AI adoption and automation.
- Maintain technical documentation and ensure solutions are scalable, secure, and maintainable.

- Bachelor's degree in Computer Science, Information Technology, Software Engineering, or a related discipline, or equivalent practical experience.
