**About the Role:**  
We are looking for an AI Developer who will design, implement, and ship end‑to‑end AI services that blend modern language models, graph‑based knowledge graphs, and autonomous agents. The ideal candidate is a strong software engineer with deep AI fundamentals, hands‑on experience with RAG pipelines, graph‑based reasoning (LangGraph/LangChain), and the ability to bring those services to production.

### Responsibilities

**Key Responsibilities**

**1) Design & build** Retrieval‑Augmented Generation pipelines (LLM + vector store + knowledge graph) from proof‑of‑concept to production.  
**2)** **Develop agentic AI components –** orchestrate multi‑step LLM agents, decision‑making loops, and task‑level execution with LangGraph/LangChain.  
**3) Write clean, test‑driven Python code** for model serving, data pipelines, and micro‑services.  
**4)** **Integrate Java services** where legacy or latency‑critical components exist, exposing them via gRPC/REST.  
**5) Containerize services** (Docker) and deploy to a Kubernetes (GKE/EKS) cluster, handling scaling, monitoring, and resilience.  
**6) Set up & maintain GitLab CI/CD –** automated unit/integration tests, security scans, Helm‑chart promotion, and blue‑green rollouts.  
**7)** **Data‑structure & algorithm optimisation –** implement efficient indexing, graph traversal, and low‑latency retrieval.  
**8)** **Collaborate cross‑functionally –** work with product, UX, data‑science, and ops teams; translate product requirements into technical specifications.  
**9)** **Mentor junior engineers** and contribute to internal knowledge‑sharing (code reviews, design docs, tech talks).  
**10) Stay current –** Evaluate emerging AI models, tools, and best practices, and propose adoption where appropriate.
