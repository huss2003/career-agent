### Role Overview

Every lead, trial, and member touchpoint at the gym runs through GoHighLevel. The GHL Specialist will design and maintain those systems — from the first ad inquiry to the membership renewal — and provide responsive, first-line support to the gym owner and staff.

This is a hands-on role for someone who can move between building and supporting in the same day: fixing a broken trial-booking workflow in the morning, walking the front desk through the pipeline after lunch, and documenting both so it doesn't happen twice. The right person treats every support request as a signal of what to systematize next.

### Key Responsibilities

**GHL Automation & Build**

- Build and maintain lead nurture workflows — speed-to-lead SMS/email, trial class and intro session booking, and follow-up for leads who don't book
- Automate the member journey — class and appointment reminders, no-show follow-up, new-member onboarding, and milestone check-ins
- Run retention and reactivation campaigns — lapsed and frozen members, expiring memberships, failed payments, and win-back offers
- Set up and manage pipelines, calendars, forms, surveys, landing pages/funnels, custom fields, and tags
- Build and send email and SMS campaigns — promotions, challenges, events, and monthly member newsletters
- Automate review requests and referral campaigns to grow the gym's reputation
- Integrate GHL with the gym's management software, payment tools, and ad lead sources (Facebook/Instagram lead forms) via native integrations, Zapier, Make, n8n, or webhooks

**Customer Support**

- Serve as first-line GHL support for the gym owner and front-desk/sales staff
- Manage and respond to member and lead inquiries in the GHL Conversations inbox (SMS, email, social DMs) within agreed response times
- Troubleshoot issues: broken workflows, failed sends, calendar sync errors, A2P/10DLC and email deliverability problems, integration failures
- Train gym staff on using GHL — pipelines, conversations, booking, and reporting — through live walkthroughs and short recorded videos
- Escalate billing, platform-level bugs, or out-of-scope requests to the gym owner with a clear summary

**Reporting, Documentation & Improvement**

- Build dashboards that track leads, trial bookings, show rates, conversions to membership, and retention
- Write SOPs and how-to guides for every recurring build and support issue
- Audit the account for broken, duplicate, or unused workflows and recommend clean-up
- Track recurring issues and propose automations or fixes that remove them at the source
- Use AI tools to speed up campaign copy, troubleshooting, and documentation
