**This position is listed on behalf of a partner company, who manages all applications and next steps. Our partner is looking for a Software Engineer, AI Integrations based in Australia.**

The Software Engineer, AI Integrations will play a key role in expanding how developers and businesses access web data through AI-powered tools and integrations. You will design, build, and maintain integrations connecting APIs and data services with coding agents, developer frameworks, cloud platforms, and automation tools. Working primarily with Python, TypeScript, and emerging AI technologies, you will help create seamless workflows that make complex data capabilities easier to adopt. You will contribute to the development of command-line tools, SDKs, connectors, and Model Context Protocol (MCP)-based solutions. Collaborating with cross-functional teams in a globally distributed environment, you will help drive developer adoption through reliable integrations and high-quality documentation. This is an opportunity to shape an evolving integration ecosystem at the intersection of software engineering, AI, and developer tools.

### Accountabilities:

In this role, you will build and maintain developer-focused integrations that make web data services accessible through AI coding agents, frameworks, cloud platforms, and automation ecosystems. Your main responsibilities will include:

- Develop and publish integrations that connect web data services with coding-agent environments such as Claude Code, Codex, Cursor, and Docker, building on existing MCP infrastructure.
- Design, develop, and release a shared command-line interface (CLI), publishing packages through PyPI and npm.
- Build and maintain packages, tools, and connectors for developer frameworks and agent SDKs, including LangChain and LlamaIndex, as well as browser automation tools.
- Implement recurring data delivery solutions for customer cloud and data platforms, including AWS, Google Cloud Storage, BigQuery, and Snowflake, leveraging existing export capabilities where possible.
- Evaluate application-building and automation platforms such as Dify, Flowise, Make, Zapier, Lovable, and Replit, developing prototypes and templates where they can generate sustained product adoption.
- Assess potential integrations by estimating technical feasibility, development effort, dependencies, and long-term maintenance requirements.
- Implement tracking mechanisms to measure signups and usage attributable to individual integrations, identifying any gaps in attribution.
- Create clear setup guides, technical documentation, and practical examples for developers and AI coding agents across public repositories and documentation platforms.
- Manage submissions to partner directories, registries, and marketplaces, including addressing technical feedback and review requirements.
- Maintain and improve existing integrations by monitoring partner tool updates, resolving issues, and managing releases and versioning.
- Collaborate with Product, Product Marketing, and Developer Relations teams on product launches, positioning, and developer community engagement.
- Work with SaaS and Platform engineering teams to ensure the stability and reliability of the underlying API and cloud infrastructure.
- Participate in code reviews, technical planning, and regular team activities within an agile, cross-functional environment.
