About Bidgely

Bidgely is a Silicon Valley AI company transforming how utilities and their customers understand and manage energy. The UtilityAI platform converts smart meter data into appliance-level insights, while Agentic CX brings GenAI assistants to utility chat, voice IVR, and customer service. Leading utilities across North America, Europe, and Asia rely on Bidgely. Our Bengaluru engineering center is a certified Great Place to Work.

The Role

We are seeking a senior software engineer to build and scale Energy Assistant, our GenAI assistant embedded in utility web portals and mobile apps. Energy Assistant answers customers' questions about usage, bills, appliances, rate plans, and savings through a multi-agent system built on LangGraph, leveraging Bidgely's energy data with tool calls and deterministic calculations. You will own major components end to end, help shape the architecture, and elevate the engineering standards of the team. This is a hands-on role working closely with GenAI engineering, platform, QA/SDET, and product teams.

Key Responsibilities

• Design and build production multi-agent systems: supervisor/router and specialist agents, LangGraph workflows, tool calling, conversation memory, and checkpointing.  
• Build typed tool contracts and data-access layers over Bidgely APIs to ensure the LLM explains results instead of generating numbers.  
• Develop RAG pipelines for utility tariff and knowledge retrieval, with tenant-isolated embeddings and retrieval.  
• Manage latency, reliability, and cost: caching, prewarming, retries, circuit breakers, and model routing across small and large model tiers.  
• Implement guardrails for prompt injection, scope control, authorization, tenant isolation, and data management.  
• Build observability and evaluation hooks with LangSmith, and collaborate with SDETs on evaluation datasets, regression suites, and release criteria.  
• Debug agent failures in production using traces, logs, and metrics, and drive root-cause fixes.  
• Support streaming (WebSocket/SSE) and widget integration with front-end teams.  
• Lead design and code reviews, set engineering standards, and mentor SDE1 and SDE2 engineers.  
• Communicate designs, trade-offs, and progress to engineering, product, and leadership.
