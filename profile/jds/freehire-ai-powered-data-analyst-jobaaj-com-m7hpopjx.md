- Write and optimize **SQL queries** to extract and analyze data

- Use **AI tools** (ChatGPT, Claude, Copilot) to speed up analysis, write queries, and summarize insights

- 0 to 2 years of experience in data analysis or related role (internships and projects count)

- Proficiency in **MS Excel** (formulas, pivot tables, lookups)
- Experience with **data visualization tools** (Power BI, Tableau, or Looker)
