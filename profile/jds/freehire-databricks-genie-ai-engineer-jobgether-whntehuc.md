**This position is listed on behalf of a partner company, who manages all applications and next steps. Our partner is looking for a Databricks Genie AI Engineer based in India.**

As a Databricks Genie AI Engineer, you will play a key role in building and optimizing AI-powered analytics solutions that make complex data accessible through natural language interactions. You will develop the semantic data foundations that enable business users to ask questions in plain language and receive accurate, reliable, and governed insights. Leveraging Databricks Genie, SQL, Python, and modern AI technologies, you will design intelligent data models and enhance the quality of AI-generated responses. You will also implement evaluation frameworks and testing processes to ensure analytical accuracy, consistency, and performance. Working closely with data engineers, platform teams, and business stakeholders, you will translate business requirements into scalable data and AI solutions. This is an exciting opportunity to contribute to the evolution of AI-driven business intelligence within a collaborative, innovative, and fully remote environment.

### Accountabilities:

- Design, develop, and maintain the semantic layer supporting Databricks Genie, including table and column descriptions, synonyms, certified data assets, sample questions, and business metric definitions.
- Build, configure, and continuously optimize Databricks Genie Spaces to improve the accuracy, relevance, and reliability of natural language query responses.
- Define and maintain metric views, business logic, and semantic models to ensure that natural language questions are accurately translated into governed business KPIs.
- Develop evaluation frameworks, automated validation processes, and regression tests to measure query accuracy, SQL quality, and the overall performance of AI-generated insights.
- Analyze and troubleshoot AI response failures, including ambiguous questions, incorrect joins, inaccurate metric interpretations, and SQL generation issues.
- Improve AI-generated results by refining data schemas, metadata, semantic definitions, instructions, and configurations.
- Collaborate with data engineering teams to enhance upstream data quality, data structures, dimensional models, and overall analytical reliability.
- Support the deployment, versioning, and monitoring of semantic assets in collaboration with DevOps and platform engineering teams.
- Translate business user feedback into actionable improvements to semantic models, data structures, prompts, and Genie configurations.
- Work closely with business stakeholders to understand analytical requirements and ensure that AI-powered data experiences deliver meaningful and trustworthy insights.
- Establish best practices for semantic modelling, data governance, testing, and continuous improvement across AI-enabled analytics solutions.
- Monitor the performance and reliability of natural language analytics capabilities and proactively identify opportunities for optimization.
