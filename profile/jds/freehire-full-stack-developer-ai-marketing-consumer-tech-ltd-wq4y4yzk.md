**About FullStackPartner.com**

FullStackPartner.com builds and runs web products for clients. We're looking for a developer who wants to use AI to power our marketing: building tools and automations rather than doing manual marketing.

**The role**

This is a part-time, remote position based in Cebu City. You'll build the systems that bring us leads and customers, using AI wherever it saves time or improves results.

**What you'll do**

- Build AI-powered tools for content creation: blog posts, landing pages, social media posts and email campaigns
- Automate SEO work: keyword research, on-page optimisation and content briefs
- Connect LLM APIs (OpenAI, Claude, etc.) to our websites and workflows
- Build and test landing pages and track conversions
- Set up analytics and reporting so we can see what works
- Suggest new ways to use AI to grow FullStackPartner.com

**What we're looking for**

- Solid full-stack skills: JavaScript/TypeScript, PHP or Python, SQL and REST APIs
- Hands-on experience with AI tools and LLM APIs
- An understanding of digital marketing, SEO and conversion tracking
- Good written English
- Self-driven, reliable and comfortable working remotely
- A portfolio or examples of projects you've built (please include links)

**What we offer**

- Flexible part-time hours, fully remote
- Work on real AI projects with plenty of freedom to experiment
- Room to grow into more hours as the work expands

**How to apply**

Apply with:

1. Your CV
2. Your expected hourly rate
3. Links to 1–3 projects you've built (AI or marketing projects especially)
4. A short answer: **What are the first things you would build for FullStackPartner.com's marketing using AI tools like Claude, and why?**
