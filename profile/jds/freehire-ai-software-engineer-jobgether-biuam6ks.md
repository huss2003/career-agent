As an AI Software Engineer, you will design, build, and deploy advanced AI-powered applications that help organizations automate complex workflows and improve operational efficiency. You will focus on developing intelligent agents, high-precision Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG) pipelines, and reliable AI-driven product features. Working closely with engineering and product teams, you will translate business requirements into technical specifications and deliver customer-facing solutions with measurable results. You will integrate foundation models, optimize AI performance, and establish robust evaluation frameworks to ensure accuracy, reliability, and responsible AI usage. ### Accountabilities

You will contribute to the development and continuous improvement of AI-powered services and intelligent automation capabilities, ensuring that solutions are scalable, secure, accurate, and aligned with customer needs. Your responsibilities will span AI agent development, backend architecture, model evaluation, and production operations.

- Design, build, deploy, and manage intelligent AI agents using modern agentic technologies, ensuring reliable performance within clearly defined constraints and safety guardrails.
- Develop, maintain, and optimize complex Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG) pipelines, improving retrieval recall, precision, and overall response quality.
- Collaborate with product managers to translate industry requirements and business needs into detailed technical specifications, applying spec-driven development practices.
- Integrate foundation models through APIs to support use cases such as document summarization, intelligent search, and automated workflows.
- Design and implement evaluation frameworks and testing patterns to measure AI model and agent performance, identify weaknesses, and continuously improve outputs.
- Build and maintain robust backend architecture using PostgreSQL, REST APIs, asynchronous processing, queueing systems, storage solutions, role-based access control (RBAC), encryption, and security practices.
- Develop automated tests and maintain effective version control and collaborative development workflows using GitHub.
- Implement monitoring systems, operational metrics, and health checks to ensure the reliability and performance of AI-powered services.
- Stay informed about emerging AI technologies, agentic architectures, and engineering practices to continuously improve product capabilities.
