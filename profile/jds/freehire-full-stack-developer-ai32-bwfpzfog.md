**About the Role**

As a Full Stack Developer, you will take ownership of the user experience, functionality, and technical development of our product.

You will be responsible for designing and developing intuitive interfaces, building scalable backend services, and ensuring seamless integration across the platform.

We are looking for someone who combines strong UI/UX capabilities with hands-on experience in React, Go, and Python, alongside the ability to make sound architectural decisions and deliver production-ready software.

You should be comfortable working independently, solving complex technical challenges, and taking ownership of projects from initial development through deployment and continuous improvement.

**Key Responsibilities**

- **Frontend Development & User Experience:** Design and develop intuitive, responsive, and user-friendly interfaces that simplify complex business workflows and improve the overall user experience.
- **Full Stack Development:** Build and maintain application modules across frontend interfaces, APIs, backend services, and databases.
- **System Architecture & Integration:** Develop reliable service boundaries, implement efficient workflows, and ensure seamless communication between system components.
- **Performance & Troubleshooting:** Identify and resolve technical issues across the browser, backend, database, and AWS infrastructure while improving system performance, security, and reliability.
- **Testing & Deployment:** Manage the development lifecycle, including implementation, code reviews, automated testing, release preparation, and post-deployment improvements.
- **Technical Documentation:** Maintain clear technical documentation and record architectural decisions to support collaboration and long-term maintainability.
