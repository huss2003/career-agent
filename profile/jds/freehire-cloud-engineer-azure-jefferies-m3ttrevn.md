**Role summary**

Drive Azure cloud adoption and engineering excellence as part of our multi-cloud infrastructure strategy. You will design, deploy, and operate Azure-based solutions, establish Azure best practices and standards, and partner with the broader cloud team to ensure seamless integration with our AWS-primary environment. This role requires deep Azure expertise combined with the ability to navigate a multi-cloud landscape, strong Terraform skills, and a focus on security, governance, and operational excellence.

You will partner closely with Platform Engineering, Cloud Security, Networking, Identity Teams, Power Platform, Database, Unix, and business line technology teams supporting Equities, Investment Banking, and other divisions. Strong collaboration, interpersonal skills, and customer service orientation are critical as you work across global distributed teams. Experience working in Agile environments using Jira and Confluence is expected.
