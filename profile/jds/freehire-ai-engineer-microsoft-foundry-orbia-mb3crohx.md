**1. MAIN PURPOSE**

The AI Foundry Engineer will design, develop, deploy, and operate AI agents, copilots, and intelligent applications on Microsoft Azure AI Foundry (formerly Azure AI Studio), including Foundry Agent Service, Prompt Flow, the model catalog, Azure OpenAI, Azure AI Search (RAG), Content Safety, and Evaluations.

The engineer will integrate Foundry-based agents with enterprise data sources such as SharePoint, ServiceNow, SAP, and Coupa, and will leverage Databricks as a secondary skill for data preparation, feature and vector pipelines, and Unity Catalog–governed data products that feed AI use cases.

The role partners closely with the AI Architect, Data Engineering Lead, Security, Integration, and Business teams to deliver production-grade Generative AI use cases across Orbia business groups, aligned with ARB governance, SOX/ITGC, responsible AI, and enterprise security guardrails.
