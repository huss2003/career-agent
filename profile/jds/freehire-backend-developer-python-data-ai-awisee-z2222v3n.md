### About the role

You will build and scale the data and AI backend behind our creator intelligence. That means scraping social platforms at scale, storing and querying large volumes of creator data, and building the AI pipelines that evaluate, classify and match creators to brand briefs. The stack today is Python and Django on AWS with ClickHouse.

### What you will do

- Build backend services and REST APIs with Python, Django and Django REST Framework.
- Build and maintain scraping systems for social platforms: scheduling, queues, proxies, rate limits and retries.
- Design data pipelines and schemas in ClickHouse and MySQL, and optimize queries on large datasets.
- Run and scale infrastructure on AWS, with monitoring and cost in mind.
- Integrate LLM APIs (OpenAI, Claude, Gemini) for classification, labeling and evaluation of creator data.
- Build AI agents, tool calling and multi-agent workflows with LangChain and LangGraph.
- Build RAG pipelines using embeddings and vector databases.
- Trace, monitor and evaluate AI workflows with LangSmith.
- Work with frontend developers and the business team to turn requirements into technical solutions.
- Write clean, reusable, tested code.
