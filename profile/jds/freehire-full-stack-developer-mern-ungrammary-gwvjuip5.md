Ungrammary is an independent UX/UI design and digital product design studio partnering with ambitious businesses to build meaningful digital experiences. We work with teams across industries including fintech, SaaS, ecommerce and consumer brands, helping turn complex business problems into clear, useful and engaging products. **The role**

We’re looking for a Full Stack Developer who can translate product and design requirements into reliable, production-ready web applications. You’ll work closely with our designers, project stakeholders and client teams to build features across the stack, make sound technical decisions, and take ownership from development through deployment. The role calls for someone who is comfortable working in a design-led, client-service environment where quality, timelines and clear communication matter.

**Responsibilities**

• Build, test and ship end-to-end web application features using MongoDB, Express.js, React and Node.js.

• Translate Figma designs and product requirements into responsive, accessible and maintainable front-end experiences.

• Develop and maintain RESTful APIs, including authentication, authorization, validation and error handling.

• Create reusable React components and manage application state using suitable patterns and libraries.

• Design MongoDB schemas; write efficient queries and aggregations; and consider indexing and performance.

• Integrate third-party services such as payment gateways, email/SMS providers, maps, cloud storage and OAuth.

• Collaborate with UX/UI designers, project managers, QA and other developers to clarify requirements and plan implementation.

• Estimate tasks, communicate progress and risks, and take responsibility for agreed deliverables.

• Write clean, testable code; participate in code reviews; and document important technical decisions.

• Troubleshoot production issues and improve application performance, security and reliability.

• Support deployment and maintenance, including environment configuration, hosting, monitoring and CI/CD workflows.

• Communicate clearly with internal stakeholders and, when required, international clients.
