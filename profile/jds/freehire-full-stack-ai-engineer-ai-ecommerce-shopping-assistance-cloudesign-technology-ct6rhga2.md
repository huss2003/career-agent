# Full-Stack AI Engineer

**Focus: Agentic AI, AI Shopping Assistants & Virtual Try-On**

## Role Summary

We are looking for a Full-Stack AI Engineer to build, deploy, and operate production-grade AI applications, including agentic shopping assistants and multimodal AI experiences. The role requires strong hands-on expertise in Python, TypeScript, LLM-based applications, real-time streaming, evaluation, and cloud infrastructure.

The ideal candidate can independently deliver end-to-end features across backend agent runtimes and frontend interfaces, with a strong focus on reliability, latency, cost optimisation, and production performance.

## Key Responsibilities

- **AI Agent Development:** Build and maintain Python-based agent runtimes using LLMs, tool calling, structured outputs, retrieval-augmented generation (RAG), multi-turn state, and human escalation workflows.
- **Full-Stack Engineering:** Develop asynchronous Python APIs and TypeScript web components, implementing server-sent events (SSE), streaming interfaces, interactive product cards, and resilient client-side error handling.
- **LLM Evaluation:** Build evaluation datasets and automated test harnesses using deterministic and model-based graders, regression gates, ablation studies, and pass@k/pass^k metrics.
- **Performance & Cost Optimisation:** Improve response latency, time to first token, token consumption, caching efficiency, context management, and cost per conversation.
- **Production Operations:** Implement distributed tracing, structured logging, LLM observability, monitoring, incident resolution, feature flags, controlled rollouts, and rollback mechanisms.
- **Cloud & DevOps:** Deploy containerised services, manage CI/CD pipelines, infrastructure as code, and production workloads on cloud platforms.
- **E-commerce AI Integration:** Integrate product search, catalogue data, product details, grounded recommendations, and commerce-related event tracking into AI-powered shopping experiences.
- **Virtual Try-On & Multimodal AI:** Contribute to image-based generative AI applications, including embeddable frontend widgets and scalable backend services.
- **Internationalisation & Scalability:** Support multilingual experiences, brand-specific configurations, localisation, currency handling, and backward-compatible integrations.
- **Cross-Functional Collaboration:** Work with product, business, and engineering stakeholders to troubleshoot quality issues and deliver measurable improvements to customer experience and business outcomes.

- Strong hands-on experience with **Python and TypeScript**.
