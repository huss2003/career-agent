## About Advantage HQ

Advantage HQ is building the next generation of creator-led growth infrastructure for consumer brands.

We combine creative strategy, creator partnerships, performance content, whitelisting, affiliate distribution, paid media, and proprietary technology into one integrated growth system.

Our team manages more than $850 million in annual media spend and works with a network of more than 400,000 creators across Meta, TikTok, and YouTube. Through Meteoric AI, our proprietary intelligence platform, we use data and AI to improve decision-making across creative production, creator selection, customer acquisition, campaign performance, and brand growth.

We work with ambitious consumer brands across health, wellness, beauty, nutrition, personal care, and lifestyle.

## The Opportunity

We are looking for a hands-on AI Engineer to bring AI into how Advantage HQ operates, across every team and every core process.

As we scale, our teams spend a large share of their time on manual work: gathering data, building reports, writing briefs, moving information between tools, and repeating the same steps across clients. We see a large opportunity to use AI to remove that work, speed up decisions, and help every team do more with the same headcount.

Your mission is to design and build the AI systems, internal tools, sites, and dashboards that make our company faster, more efficient, and more data-driven.

You will own this work end to end: mapping how teams work today, identifying where AI and automation can make the biggest difference, building the solution, rolling it out, and making sure people use it.

This is not a research role or a prompt-writing role. We are looking for an engineer who writes production code, understands how to apply LLMs to real business processes, and knows how to build tools that people rely on every day.

You will work closely with leadership and with our Growth, Creative, Media Buying, Client Services, Finance, People, and Technology teams to find the highest-value problems and solve them with AI.

Success means AI is built into how Advantage HQ works: core processes are automated, teams have the tools and dashboards they need, and the business runs measurably faster and more efficiently.

## What You'll Own

### AI Strategy & Process Transformation

- Map current workflows across departments and identify where AI and automation can save time, reduce errors, or improve decisions.
- Build and maintain a prioritized roadmap of AI opportunities based on business impact and effort.
- Redesign processes around AI, not only add AI to existing steps, working with team leads to agree on the new way of working.
- Recommend which problems need custom builds and which are better solved with existing AI tools and platforms.
- Track and report the impact of each initiative to leadership in terms of time saved, cost, and quality.
- Stay current with new models, tools, and techniques, and assess which ones are ready for use across the company.

### AI Systems, Agents & Automation

- Build AI agents and multi-step workflows that automate research, briefing, reporting, analysis, and operational tasks.
- Develop LLM-powered applications with Claude as our primary model, using other providers where they fit better.
- Automate repetitive work between tools, such as data entry, status updates, client reporting, onboarding steps, and approvals.
- Build AI assistants that help teams draft, summarize, review, and answer questions using company knowledge.
- Write, test, and version prompts, tool definitions, and structured outputs as part of the codebase.
- Add human review steps where accuracy matters, so automation stays reliable.

### Internal Tools, Sites & Dashboards

- Design and build internal web apps, portals, and sites that give teams one place to access the tools and information they need.
- Build dashboards that give leadership and teams real-time visibility into performance, operations, and key business metrics.
- Connect dashboards to live data sources so reporting is automatic rather than built by hand.
- Create AI-powered features within internal tools, such as natural-language search, automated insights, and summaries.
- Build tools quickly using a mix of custom code and low-code platforms, choosing whichever fits the problem best.
- Maintain and improve internal tools based on user feedback and changing business needs.

### Data, Integrations & Knowledge Systems

- Integrate the systems we use every day, including Google Workspace, Slack, project management tools, CRM, HR systems, and advertising platforms.
- Build data pipelines that collect, clean, and structure data from internal and external sources.
- Build retrieval-augmented generation (RAG) systems over SOPs, playbooks, client information, and internal knowledge so teams can find answers instantly.
- Organize company knowledge so it is accurate, current, and usable by both people and AI systems.
- Work with team leads to define data standards and keep data clean across systems.
- Connect with the Technology team on Meteoric AI where internal tools and our proprietary platform overlap.

### Quality, Security & Reliability

- Build evaluation checks that measure the accuracy and usefulness of AI outputs before and after changes.
- Monitor AI systems and automations for errors, failures, and drift, and fix issues quickly.
- Ensure company and client data is handled securely, with the right access controls and data privacy practices.
- Set up logging and monitoring so every system can be traced and debugged.
- Monitor and manage the cost of AI tools, APIs, and infrastructure.
- Document every system clearly so it can be maintained, handed over, and improved.

### Adoption, Enablement & Collaboration

- Work closely with each team to understand their workflows, pain points, and goals before building.
- Roll out new tools with clear guides, demos, and training so people use them.
- Help teams use Claude effectively in their daily work, including setting up shared Projects, Skills, and connectors for each team.
- Collect feedback, track usage, and improve tools that are not getting adopted.
- Explain technical trade-offs and results clearly to non-technical teammates and leadership.
- Help build a culture where every team looks for ways to work smarter with AI.

## What Success Looks Like

Your performance will be measured primarily by how much AI improves the way Advantage HQ operates.

Key metrics include:

- Processes Automated: Number of core business processes automated or redesigned with AI.
- Time Saved: Hours of manual work removed each month across teams.
- Tool Adoption: Active, regular use of AI systems, internal tools, and dashboards by each team.
- Delivery: Number and quality of AI systems, sites, and dashboards shipped on schedule.
- Reporting Coverage: Share of key business metrics available in automated, real-time dashboards.
- Process Speed: Reduction in turnaround time for key workflows such as reporting, briefing, and onboarding.
- Output Quality: Accuracy and usefulness of AI outputs, measured against defined checks.
- System Reliability: Uptime and error rates of AI systems and automations.
- Cost Efficiency: Cost of AI tools and infrastructure relative to the time and money they save.
- Stakeholder Satisfaction: Feedback from the teams who rely on the tools you build.
