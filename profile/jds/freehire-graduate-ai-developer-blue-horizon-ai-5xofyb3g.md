If you've built something with Claude Code, Codex or similar on a weekend and wished someone would pay you to do it, this is that job. Graduate role, full-time contract, remote within New Zealand.

**About us**

We're Caique and Donncha, the two founders of Blue Horizon AI. We're a small, ambitious team that likes to travel, works hard and wants to build something great. We strongly believe in enjoying the ride in the meantime.

We build AI operating systems that businesses run their day-to-day on, mostly for insurance brokerages across New Zealand and Australia.

**Who we're looking for**

Someone ambitious who wants to grow with the business and never stops upskilling. In our day-to-day there are three things we hold each other to:

• Everyone has a say. If you see a better way to build it, or a problem the client hasn't spotted, say so.

• Beat your prior best. Make each build better than your last by looking at what worked, what didn't, and improving on it.

• Think long term. Our goal is to be the go-to people in NZ/AU to revolutionise the way these brokerages operate. Getting there means hard problems and a few lessons learnt the hard way, and we think every one of them is worth it. If you see programming as a craft and take pride in building something properly, you'll fit right in.

**What we do and what you'll do**

We take on the processes that keep our clients from making more money. We commonly see them go from 90 minutes of task admin down to 5, and across a whole business that adds up fast.

You will...

1. Install and run AI operating systems for businesses. That means AI workspaces, skills, plugins and MCP connectors into their email, documents and broking systems. Each install gives a team back hours every week.
2. Build what sits underneath. Python pipelines, document extraction from renewal schedules and policy wordings (some of them scanned), Postgres and Supabase, Next.js dashboards, mostly on Azure and AWS.
3. Run the monthly call with the brokers you build for. You'll hear what they're paying for, what saved them time and what to build next.

Coding agents write a lot of the solutions with your direction. Your time goes on installing the operating system and building the workflows that benefit the brokerage on top over the months that they've signed with us for.

**Opportunity to step up**

You'll ship to a live business in your first fortnight, work directly with both founders and sit in on the calls where client workflows get shaped. As we grow, there's a real path to leading client accounts and owning more of the engagement.

**What's in it for you**

• Remote, from anywhere in New Zealand.

• Travel around NZ/AU to deploy client systems.

• A learning budget for courses, events and hackathons.

It's not a fit if you're based outside New Zealand or run an agency or dev shop (this is a job on our team).

**Pay**

NZ$30-40 an hour as a contractor (you invoice us).

**If you're serious**

Apply through SEEK and answer two things in your cover letter. First, one system you've built that's still in use. Second, how you'd handle this: a broker gets renewal schedules from five insurers as PDFs, two of them scanned, and the key fields have to be right before the renewal goes out. How would you build it, and how would you know it's right? We care more about how you'd check the answer than which tool you'd pick.

We read every application ourselves, and if it's a fit, Caique will give you a call within a few days.
