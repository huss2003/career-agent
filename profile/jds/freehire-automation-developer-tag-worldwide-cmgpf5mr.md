**Role definition :**

A hands-on developer who implements automation features within a creative production capability area. The Mid Automation Developer is the day-to-day execution muscle of a squad - pairs with a Senior Engineer, takes ownership of 2 - 3 sprint stories at a time, and converts designs and prototypes into shippable, documented, tested code.

This role exists to multiply the Senior Engineer's leverage. Where the Senior designs the architecture and runs the discovery, the Mid implements, tests, and iterates. Over 12 - 18 months in role, a Mid is expected to grow into owning sprint stories end-to-end and eventually mentoring a Junior Developer. Day-to-day : 70% hands-on implementation (code, tests, documentation); 20% pair-programming and code review with the Senior; 10% participation in design reviews, demos, and team rituals.

**Mission :**

\- Pair-program with the Senior Engineer on day-to-day automation builds.

\- Convert sprint stories into production code; participate fully in design, build, test, demo, and post-go-live ramp cycles.

\- Grow toward owning sprint stories independently within 12 - 18 months.

**Key responsibilities :**

\- Implement 2 - 3 sprint stories per cycle under the Senior Engineer's direction.

\- Build unit tests and basic regression checks for every script before handing off to QA.

\- Convert prototype scripts and notebooks into production-grade, version-controlled, documented code.

\- Maintain the team's shared script library - refactor older scripts when patterns evolve.

\- Participate actively in design reviews, sprint demos, and retrospectives.

\- Hand off completed work to the QA & Release Engineer with test artefacts and clear runbooks.

\- Use LLM-assisted development tools (Cursor / Claude Code / Copilot) to accelerate delivery while maintaining code quality.

\- Mentor junior developers when the team adds them (typically within 6 months of joining).
