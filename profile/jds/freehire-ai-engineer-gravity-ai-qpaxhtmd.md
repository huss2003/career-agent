### About Gravity

Great AI agents already exist. They’re stuck. The people who build them have no good way to earn from them, and everyone who wants one has to rebuild it from scratch. Chatbots answer or code. ### The role

This role is half **AI product manager**, half **agent engineer**. You will not hand specs to a dev team. You decide what problem to solve, design the agent behind it, build it with LangGraph, test it and ship it.

You work directly with the founder and own AI features **from the first idea to production.**

**Fixed + Performance-based pay:** you get the builder's cut of the revenue your agents earn, so your pay grows with what you ship.

### What you will do

**Product**

- **Break user problems down:** understand each one deeply and split it into parts an agent can solve one at a time.
- **Draw the lines:** decide what an agent should do, what it should not, and where a human stays in the loop.
- **Write it down first:** clear requirements, success metrics and test scenarios.
- **Learn from real usage:** read the traces and failures, then decide what to fix next.

**Agent building**

- **Build agent workflows** with LangGraph and LangChain.
- **Manage context and memory:** what the agent remembers, forgets and retrieves.
- **Write, test and improve prompts,** with evaluations that measure their quality.
- **Keep cost and latency in check** with prompt caching, model choice and similar techniques.
- **Connect agents to tools, APIs and data sources.**
- **Debug agent failures:** loops, hallucinations, wrong tool calls, context overflow.

**Ownership**

- **Ship fast, learn from users, iterate.**
- **Document what you build** so others can understand and extend it.

### What we are looking for

- **You have shipped real products,** not just prototypes, as a product manager, product engineer or similar.
- **You have built with LangGraph or LangChain** and can show it: a GitHub repo, a demo or a live product.
