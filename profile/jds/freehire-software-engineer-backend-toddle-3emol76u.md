Toddle is an AI-powered teaching and learning platform for K–12 schools. Built by educators, for educators, it is used by over 200,000 educators across 2,500+ schools in 100+ countries.

Teachers today work 50+ hours a week. Too much of that time is spent navigating fragmented systems and administrative overhead. Our BHAG (Big Hairy Audacious Goal) is to save teachers 10+ hours every week while helping elevate their practices, so that time can go back to what matters most - teaching, learning, and meaningful time with students.

Our approach is three-pronged.

First, Toddle brings the entire teaching and learning lifecycle into one place. Teachers plan and map curriculum, create assessments and gradebooks, maintain student portfolios, communicate with families, and generate progress reports and transcripts - all from a single, coherent system. Toddle also supports adjacent workflows where teachers are closely involved, including attendance, timetables, calendars, accreditation, behaviour management, analytics, and insights.

Second, every layer of the platform is deeply AI-powered. Educators work with a thoughtful AI partner to plan curriculum, create lessons and assessments, assist with grading, write progress report comments, and derive insights from data. Toddle also offers student-facing AI Tutors, enabling highly personalized and immersive learning experiences.

Third, Toddle integrates seamlessly with the broader school technology ecosystem. The platform supports 50+ integrations across SIS platforms, content providers, data &amp; integration platforms, and workspaces such as Google, Apple, and Microsoft.

### **Here’s what you’ll be doing**

We are looking for a passionate and motivated product engineer who partners with us to build intuitive, scalable, and delightful user experiences for teachers, students, and parents.

As a product Engineer, you will contribute to designing, developing, and deploying high-quality product. You will collaborate closely with engineers, product managers, designers, educators, and gain exposure to real-world usage in schools.

This role offers hands-on ownership, continuous technical growth and product understanding in a fast-growing SaaS environment.

**What you’ll achieve in your first 3 months**

- Understand the codebase, backend architecture, and core product workflows
- Build strong fundamentals in **PostgreSQL**, **GraphQL**, and basic query writing
- Start contributing to feature development, production fixes, and basic query optimization

**What you’ll achieve in your first 6 months**

- Independently deliver major backend features by writing **clean, modular, and optimized code**
- Become comfortable with **query optimization**, and writing scripts for **analytics and database migrations**

**What you’ll achieve in your first 12 months**
