**Role & responsibilities**

- Engage directly with clients to understand business requirements, workflows, and technical challenges.
- Translate ambiguous business problems into technical specifications, solution architectures, prototypes, and production-ready AI applications.
- Design, develop, and deploy AI-powered applications, intelligent agents, APIs, data pipelines, and full-stack solutions.
- Build and manage agentic workflows involving planning, tool selection, execution, observation, error recovery, and termination.
- Implement AI system components such as context management, memory, permissions, runtime controls, and observability.
- Develop workflow orchestration using graphs, state machines, and multi-step execution patterns.
- Integrate LLM-based solutions with enterprise applications, databases, APIs, cloud platforms, and third-party services.
- Build evaluation frameworks to measure AI accuracy, reliability, task completion, latency, and operational cost.
- Troubleshoot AI-related issues, including hallucinations, context loss, unreliable tool calls, infinite loops, and prompt injection.
- Collaborate with product, engineering, data, security, and client teams throughout the solution lifecycle, from discovery to production.
- Monitor, optimize, and maintain AI applications for scalability, performance, reliability, and security.
- Communicate technical designs, implementation risks, limitations, and trade-offs to technical and non-technical stakeholders.

**Preferred candidate profile**

- 7+ years of professional experience in software engineering, AI engineering, application development, or related technical roles.
- Strong hands-on programming experience in Python, TypeScript, or JavaScript, with a proven track record of delivering production-grade applications.
- Practical experience building and deploying LLM-powered applications, AI agents, or agentic workflows.
- Strong understanding of LLM concepts, prompt engineering, tool calling, context management, structured outputs, and memory.
- Experience with agent orchestration frameworks such as LangGraph, LangChain, or equivalent custom orchestration solutions.
- Hands-on experience with REST APIs, databases, cloud infrastructure, enterprise integrations, and modern software development practices.
- Familiarity with RAG architectures, embeddings, vector databases, AI evaluation, and production monitoring.
- Understanding of AI security, access control, data protection, and reliable agent execution.
- Experience taking solutions from business discovery and rapid prototyping through validation, deployment, and production support.
- Strong client-facing communication, problem-solving, debugging, and stakeholder management skills.
- Ability to work independently in a fast-paced, remote, customer-facing engineering environment.
- Flexibility to work in CST business hours.

**Preferred:** Experience in consulting, solutions engineering, professional services, enterprise AI implementations, multi-agent systems, and cloud-native deployments using AWS, Azure, or GCP.
