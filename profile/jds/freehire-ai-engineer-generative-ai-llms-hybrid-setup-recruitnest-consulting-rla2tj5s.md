**About the Job:**

We are looking for an AI Engineer to join our client's team and develop AI-powered applications using Generative AI, Large Language Models (LLMs), and AI Agent technologies. In this role, you will design and build solutions involving Retrieval Augmented Generation (RAG), enterprise knowledge retrieval, document processing, and AI agents that integrate with business systems and applications. You will also work with cross-functional teams to evaluate, optimize, and deploy AI solutions while staying up to date with emerging AI technologies and development practices.

**Key Responsibilities:**

- Design and develop AI-powered applications using Python, Generative AI, and Large Language Models (LLMs).
- Build and optimize RAG solutions, document-processing workflows, and enterprise knowledge-retrieval systems.
- Develop AI agents that interact with business systems, APIs, and enterprise platforms.
- Integrate AI solutions with client systems, business applications, and external services.
- Implement agent memory, context management, and orchestration mechanisms.
- Evaluate and improve the quality, reliability, and performance of AI applications.
- Support the deployment of AI solutions and collaborate with Systems Engineers on production implementation.
- Contribute to proof-of-concept projects, rapid prototyping, and knowledge-sharing initiatives.

**Qualifications:**

- Bachelor's degree in Computer Science, Information Technology, or a related field.
- At least 4 years of relevant experience in software development, AI engineering, or related roles.
- Proficiency in Python programming and software development best practices, including testing and version control.
- Strong knowledge of Generative AI, Large Language Models (LLMs), Retrieval Augmented Generation (RAG), and AI Agent architectures.
- Hands-on experience with AI and agent frameworks such as LangGraph, OpenAI Agents SDK, PydanticAI, LlamaIndex, CrewAI, Google ADK, or Microsoft Agent Framework.
- Experience integrating applications with REST APIs, external services, and enterprise systems, including familiarity with MCP (Model Context Protocol).
- Knowledge of vector databases, structured and unstructured data, hybrid search, and enterprise knowledge retrieval.
- Experience with Docker and cloud environments, preferably Azure AI Foundry, Azure OpenAI Service, or Azure AI Search.
- Strong analytical and problem-solving skills, with excellent written and verbal English communication skills.
