\# Job Profile: Performance Marketer — AI Creative & Analytics

\## Role title

\*\*Performance Marketing Specialist (AI-Driven) — Creative & Analytics\**

Alt titles: AI Performance Marketer, Growth Marketer (Creative + Data)

\## About the role

We're hiring a performance marketer who sits at the intersection of \*\*creative production\** and \*\*data analysis\*\*, using AI as the main lever on both sides. This isn't a traditional "designer who also runs ads" or "analyst who also writes briefs" split — it's one person who can concept and produce ad creative with AI tools, then read the performance data on that same creative and iterate, without waiting on a separate design or analytics resource.

They will own the full loop: \*\*creative in → data out → decision → next creative\*\*, for paid campaigns across Meta, Google, and LinkedIn.

\## Key responsibilities

\### Creative (AI-assisted production)

\- Concept and produce ad creatives (static, carousel, short-form video, UGC-style) using AI generation tools (image, video, voice, avatar) rather than relying solely on manual design/shoot cycles.

\- Maintain brand consistency and voice across AI-generated creative; know when AI output needs a human design pass vs. when it's ship-ready.

\- Rapidly produce creative variants for testing (hooks, angles, formats, CTAs) at a volume manual production can't match.

\- Adapt winning creative across platforms and aspect ratios (Reels, Stories, Shorts, LinkedIn feed, static display).

\### Analysis (data-driven decisions)

\- Read campaign performance data (CTR, CPM, CPL/CPA, ROAS, hook rate, thumb-stop rate, frequency/fatigue) across Meta, Google, and LinkedIn ad platforms and connect it back to specific creative elements.

\- Diagnose *\*why\** a creative is winning or dying (fatigue, audience mismatch, hook failure, landing page drop-off) — not just report the number.

\- Build and maintain lightweight dashboards/reports (Sheets, GA4, ad platform reporting) that the team can act on weekly.

\- Run structured creative testing (one-variable-at-a-time where possible) and document learnings so insights compound across campaigns, not just within one.

\### Automation (AI as a force multiplier)

\- Set up and use AI automations/workflows (e.g., prompt pipelines, scheduled reporting, auto-tagging of creative performance, AI-assisted copy/caption variants) to cut manual production and reporting time.

\- Comfortable working with (or quickly learning) the agency's existing AI/automation stack: creative generation tools, ad-platform APIs/MCP tools, Google Sheets-based reporting pipelines, and n8n/Zapier-style automations.

\- Identify repetitive creative or reporting tasks and propose/build an automation for them rather than repeating manual work.

\## Required skills & background

\- \*\*Creative background\*\*: prior experience in graphic design, video editing, copywriting, or content creation — someone who understands what makes an ad *\*look and feel\** right, even if AI tools now do the heavy lifting.

\- \*\*Working fluency with AI creative tools\*\*: image/video generation (e.g., Midjourney, Runway, Higgsfield, HeyGen, or similar), AI-assisted editing, and prompt-based iteration. Strong plus if they've used any AI video/creative pipeline professionally, not just for personal projects.

\- \*\*Analytical skill\*\*: comfortable in spreadsheets (Sheets/Excel — formulas, pivot tables), reading ad-platform dashboards (Meta Ads Manager, Google Ads, LinkedIn Campaign Manager, GA4), and translating numbers into a plain-English "what to do next."

\- \*\*Automation mindset\*\*: has used or is eager to learn no-code/low-code automation tools or basic scripting to remove manual steps from creative or reporting workflows.

\- 1–3+ years in performance marketing, growth, or a hybrid creative/media-buying role (range flexible based on portfolio strength).

\## Nice to have

\- Experience running or assisting on paid social/search campaigns directly (not just creative supply).

\- Familiarity with AI copywriting for ad copy and captions.

\- Basic SQL or comfort pulling data from a CRM for lead-quality feedback loops.

\- A portfolio showing before/after: a creative iteration that was changed *\*because of\** a data insight.

\## What success looks like

\- Owns creative production for at least one live client account end-to-end using AI tools, with a visible drop in time-per-creative vs. the prior manual process.

\- Can explain, unprompted, why a given ad is winning or losing using the platform data — not just restate the metrics.

\- Has shipped at least one automation (reporting, creative variant generation, or tagging) that saves recurring manual work.
