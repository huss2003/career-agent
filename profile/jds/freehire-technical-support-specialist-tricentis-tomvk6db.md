**About the role**

As a Technical Support Specialist, you’re the expert our customers turn to when something isn’t working. You’ll analyze, troubleshoot, and resolve technical cases from customers of every size, from fast-moving product teams to Global 2000 enterprises running mission-critical SAP, Salesforce, and Oracle landscapes.

You’ll work with customers through the Tricentis Support Hub, email, chat, and the occasional call or remote session. You’ll also partner with Tier 2, R&D, Product, and Customer Success to get to root cause. We’ll train you in depth on one or more products in our platform: Tosca, Tosca Cloud, qTest, NeoLoad, LiveCompare, SeaLights, Vera, Testim, or Data Integrity, including the agentic AI capabilities that now run across them.

**What you’ll do**

- **Own cases end to end.** Reproduce issues, analyze logs, HAR files, and configurations, and drive each case to resolution within SLA.
- **Diagnose across the stack.** Troubleshoot installation, licensing, configuration, integration, authentication, and performance issues in both on-premises and SaaS environments.
- **Find the real defects.** Recognize when a behavior is a product bug, build a clean reproduction, and escalate to Tier 2 and R&D with the evidence they need.
- **Support AI-powered features.** Help customers set up and troubleshoot agentic capabilities such as Tricentis Agentic Test Automation and our remote MCP servers.
- **Communicate with clarity.** Set expectations, give regular updates, and translate technical findings into plain language for both engineers and business stakeholders.
- **Work smarter with AI.** Use AI-assisted tools and our internal knowledge bases to research faster, while applying your own judgment to every answer you send.
- **Grow our knowledge base.** Write and improve knowledge articles so customers and colleagues can solve known issues themselves.
- **Spot patterns.** Flag recurring issues across cases and share them with Product and Engineering to improve the product.

**What you bring**

**Must have**

- A bachelor’s degree in Computer Science, Computer Engineering, or a related field.
- 3+ years in technical, application, or product support in a customer-facing role.
- A solid understanding of software development and testing concepts (SDLC, STLC, and test automation).
- Working knowledge of databases and SQL, networking (HTTP/S, proxies, certificates), authentication (SSO, SAML, OAuth), and at least one scripting or programming language.
- Comfort with Windows and Linux, and familiarity with cloud platforms (AWS or Azure) and containers (Docker, Kubernetes).
- Strong written and verbal communication skills in English, and a customer-first mindset when making decisions.
- Experience with ITIL practices in a service desk or support environment, and with tools such as ServiceNow and Jira.
- Ability to work in a hybrid arrangement, with a minimum of 3 days per week onsite at our office.

**Highly desirable**

Hands-on experience with any of these Tricentis products, or similar tools:

- **Tosca and Tosca Cloud.** Codeless, model-based test automation for enterprise apps, powered by Vision AI and agentic AI. Related technologies include Distributed Execution (DEX), API testing and service virtualization (OSV), mobile testing, SAP, Salesforce, database, and mainframe engines, and Tricentis Agentic Test Automation.
- **qTest.** Centralized test management that unifies and scales testing across teams. Related technologies include Jira integration, CI/CD (Jenkins, Azure DevOps, GitHub Actions), REST APIs, PostgreSQL, Docker, AWS, and Linux.
