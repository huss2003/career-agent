# About Comprinno

Comprinno is a NASSCOM-incubated company headquartered in Bangalore, with offices in Pune, Coimbatore, and the United States. We specialize in cloud transformation, DevOps, infrastructure automation, and Artificial Intelligence, enabling organizations to build scalable, secure, and high-performing cloud environments on AWS.

Our flagship SaaS platform, **Tevico**, offers intelligent cloud governance and observability for AWS workloads. It helps enterprises improve uptime, reduce costs, and ensure compliance by proactively detecting anomalies and triggering automated remediation workflows.

As an AWS Advanced Consulting Partner, Comprinno helps organizations migrate, modernize, secure, and operate cloud environments while adopting emerging technologies such as Data Analytics, Machine Learning, Generative AI, and Agentic AI.

# About the Role

We are looking for a visionary, entrepreneurial, and execution-focused **Head – Data Analytics & AI** to establish, scale, and lead Comprinno's Data Analytics & AI function.

Reporting directly to the CTO, this role will be responsible for defining the strategy, building the team, developing service offerings, driving innovation, ensuring delivery excellence, and shaping Comprinno's long-term Data & AI vision.

This is a leadership role with end-to-end ownership of the Data & AI organization, including Data Engineering, Analytics, Machine Learning, MLOps, Generative AI, and Agentic AI capabilities. The role will work closely with Sales, Marketing, Alliances, Cloud Engineering, and Customer Success teams to build a strong market presence and accelerate business growth.

The ideal candidate combines deep technical expertise with strong leadership and consulting experience and has a proven track record of building high-performing teams and delivering impactful data-driven solutions for enterprise customers.
