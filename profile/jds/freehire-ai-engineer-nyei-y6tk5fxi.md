**AI Engineer – Autodesk Automation & AI Agents**

**About the Role**

We are looking for an AI Engineer to develop AI-powered tools, plugins, and automation solutions for Autodesk applications, particularly Revit and AutoCAD. The role involves building intelligent systems that understand user instructions, interact with CAD/BIM models, automate engineering workflows, and reduce manual effort.

Candidates from Computer Science, IT, Software Engineering, AI, Electronics, or related technical backgrounds are welcome. Prior MEP industry experience is not mandatory.

**Key Responsibilities**

- Develop custom plugins, add-ins, and automation tools for Autodesk Revit and AutoCAD.
- Build AI agents and chatbots that understand natural-language instructions and execute engineering tasks.
- Integrate LLMs such as OpenAI GPT into Autodesk applications and engineering workflows.
- Automate drafting, modeling, annotation, dimensioning, scheduling, and documentation processes.
- Develop APIs and backend services to connect Autodesk applications with AI platforms and external systems.
- Build AI-powered tools for drawing analysis, data extraction, design validation, and error detection.
- Test, debug, optimize, and maintain software solutions.
- Collaborate with engineering teams to identify automation opportunities and develop practical solutions.

**Required Technical Skills**

- Strong programming knowledge in Python and/or C#.
- Understanding of .NET development and object-oriented programming.
- Hands-on experience with at least one Autodesk API: Revit API, AutoCAD .NET API, AutoLISP, ObjectARX, or Autodesk Platform Services (APS).
- Experience developing plugins, add-ins, automation tools, or software applications.
- Knowledge of LLM APIs, prompt engineering, AI agents, and chatbot development.
- Familiarity with frameworks such as LangChain, LangGraph, or Semantic Kernel.
- Understanding of REST APIs, JSON, databases, Git, and software integration.

**Preferred Qualifications**

- Experience working with AI-powered automation or agent-based applications.
- Knowledge of CAD/BIM workflows.
- Experience integrating third-party applications and cloud-based services.
