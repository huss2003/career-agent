# **About FloBiz**

FloBiz builds AI for the world’s small businesses. Our flagship product, myBillBook, is India’s #1 billing, stock management & accounting platform, trusted by over 1 crore MSMEs across 18,000+ pincodes. On top of that foundation, we are now building a family of agentic AI products. This is AI that doesn’t just answer questions but does the work: making calls, booking appointments, collecting payments, and closing loops that used to need a full-time employee.

Today that family of products live in two markets: MyCashFlo, our AI collections manager for Indian businesses, and heyAnaya, our AI front desk for clinics in the UAE. Both run on a voice AI platform we built entirely in-house, with 881,000+ completed calls and counting across 12 languages from Hindi to Gulf Arabic.

We are backed by Peak XV Partners, Elevation Capital, Greenoaks, BEENEXT and Think Investments. Our tech and product teams are remote by design, with in-person offsites, hackathons and team weeks to bring everyone together.

# **About MyBillBooK**

Website :

myBillBook is India's leading GST billing & accounting software with mobile, web app & native desktop offerings and runs on Android as well as iOS. myBillBook has been designed to aid SMB owners to conduct their operations from anywhere and anytime and provides a secure platform for business owners to record transactions & track business performance on the go. It is an ideal software for GST registered businesses where invoicing is one of the core business activities. Also, businesses looking to digitise their operations to understand their financial position better can use this software. It helps them create bills (GST & non-GST), record purchases & expenses, manage inventory and track payables/receivables directly from their mobile phones or computers. Also, the app generates 25 critical business reports that help business owners make effective business decisions. myBillBook is currently available in English, Hindi, Gujarati & Tamil.

Currently, the app has been downloaded by over 6.5M SMBs across the country with over 10x growth in user base in the last 12 months alone. Even with such pace of adoption of the product, myBillBook continues to be the highest rated application in its category on Google Play Store.

The engineering problems are the kind only this scale creates. Core transaction tables run into billions of rows and still have to answer in milliseconds, because a shopkeeper mid-sale does not wait. Invoices, payments and stock movements land every second from mobile, web and desktop, and every one of them feeds user-facing analytics: 25 reports, dashboards and insights computed over years of transaction history, read by business owners every morning and served fresh.

myBillBook is also where FloBiz's AI meets its largest audience. Our document AI reads crumpled bills, thermal prints and handwriting into ledger-ready data - 6.68 million line items across 108,000+ businesses so far. An agent tool layer exposes the platform's core operations - stock.query, invoice.create, payment.reconcile, ledger.balance - so AI agents can raise an invoice or reconcile a payment with the same guarantees as a human tapping a button.

The problems worth solving here are still open. How do you keep queries fast when a table crosses its next billion rows? How do you give a crore of businesses the analytics of a company? How do you let an AI agent write to real books safely, and evaluate it before it touches real money? And how do you ship all of this fast, every week, to businesses that run on it? If you want your code to run the books of a crore small businesses, and to put AI to work inside them, this is the team.
