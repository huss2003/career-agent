You will work on semantic search, intelligent information retrieval, ranking algorithms, personalization, and LLM-powered applications across business domains such as Banking, Loans, Insurance, and Investments.

This role is ideal for engineers passionate about developing scalable, production-ready AI/ML solutions and transforming complex data into intelligent business applications.

- Participate in AI/ML proof-of-concept initiatives and evaluate emerging technologies.
- Implement engineering best practices, including Git, CI/CD, automated testing, model monitoring, and deployment.
- Stay updated with advancements in Generative AI, LLMs, NLP, and Machine Learning.
