This is a hands-on builder role on the AI Transformation team. The AI Engineer will design, deploy, and support the systems that make AI tools usable and effective across the organization, with a primary focus on Anthropic technologies: skills and plugins, Claude Managed Agents, and Claude Tag. The work also includes agentic workflows and operational configurations. This person will help turn AI from a promising capability into practical, reliable infrastructure that teams can use every day.

**About the team:**

The AI Transformation team is responsible for deploying and operationalizing AI tooling, including Claude-based tools such as Chat, Cowork, and Claude Code, and agents built on Claude Managed Agents and Claude Tag. The team's impact comes from making powerful AI systems usable at scale across the organization, improving how teams work, and helping the business adopt AI in a practical, secure, and sustainable way.

*If you are passionate about complex problem solving and motivated by scale, then this is the role for you!*

**Who will you work with?**

You will work closely with the Director of AI Transformation, cross-functional business teams, engineers, and end users across the organization. This role requires partnering with stakeholders who range from people with little familiarity with AI systems to highly technical collaborators who want to go deep on workflows, prompts, and agent architecture. You will also help teams determine which tools best fit their needs across both the Anthropic and Microsoft ecosystems.

**What are some of the exciting challenges you will be working on?**

- Build and maintain skills and plugins used across the organization.
- Design, build, and operate agents on Claude Managed Agents, including agent and environment configuration, tool permissions, session budgets, and evaluation before release.
- Build and support workflows that run from Claude Tag, including channel configuration, access scoping, instructions, and routines.
- Build and evaluate agents with LangGraph and LangChain, using LangSmith for tracing, evaluation, and monitoring.
- Build and maintain agentic workflows used across the organization.
- Troubleshoot broken workflows, tool failures, and user issues to keep systems running effectively.
- Manage operational configuration in the Anthropic console, including model access, rate limits, usage policies, and team settings.
- Support prompt engineering efforts by helping teams create system prompts and instructions that are reliable in real-world use.
- Work across Anthropic and Microsoft environments, including connecting tools through agentic workflows.
- Advise teams on tool selection and practical AI implementation based on specific business problems.
- Translate emerging AI capabilities into scalable, usable solutions for internal teams.
- Operate effectively in an evolving environment where tooling, priorities, and use cases change quickly.

**What does it take to work in this role?**
