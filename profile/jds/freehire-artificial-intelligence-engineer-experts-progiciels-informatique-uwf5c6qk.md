ExpertsPro is a technology and digital transformation company helping businesses accelerate innovation through enterprise technology, AI, data and intelligent automation.

**The Role**

We are looking for an AI Engineer with practical experience in Generative AI, Large Language Models (LLMs), Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG) and AI agents.

You will work on building intelligent applications, automating enterprise workflows and integrating AI capabilities into business systems.

**Key Responsibilities**

- Design, develop and deploy AI-powered applications using Generative AI and LLMs.
- Build AI agents and multi-agent workflows to automate business processes.
- Develop RAG pipelines, semantic search and knowledge-based AI applications.
- Integrate LLMs with enterprise applications, APIs, databases and business systems.
- Work with models and APIs from OpenAI, Anthropic, Google Gemini and open-source providers.
- Implement prompt engineering, model evaluation, AI guardrails and output validation.
- Optimise AI applications for accuracy, latency, scalability and inference costs.
- Build and integrate backend services and APIs for AI-powered applications.
- Collaborate with engineering and client-facing teams to translate business requirements into working AI solutions.
- Contribute to technical POCs, solution demonstrations and production deployments.

- 3–6 years of experience in software development, AI or machine learning engineering.
- Strong programming skills in Python.
- Hands-on experience building applications using LLMs and Generative AI.
- Experience developing RAG-based applications using LangChain, LlamaIndex or similar frameworks.
- Knowledge of vector databases such as Pinecone, Weaviate, Chroma or FAISS.
- Experience with REST APIs, FastAPI and backend integrations.
- Understanding of embeddings, semantic search, prompt engineering and model evaluation.
- Familiarity with Git, Docker and cloud platforms such as AWS, Azure or GCP.
- Ability to take an AI solution from concept to deployment.

**Good to Have**
