**So, what's the role all about?**

You will join NiCE's CX engineering organization and help build cloud-native, enterprise-grade products. You will not just implement tickets: you will use AI to move faster, raise quality, and rethink how software is built.

**How will you make an impact?**

- Use AI as part of every step of your work: turn requirements into specs, generate and refactor code, write tests, review changes, and troubleshoot issues
- Design, build and deliver enterprise-grade backend features in Java/J2EE that perform, scale and integrate into a broad enterprise ecosystem, running on AWS or on-prem
- Understand business requirements and translate them into technical designs; implement business and technical logic in backend code, including REST/SOAP web services and integrations with third-party systems such as caching and message brokers
- Ramp up quickly on unfamiliar codebases and technologies, with AI as your accelerator
- Handle edge cases, error handling, performance, scalability and security before users find them, and analyze and fix defects quickly
- Help shape our agentic development lifecycle as it evolves: explore new AI tools, agents and automations, bring what works into how the team plans, builds, tests and releases, and share it with the team
- Collaborate with Product Management, Architects and Operations, build positive working relationships across teams, and take high accountability for delivering planned work

**Have you got what it takes?**

- B.Sc. / B.E. / B.Tech. in Computer Science or equivalent from a reputed institute, or a comparable track record of building real software
- 2+ years of recent hands-on experience in Java technologies
- **AI-first mindset (core requirement):** daily, hands-on use of AI coding tools and agents; ability to show concrete examples of results you delivered faster or better with AI; good judgment about when not to trust AI output; curiosity to keep experimenting
- Strong Java fundamentals: Core Java, J2EE ecosystem, algorithms, data structures, OO design and design patterns, with strong coding and defect-analysis skills
- Hands-on experience with Spring, Spring Boot, Hibernate and Tomcat, building web services using REST / SOAP, and SQL database development
- Excellent analytical and problem-solving skills, good communication (oral and written), time management, teamwork and fluent English
