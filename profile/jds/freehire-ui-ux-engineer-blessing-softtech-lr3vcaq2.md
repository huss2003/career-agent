**UI/UX Engineer**

**Company:** Blessing Softtech (OPC) Private Limited

**Location:** Pune, Maharashtra (In-person) or Remote

**Employment Type:** Full-time

**Reporting To:** Founder & CEO

**About Blessing Softtech**

Blessing Softtech is a DPIIT-recognised AI and technology company based in Pune, building next-generation AI-native products. We create innovative solutions across conversational AI, voice technology, intelligent automation and agentic systems.

We are looking for a creative and technically strong **UI/UX Engineer** who can transform complex ideas into intuitive, engaging and high-performance digital experiences.

**Role Overview**

As a UI/UX Engineer, you will take ownership of the complete product design and development lifecycle, from understanding user problems and creating wireframes to building interactive prototypes and shipping production-ready interfaces.

You will work directly with the Founder, engineering team and AI specialists to design experiences for modern AI-powered applications, including conversational interfaces, real-time voice systems, AI agents and enterprise platforms.

**Key Responsibilities**

- Design and develop end-to-end user experiences for web and mobile applications.
- Create wireframes, user flows, prototypes and high-fidelity UI designs using Figma.
- Build responsive and production-ready interfaces using React, Next.js, TypeScript and Tailwind CSS.
- Develop and maintain scalable design systems, reusable components and UI standards.
- Design intuitive interfaces for AI-powered products, chatbots, voice assistants and autonomous AI agents.
- Implement animations, transitions and micro-interactions using Framer Motion, Rive or Lottie.
- Conduct user research, usability testing and continuously improve product experiences.
- Ensure accessibility, responsiveness and cross-browser compatibility.
- Collaborate with backend and AI engineering teams to integrate frontend experiences with APIs and real-time systems.
- Monitor product performance, user engagement and conversion metrics.
- Use AI-assisted development tools such as Claude, Cursor, v0 and Figma AI to accelerate design and development.

**Required Technical Skills**

- Expert-level Figma knowledge.
- Strong HTML5, CSS3, JavaScript and TypeScript.
- Hands-on experience with React.js and Next.js.
- Tailwind CSS or equivalent modern styling frameworks.
- Experience building reusable design systems and component libraries.
- Knowledge of Framer Motion, Rive or Lottie.
- Understanding of responsive and mobile-first design principles.
- Familiarity with WCAG accessibility standards.
- Experience with prototyping and user testing.
- Knowledge of product analytics tools such as Mixpanel, Amplitude or PostHog.
- Familiarity with AI-assisted design and development workflows.

**Preferred Qualifications**

- 3+ years of experience designing and shipping digital products.
- Experience taking at least one product from concept to production.
- Strong portfolio demonstrating real-world projects and measurable outcomes.
- Experience writing production-level React frontend code.
- Experience designing AI, conversational, voice or agent-based interfaces.
- Knowledge of enterprise dashboards and data-intensive applications.
- Understanding of performance optimization and Core Web Vitals.
