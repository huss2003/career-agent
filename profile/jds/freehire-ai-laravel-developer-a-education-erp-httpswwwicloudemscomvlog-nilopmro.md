**AI Laravel Developer – Education ERP**

We are looking for an **AI Laravel Developer** who combines strong, hands-on experience in **Artificial Intelligence / Machine Learning** with solid backend development skills in **Laravel, Core PHP, or Node.js**. The primary focus of this role is **building and integrating AI-powered features** into our Education ERP and EdTech platforms.

The ideal candidate should be able to take an AI capability from idea to production — working with LLMs, ML models, and AI APIs — and wire it cleanly into an existing Laravel/PHP or Node.js codebase through robust APIs, background jobs, and well-designed data flows.

**Key Responsibilities**

• Design, build, and deploy **AI-powered features** for Education ERP and EdTech products—

• Integrate **Large Language Models (LLMs)** and AI APIs

• Build **RAG (Retrieval-Augmented Generation)** pipeline

• Write effective **prompts**, and design structured outputs, function/tool calling, and agent-style workflows for real business use cases.

• Develop, train, fine-tune, or adapt **ML models** in **Python**

• Develop and maintain backend modules in **Laravel / Core PHP / Node.js**, including **RESTful APIs** with authentication.

• Handle AI workloads efficiently using **queues, background jobs, caching, and streaming responses**; monitor cost, latency, and accuracy of AI features.

• Evaluate and improve AI output quality — testing, guardrails, hallucination checks, and handling sensitive student/institution data responsibly.

• Fix bugs, debug production issues, and troubleshoot data-related problems across AI and application layers.

• Coordinate with product, senior developers, and other teams to understand requirements and deliver features on time.

**Required Skills**

**AI / ML (Primary Focus)**

• Strong, hands-on experience building and shipping **AI/ML or GenAI** features in real projects

• Practical experience with **LLM APIs** (OpenAI, Claude, Gemini, or open-source LLMs via Ollama / Hugging Face).

• Good understanding of **prompt engineering, embeddings, vector search, and RAG** architectures.

• Working knowledge of **Python** for AI/ML, along with libraries/frameworks such as **LangChain, LlamaIndex, scikit-learn, TensorFlow, PyTorch, or Hugging Face Transformers**.

• Understanding of core **ML concepts** — data preprocessing, model training and evaluation, classification/regression, NLP basics.

**Backend Development**

• Hands-on experience with **Laravel Framework** and/or **Core PHP (OOP)**; **Node.js** (Express/NestJS) experience is a strong plus.

• Experience building and consuming **REST APIs**, and integrating third-party APIs and services.

• Good understanding of **MySQL / SQL** — joins, aggregation, query optimization, and database design/relationships.

• Familiarity with queues/jobs (Laravel Queues, Redis, BullMQ), Composer/npm, and Git/version control.

• Working knowledge of HTML, CSS, and JavaScript for front-end integration.
