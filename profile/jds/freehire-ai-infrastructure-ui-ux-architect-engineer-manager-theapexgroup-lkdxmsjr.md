**Job Description: MERN Stack Developer (AI Applications)**

**Experience:** 2-6 Years

**Role Overview**

We are seeking a skilled **MERN Stack Developer** with experience in building AI-powered web applications. The ideal candidate will have strong expertise in MongoDB, Express.js, React.js, and Node.js, along with exposure to Generative AI, LLMs, AI agents, and AI integration frameworks.

**Key Responsibilities**

- Design, develop, and maintain scalable web applications using the MERN stack.
- Integrate AI/ML models, Large Language Models (LLMs), and Generative AI services into business applications.
- Develop AI-driven features such as chatbots, virtual assistants, document processing solutions, and recommendation systems.
- Create and consume RESTful APIs and microservices for AI applications.
- Work with vector databases, embeddings, and Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG) architectures.
- Optimize application performance, security, and scalability.
- Collaborate with AI Engineers, Solution Architects, and Product Teams to deliver innovative solutions.
- Implement CI/CD pipelines and ensure code quality through testing and code reviews.
- Troubleshoot and resolve production issues in a timely manner.

**Required Skills**

- Strong experience with **MongoDB, Express.js, React.js, and Node.js**.
- Proficiency in JavaScript (ES6+), TypeScript, HTML5, CSS3, and React frameworks.
- Experience integrating AI services such as **OpenAI, Azure OpenAI, Anthropic, Gemini, or similar platforms**.
- Knowledge of AI concepts including **LLMs, Prompt Engineering, RAG, AI Agents, and Vector Databases**.
- Experience building REST APIs and WebSocket-based applications.
- Familiarity with cloud platforms such as **Azure, AWS, or GCP**.
- Understanding of authentication mechanisms, security best practices, and database design.
- Experience with Git, CI/CD pipelines, and Agile development methodologies.

**Preferred Skills**

- Experience with Azure AI Services, LangChain, Semantic Kernel, or Microsoft Agentic Framework.
- Exposure to Python for AI integrations and model development.
- Knowledge of Docker, Kubernetes, and containerized deployments.
- Understanding of Machine Learning and MLOps concepts.

**Educational Qualification**

- Bachelor's or Master's degree in Computer Science, Information Technology, or a related field.

**Key Competencies**

- Strong problem-solving and analytical skills.
- Excellent communication and stakeholder management abilities.
- Ability to work in a fast-paced, collaborative environment.
- Passion for AI, emerging technologies, and innovation
