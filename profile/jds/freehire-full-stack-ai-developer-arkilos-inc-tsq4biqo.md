About the Role  
We are seeking an experienced Senior Full Stack Developer to build, deploy, and maintain scalable web applications and AI-enabled business solutions.  
You will work across React.js frontends, Node.js and Python FastAPI backends, databases, and AWS infrastructure. The role includes integrating large language model (LLM) APIs and Python-based automation into business applications.  
The successful candidate will take ownership of technical delivery, work independently, and communicate effectively with colleagues and stakeholders across North America and India.

• Implement authentication, authorization, input validation, and secure handling of credentials and application data.  
• Write clean, maintainable code supported by appropriate such as playwright and postman automated tests and technical documentation.  
• Troubleshoot production issues and improve application performance, reliability, and scalability.

• Collaborate with stakeholders to translate business requirements into practical technical solutions.
