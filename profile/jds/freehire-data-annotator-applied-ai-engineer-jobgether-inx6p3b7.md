**This position is listed on behalf of a partner company, who manages all applications and next steps. Our partner is looking for a Data Annotator – Applied AI Engineer based in India.**

The Data Annotator – Applied AI Engineer will contribute to the development and improvement of advanced artificial intelligence systems through specialized technical evaluation and data annotation. This role goes beyond traditional annotation work, focusing on large language models (LLMs), generative AI applications, and intelligent agent workflows. You will assess AI-generated content, review technical implementations, and evaluate the accuracy and reliability of AI-powered solutions. Working with modern technologies such as Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG), Python, and AI development frameworks, you will help improve model performance and reasoning capabilities. The position offers the opportunity to apply practical AI engineering expertise to real-world model training and evaluation projects. This is a flexible, fully remote, part-time opportunity suited to professionals with hands-on experience in applied AI.

### Accountabilities:

- Analyze existing AI-powered applications, review their implementation logic, assess system architectures, and evaluate technical correctness.
- Evaluate large language models, transformer-based architectures, model configurations, and LLM integration workflows.
- Assess Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG) pipelines, including embedding models, vector databases, retrieval mechanisms, and context-generation processes.
- Review AI agent architectures, tool-calling mechanisms, multi-agent workflows, orchestration logic, and autonomous task execution.
- Perform specialized technical data annotation involving prompts, AI-generated responses, code, agent workflows, and technical documentation.
- Evaluate AI-generated outputs for factual accuracy, logical reasoning, contextual relevance, instruction adherence, and technical quality.
- Review and assess prompt engineering techniques, system instructions, prompt chains, and AI-generated responses.
- Examine Python-based AI implementations, identify logical errors, assess integration approaches, and recommend technically sound improvements.
- Evaluate LLM APIs, model integrations, fine-tuning strategies, inference pipelines, and AI model deployment workflows.
- Assess AI applications developed using frameworks such as LangChain, LlamaIndex, and other generative AI development technologies.
- Produce clear technical explanations, structured annotations, justifications, and actionable feedback to support AI model evaluation and improvement.
- Maintain high standards of annotation accuracy, consistency, and quality while following project-specific guidelines and evaluation criteria.
