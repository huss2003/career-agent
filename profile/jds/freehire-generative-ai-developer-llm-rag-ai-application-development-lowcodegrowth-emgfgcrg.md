**About the Project**

iOwnMyBenefits is developing an AI-powered employee benefits management platform that enables employees to track employer-provided and personally purchased insurance benefits, assess their coverage, identify potential gaps and obtain quotes for additional insurance products.

We are looking for a hands-on Generative AI Developer who can design, develop and integrate AI capabilities into our existing WordPress-based website and associated web application.

The selected candidate will be responsible for developing intelligent document processing, AI-powered benefits analysis, conversational AI assistants and automated workflows.

This is an opportunity to build an AI-driven fintech/insurtech application from the ground up.

**Key Responsibilities**

01. Develop AI-powered applications using OpenAI APIs and other Large Language Models (LLMs).
02. Build intelligent document processing capabilities to extract information from insurance policies, benefit statements and PDF documents.
03. Develop Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG) systems to enable AI to understand and answer questions based on uploaded insurance documents.
04. Build an AI Benefits Advisor chatbot that can understand employee benefits and respond to user queries.
05. Develop AI-based insurance coverage gap analysis and comparison functionality.
06. Implement AI workflows to identify coverage overlaps, limitations, exclusions and potential protection gaps.
07. Build automated data extraction and structured data storage pipelines.
08. Develop AI agents and workflow automation for benefits assessment, report generation and quote request processing.
09. Integrate AI services with WordPress and external applications through REST APIs.
10. Implement secure document processing, authentication, data privacy and access controls.
11. Optimize AI response accuracy, performance, reliability and API costs.
12. Work with the existing WordPress developer to integrate AI functionality into the platform.

**Initial Project Deliverables**

The candidate will initially work on developing the following AI capabilities:

1. **AI Policy Reader:** Upload insurance policy PDFs and automatically extract policy details, coverage amounts, premiums, exclusions and renewal dates.
2. **AI Benefits Inventory:** Convert extracted policy information into structured employee benefits records.
3. **AI Benefits Advisor:** Build a conversational assistant that answers questions based on the employee's actual benefits and uploaded policy documents.
4. **AI Coverage Gap Analysis:** Compare existing insurance coverage against employee income, family needs and financial protection requirements.
5. **AI Benefits Report:** Generate a personalized report highlighting existing coverage, potential gaps, overlaps and recommended follow-up actions.
6. **AI Quote Assistant:** Collect relevant information and prepare structured insurance quote requests for licensed insurance professionals.
