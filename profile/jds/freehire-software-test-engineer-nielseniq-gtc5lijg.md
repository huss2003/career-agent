We are seeking a talented and passionate **Performance Engineer** to join the NIQ Performance Engineering team.

The ideal candidate should have a solid foundation in performance testing, automation, and cloud-native technologies, along with strong programming skills.

This role offers the opportunity to work on next-generation performance engineering practices **leveraging AI,** observability, and automation to improve software quality and system scalability.

**Key Responsibilities**

- Design, develop, and execute performance tests for web, API, and microservices-based applications.
- Create and maintain performance test scripts using **JMeter, LoadRunner**, or similar tools.
- Support workload modeling, performance benchmarking, and capacity testing activities.
- Analyze test results and identify application, database, or infrastructure bottlenecks.
- Develop automation utilities and reusable components using Python etc.
- Utilize observability and monitoring platforms to investigate performance issues.
- Collaborate with development teams to troubleshoot and optimize system performance.
- Support AI-assisted initiatives such as test generation, analysis automation, and reporting enhancements.
- Contribute to continuous improvement of performance engineering frameworks and best practices.

**Required Skills & Experience**

- Solid experience in Performance Testing or Performance Engineering.
- Hands-on experience with **JMeter, LoadRunner, VTS**, or equivalent tools.
- Strong programming skills.
- Understanding of APIs, microservices, REST services, and distributed systems.
- Familiarity with **Kubernetes**, Linux, and cloud environments.
- Exposure to observability/APM solutions such as **Datadog, Dynatrace, Coralogix, New Relic, or AppDynamics**.
- Basic understanding of Generative AI tools such as **GitHub Copilot or Azure OpenAI**.
- Strong analytical and troubleshooting skills.

**Preferred Qualifications**

- Should have 3-5 years of strong experience with CI/CD pipelines and **GitHub Actions**.
- Exposure to log analytics platforms such as **Splunk** or **Coralogix**.
- Knowledge of performance testing within cloud-native architectures.
- Familiarity with AI-assisted engineering workflows.

**Ideal Candidate Profile**

✅ Strong foundation in performance testing and automation  
✅ Good development skills  
✅ Understanding of cloud-native applications and Kubernetes  
✅ Passion for leveraging AI and modern engineering practices  
✅ Strong analytical and problem-solving mindset
