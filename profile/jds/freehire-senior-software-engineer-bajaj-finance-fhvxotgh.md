The Product Engineer - VoiceNext AI Platform is responsible for developing, deploying, and supporting AI-powered conversational and generative AI solutions for the VoiceNext product ecosystem. The incumbent will work closely with Product, Business, AI, and Technology teams to build scalable AI applications leveraging Large Language Models (LLMs), Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG), APIs, and cloud-native platforms.

The role holder will support end-to-end product engineering activities including solution development, prompt engineering, AI application deployment, system integration, performance optimization, and platform monitoring. The role ensures VoiceNext AI solutions are scalable, secure, business-aligned, and deliver superior customer and employee experiences across channels.
