## What is your mission?

We are looking for an **AI Engineer (Azure AI & Agent Development)** to design, build, deploy, and optimize AI agents and LLM-powered solutions using **Azure AI Foundry**, **Azure AI Agent Service**, and modern agent frameworks. This role focuses on enterprise AI integrations, agent orchestration, model customization, cloud infrastructure, and delivering secure, scalable AI solutions that automate business workflows and drive business value.

**You will provide the best service to our partner brands by performing these tasks:**

- Design, develop, deploy, and maintain AI agents capable of performing multi-step tasks and automating business workflows.
- Build AI agents using **Azure AI Foundry**, **Azure AI Agent Service**, **LangGraph**, **Semantic Kernel**, and similar agent-development frameworks.
- Develop agent capabilities including **tool calling**, function execution, **retrieval-augmented generation (RAG)**, memory management, and workflow orchestration.
- Integrate AI agents with internal applications, external APIs, databases, and enterprise systems while ensuring secure data access and user-level permissions.
- Implement multi-agent workflows, human-in-the-loop approval processes, and security guardrails.
- Test, evaluate, troubleshoot, and optimize AI agent accuracy, reliability, scalability, and performance.
- Build and deploy AI agents within **Microsoft Teams** and other **Microsoft 365** applications.
- Integrate AI solutions with **Microsoft Graph API** to securely access authorized resources including **SharePoint**, **Outlook**, **OneDrive**, **Teams**, and calendars.
- Implement secure authentication and authorization using **Microsoft Entra ID**, **OAuth 2.0**, single sign-on (SSO), delegated permissions, application permissions, app registrations, and On-Behalf-Of (OBO) authentication flows.
- Develop backend services and REST APIs that support AI agent interactions with enterprise applications and business systems.
- Work with commercial and open-source/open-weight LLMs, including **Llama**, **Qwen**, and **Mistral**.
- Fine-tune and customize LLMs for domain-specific applications, enterprise workflows, and specialized use cases.
- Apply parameter-efficient fine-tuning techniques such as **LoRA** and **QLoRA**.
- Prepare training datasets, perform data preprocessing, and develop model evaluation pipelines.
- Evaluate, benchmark, and compare LLMs based on accuracy, latency, scalability, and cost.
- Optimize model inference, quantization, deployment, and serving performance.
- Utilize frameworks and libraries including **Hugging Face Transformers**, **PEFT**, **PyTorch**, and related tools.
- Support the deployment and integration of fine-tuned models into AI applications and agent workflows.
- Provision, configure, and manage AI infrastructure within **Microsoft Azure**.
- Build and maintain CI/CD pipelines using **Azure DevOps**, **Git**, and related deployment tools.
- Support infrastructure-as-code initiatives using **Terraform** or **Bicep**.
- Implement monitoring, logging, observability, troubleshooting, and production support for AI systems.
- Optimize cloud infrastructure for performance, reliability, security, and cost efficiency.
- Communicate AI solutions to clients, translate technical concepts into business value, and deliver training or knowledge-transfer sessions when required.
