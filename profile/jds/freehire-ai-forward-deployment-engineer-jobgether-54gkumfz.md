**This position is listed on behalf of a partner company, who manages all applications and next steps. Our partner is looking for an AI Forward Deployment Engineer based in India.**

As an AI Forward Deployment Engineer, you will play a key role in designing, developing, and deploying production-ready AI agents and intelligent features across enterprise software products and internal business functions. You will join a global AI Center of Excellence, helping shape the organization's AI capabilities while supporting engineering teams in adopting advanced AI technologies. Working primarily with Claude and Azure AI Foundry, you will build scalable agentic systems, retrieval solutions, and AI-powered applications. You will take ownership of the entire development lifecycle, from rapid prototyping to production deployment, evaluation, and continuous improvement. This role combines hands-on software engineering, AI innovation, and technical enablement in a fast-paced environment where solutions move from concept to production within weeks. You will also help establish engineering standards, strengthen AI governance, and accelerate the responsible adoption of AI across the organization.

### Accountabilities:

- Design, develop, deploy, and maintain production-grade AI agents and AI-powered features, including new solutions and enhancements to existing applications.
- Build end-to-end agentic systems, covering task decomposition, tool integration, context management, memory strategies, orchestration, human-in-the-loop workflows, and error recovery.
- Develop retrieval-augmented generation (RAG) solutions through document processing, chunking, embeddings, hybrid search, reranking, and citation mechanisms to ensure accurate, data-grounded responses.
- Deploy and manage AI workloads through Azure AI Foundry, including model deployments, endpoints, content safety configurations, private networking, and cost and quota optimization.
- Use Claude Code as a core development tool while establishing reusable engineering practices and standards for other technical teams.
- Design and maintain evaluation frameworks, golden datasets, and automated testing processes to measure AI accuracy, latency, cost efficiency, retrieval quality, and tool-call reliability.
- Implement AI security measures, including guardrails, prompt-injection protection, personally identifiable information (PII) handling, least-privilege access, and data protection controls.
- Integrate AI solutions into CI/CD pipelines using version-controlled prompts and agent configurations, while implementing tracing, monitoring, and token-cost observability.
- Support internal engineering teams in adopting shared AI services by reviewing architectures, troubleshooting integrations, sharing technical guidance, and documenting reusable development patterns.
- Define and monitor key performance indicators related to AI adoption, solution quality, and cost per outcome, using insights to drive continuous improvement.
- Collaborate within an agile development environment, manage sprint responsibilities, maintain accurate project documentation, and ensure timely delivery of assigned work.

## Requirements:

- 4–6 years of software engineering experience, including at least 2 years of hands-on experience building and deploying production-grade applications powered by large language models (LLMs).
- Proven experience developing agentic AI systems involving multi-step tool usage, structured outputs, state management, memory, and error recovery, with the ability to determine when alternative approaches are more appropriate.
- Strong knowledge of Claude or comparable advanced LLM platforms, including APIs, tool integrations, and agent development SDKs.
- Practical experience with AI-assisted development tools, particularly Claude Code, and the ability to incorporate them into everyday engineering workflows.
- Solid understanding of retrieval engineering, including embeddings, vector databases, hybrid search, reranking, and retrieval evaluation methodologies.
- Experience with AI evaluation and observability, including offline testing, human review processes, tracing, performance monitoring, and cost instrumentation.
- Strong Python development skills, complemented by proficiency in at least one additional language such as TypeScript, C#, or Java.
- Hands-on experience with Azure AI Foundry or Azure OpenAI, including model deployment, endpoint configuration, and content safety controls.
- Good knowledge of Azure services such as App Service, Function Apps, Key Vault, Storage, Entra ID managed identities, and Application Insights.
- Experience implementing CI/CD pipelines with Azure DevOps, including YAML configurations, multi-stage deployments, and secrets management.
- Familiarity with REST APIs, asynchronous application design, enterprise system integrations, SQL and NoSQL databases, vector stores, Git, and code review practices.
- Strong understanding of AI security principles, including access control, secrets management, data exfiltration risks, and compliance requirements.
- Excellent communication and collaboration skills, with the ability to explain technical concepts, architectural decisions, and AI-related trade-offs to both technical and non-technical stakeholders.
- Experience working in agile environments and adapting to fast-paced, AI-driven software development lifecycles.
- Bachelor's degree in Computer Science or a related discipline, or equivalent practical experience.

Additional experience considered an advantage:

- Experience with Model Context Protocol (MCP), including developing tools and connectors that enable governed access to internal systems.
- Background developing AI features for commercial SaaS platforms, particularly in multi-tenant environments with strict data-isolation requirements.
- Experience with document AI, large-scale unstructured data extraction, classical machine learning, or model fine-tuning.
- Familiarity with Databricks, Azure Data Factory, or comparable data orchestration platforms.
- Knowledge of AI governance frameworks such as the EU AI Act, NIST AI Risk Management Framework, or ISO 42001.
