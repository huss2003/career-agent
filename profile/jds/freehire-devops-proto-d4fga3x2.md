**The role**

Proto is hiring a DevOps Engineer to manage, scale, and secure the cloud and infrastructure powering our AI agent platform – products that put AI agents in the hands of governments, regulators, and businesses across emerging markets.

You will own infrastructure as code, automated CI/CD pipelines, cloud infrastructure, and site reliability from day one. You will also play an important role in deploying and managing GPU infrastructure for AI workloads, supporting LLM and other AI model deployments, and helping enterprise clients implement Proto's platform within their own on-premise environments.

You will work in a lean, remote-first team where infrastructure improvements, AI deployments, and customer implementations happen in days, not quarters.

**What you will do**

You will build and maintain resilient cloud infrastructure, GPU environments, and automated release pipelines while supporting enterprise and on-premise deployments.

- Design, build, and maintain scalable cloud infrastructure using Infrastructure as Code (IaC) tools like Terraform.
- Develop, automate, and optimize robust CI/CD deployment pipelines for high availability and zero-downtime releases.
- Manage Kubernetes clusters, container orchestration, and microservices deployment across cloud and on-premise environments.
- Deploy, configure, and optimize GPU infrastructure for AI workloads, including GPU-enabled servers, NVIDIA GPUs, CUDA, and GPU drivers.
- Support deployment and operation of AI models, LLM inference services, and GPU-accelerated applications.
- Assist with infrastructure sizing, GPU resource allocation, and performance optimization for AI model serving and inference workloads.
- Lead and support customer deployments in on-premise and private cloud environments, including restricted or air-gapped networks.
- Package, configure, and deploy containerized applications using Docker and Docker Compose, including Kubernetes-based deployments.
- Work with customer IT and infrastructure teams to troubleshoot deployment issues involving networking, firewalls, proxies, storage, and system dependencies.
- Implement monitoring, logging, tracing, and alerting systems to ensure high service uptime and performance.
- Drive Site Reliability Engineering (SRE) practices, incident management, and automated failover and recovery processes.
- Collaborate with development, security, and QA teams to enforce DevSecOps best practices and infrastructure security standards.
