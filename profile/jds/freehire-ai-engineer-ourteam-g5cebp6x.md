Position Overview

We are seeking an experienced Automation & AI Integration Specialist to own the technical build, optimization, and scale of our internal and client-facing workflow automations. You will translate task-deconstruction strategies into functional automated pipelines—connecting CRMs, analytics tools, candidate data flows, and AI agents.

Key Responsibilities

1\. Core Workflow & Process Automation

- Pipeline Build & Maintenance: Design, build, and maintain end-to-end automated workflows using platforms like Make (Integromat), Zapier, n8n, or custom Python/API scripts.
- Google Workspace & Spreadsheet Integration: Automate data extraction, transformation, and reporting across Google Sheets, Google Docs, Drive, and external databases.
- System Integration: Connect key platforms across HR Tech, Applicant Tracking Systems (ATS), CRM systems, GA4 analytics, and candidate survey databases.

2\. AI Teaming & Task Automation (Step 5 Execution)

- AI Tool Deployment: Integrate LLMs (OpenAI API, Claude API, Gemini API) and custom AI agents into business workflows to automate repetitive admin, data enrichment, and reporting.
- Task Deconstruction Support: Work alongside our HR transformation leads to turn manual business processes into automated, event-driven pipelines.

3\. Lead Generation & Diagnostic Automation

- Interactive Diagnostic Tools: Build and maintain automated scoring engines for client lead magnets (e.g., 2026 Work Redesign Diagnostic Tool).
- Candidate & Client Data Mining: Automate the ingestion, aggregation, and anonymization of candidate/market survey data to generate automated benchmarking reports.
