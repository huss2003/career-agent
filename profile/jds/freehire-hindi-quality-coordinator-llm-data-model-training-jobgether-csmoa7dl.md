In this role, you will contribute to the development and improvement of advanced artificial intelligence systems by ensuring the quality, accuracy, and linguistic integrity of Hindi-language data used to train and evaluate Large Language Models (LLMs). You will support multilingual AI projects by coordinating language quality activities and ensuring that datasets meet established linguistic and project-specific standards. Working at the intersection of language expertise, data quality, and AI technology, you will help improve how AI models understand, interpret, and generate Hindi content. You will collaborate with linguists, data specialists, and project stakeholders to maintain consistent quality across training and evaluation workflows. This opportunity is ideal for a detail-oriented Hindi language professional interested in the rapidly evolving fields of artificial intelligence, natural language processing, and machine learning. ### Accountabilities:

- Coordinate Hindi language quality activities: Support the planning and execution of linguistic quality processes for AI training, data generation, and model evaluation projects.
- Ensure linguistic accuracy: Review Hindi-language content and datasets to assess grammar, spelling, vocabulary, fluency, cultural relevance, and overall linguistic consistency.
- Support LLM data preparation: Contribute to the validation, annotation, and refinement of Hindi-language datasets used for training and evaluating large language models.
- Monitor data quality: Identify linguistic inconsistencies, errors, ambiguities, and potential quality issues, ensuring that data meets established project guidelines and quality benchmarks.
- Coordinate quality workflows: Work with linguists, reviewers, data specialists, and project teams to organize quality assurance activities and maintain consistent delivery standards.
- Evaluate AI-generated content: Assess Hindi-language model outputs for accuracy, relevance, naturalness, contextual understanding, and alignment with linguistic and cultural expectations.
- Provide feedback and recommendations: Document recurring issues, communicate quality findings, and recommend improvements to annotation guidelines, review processes, and linguistic standards.
- Maintain project documentation: Ensure quality reports, review results, feedback records, and project-related documentation are accurate and up to date.
- Support continuous improvement: Identify opportunities to enhance linguistic evaluation methods, data quality processes, and overall model training effectiveness.
- Collaborate across teams: Coordinate with internal and external stakeholders to address quality concerns, resolve project challenges, and ensure alignment with delivery expectations.
