**Position Overview**

We are seeking a detail-oriented and technically proficient professional to oversee the management and continuous development of our web infrastructure and version-controlled repositories. The successful candidate will be responsible for maintaining our Jekyll-based website, administering GitHub repositories, leveraging AI-assisted development platforms to deliver high-quality web solutions, and serving as the primary technical steward of the organization's digital presence.

**Key Responsibilities**

*Website Development & Maintenance*

- Administer and update the organization's Jekyll-based website, including the authoring and modification of content, templates, and configurations in accordance with established standards
- Ensure the website remains current, functional, and aligned with organizational objectives across all browsers and devices
- Optimize website performance, speed, and capacity to effectively handle traffic demands
- Implement and maintain website security measures to protect sensitive data and user information
- Monitor website traffic, uptime, and performance metrics, escalating issues as appropriate

*Code Quality & Testing*

- Evaluate and author code to ensure it meets industry standards, is properly structured, and is compatible with relevant browsers, devices, and operating systems
- Conduct thorough testing and debugging to identify and resolve technical issues in a timely manner
- Write clean, well-documented, and maintainable code in accordance with organizational development standards

*Repository Management*

- Create, organize, and maintain GitHub repositories; enforce version control best practices including branching strategies, pull request workflows, and comprehensive documentation to support team collaboration and code integrity
- Author and maintain README files, wikis, technical guides, and internal documentation for all managed codebases and workflows

*AI-Assisted Development & Hosting*

- Utilize Claude Code to develop, test, and deploy code efficiently and accurately
- Employ Cowork to design, build, and host web properties on behalf of the organization
- Stay current with emerging AI-assisted development tools and apply relevant advancements to improve operational efficiency

*Integrations & Data Management*

- Manage integrations with third-party APIs, plugins, and back-end web services as required
- Oversee the integrity and functionality of data connections between front-end interfaces and underlying systems

*Stakeholder Collaboration & Communication*

- Serve as the primary technical liaison between non-technical staff, designers, and external vendors, translating business requirements into actionable technical solutions
- Collaborate with designers and content stakeholders to ensure visual and functional design intent is accurately realized
- Communicate proactively with relevant stakeholders regarding project status, technical decisions, and potential risks
- Gather, clarify, and refine specifications and requirements based on organizational and technical needs

*Continuous Improvement*

- Maintain awareness of evolving web technologies, industry trends, and best practices, and apply relevant advancements to organizational operations
- Identify opportunities to improve the efficiency, reliability, and scalability of the organization's web infrastructure on an ongoing basis

**Required Qualifications**

- Demonstrated proficiency with **Jekyll** or comparable static site generator frameworks
- Working knowledge of **HTML, CSS, Markdown**, and **Liquid templating**
- Proven experience with **Git and GitHub**, including repository administration, branching, pull requests, and issue tracking
- Ability to effectively operate **Claude Code** and **Cowork** in a professional development environment
- Strong analytical and problem-solving skills with a high degree of independent judgment
- Excellent written and verbal communication skills

**Preferred Qualifications**

- Experience with CI/CD pipelines and GitHub Actions
- Familiarity with web hosting environments and deployment workflows
- Experience with third-party API integrations and back-end data services
- Prior experience integrating AI-assisted tooling within a structured development practice
- Knowledge of web security principles and best practices

**Work Environment & Expectations**

The role requires a high degree of self-direction, technical accountability, and the ability to manage multiple concurrent workstreams. The candidate is expected to maintain thorough documentation, adhere to organizational coding standards, and communicate proactively with stakeholders regarding project status and technical decisions.
