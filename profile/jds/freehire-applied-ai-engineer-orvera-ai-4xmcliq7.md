**About Orvera**

Orvera AI builds voice AI agents that answer and make phone calls for enterprises in healthcare, insurance and contact-center operations. We are a sister company of AMInfoweb, which has run contact-center and healthcare operations for about 18 years, so our agents are built on real knowledge of how these calls work.

**About the Role**

As an Applied AI Engineer on Orvera' s Forward Deployed Engineering team, you will design, build and ship the voice AI agents our enterprise customers run in production. You own the agent: conversation design, tool calls, integrations, evaluations, and reliability on live calls. You will work alongside Forward Deployed Engineers, who own customer discovery and deployment.

The agents you build handle high-volume, high-visibility calls in regulated industries, often across federated environments with many applications, teams and operational constraints.

**What You Will Do**

1. Build and deploy production-grade voice AI agents for inbound and outbound enterprise call workflows.
2. Turn call flows and business rules into robust agent architectures: LLM orchestration, tool calling, business logic and fallbacks.
3. Engineer for real-time voice: latency budgets, turn-taking, interruptions, silence handling, and speech-to-text and text-to-speech tuning.
4. Build backend services and integrations with Python, FastAPI, REST APIs and webhooks, connecting agents to CRMs, contact-center platforms and systems of record.
5. Apply strong practices in promptand context engineering, evaluations, guardrails and observability, including automated test calls and regression suites.
6. Debug issues across AI behavior, audio, telephony, integrations, application code and production environments.
7. Join customer calls with the FDE when technical depth is needed, and explain trade-offs clearly.
8. Own your agents from design through go-live and continuousimprovement, using call transcripts and metrics to drive fixes.
9. Turn repeatable patterns into reusable components and engineering playbooks.

We value engineers who combine technical depth, customer empathy and a bias for shipping. You should be comfortable moving from a customer conversation to architecture, into code, and into production debugging when needed. We work with direct communication and high standards.
