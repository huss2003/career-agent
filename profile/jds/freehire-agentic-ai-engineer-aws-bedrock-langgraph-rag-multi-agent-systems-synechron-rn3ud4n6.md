**Job Summary**

Synechron is seeking a **Senior Agentic AI / Generative AI Engineer** to design, develop, and scale autonomous, production-grade AI systems.

The role will focus on building complex multi-agent orchestration frameworks and advanced Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG) systems using AWS Bedrock as the primary cloud foundation model platform. The engineer will work with structured and unstructured enterprise datasets to develop autonomous reasoning, planning, memory, and tool-calling workflows that safely automate complex business processes.

The position will contribute to business objectives by enabling intelligent workflow automation, improving operational efficiency, supporting reliable decision-making, and delivering secure, scalable, measurable, and maintainable AI solutions.
