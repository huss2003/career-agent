**About the Role:**

We are seeking a **Product-oriented AI Engineer** to build the intelligent engine behind our Pharma Commercial modules. In this role, you will build Python-based agents and RAG systems designed to optimize the "Commercial Loop"—from personalizing HCP interactions to automating marketing content reviews. This is not just about prompt engineering; it is about building robust data pipelines that feed into **LLMs** and **SLMs** to drive real-world product outcomes. You will be responsible for translating complex customer pain points into reliable, scalable, and delightful AI Product experiences using a Big Data backbone that drive field-force effectiveness and brand performance.

**Key Responsibilities:**

- **High-Performance Python Engineering:** Develop production-grade Python services using **FastAPI** or **Pydantic** to orchestrate AI Agents, ensuring asynchronous handling of long-running LLM tasks.
- **Feature Ownership:** Lead the technical design and implementation of AI features (e.g., automated reasoning, smart summaries, predictive agents) from ideation to production. Build Python-based agents that automate "Next Best Action" (NBA) logic, helping sales reps prioritize which HCPs to visit based on real-time prescription (Rx) data and sentiment analysis from CRM logs.
- **Large-Scale RAG Orchestration:** Architect Retrieval-Augmented Generation systems that interface directly with Big Data environments (**Snowflake**, **Databricks**, or **Hadoop**) to provide instant answers on product positioning, competitor intelligence, and formulary access.
- **Big Data Intelligence:** Leverage **PySpark** or **Dask** to preprocess, clean, and vectorize massive datasets for model grounding and fine-tuning.
- **Commercial Data Intelligence:** Leverage PySpark to process and vectorize massive commercial datasets, including Veeva CRM activity, IQVIA/Symphony claims data, and digital marketing engagement metrics.
- **Agentic Logic & Tools:** Build Python-based "tools" for agents that can query structured data warehouses, execute code, or interface with external APIs to fulfill user requests. Build AI Agents that don't just "chat" but perform multi-step actions using tools/APIs. You will optimize the "chain of thought" to ensure users get predictable, high-quality outcomes.
- **Performance & Latency Engineering:** Balance the use of **LLMs** (for complex reasoning) and **SLMs** (for speed and cost-efficiency) to ensure a snappy user experience.
- **Data-Driven Iteration:** Use product analytics and AI observability tools to identify where models fail in the "real world" and iterate on prompts, data chunks, or model architectures accordingly.

**Role Requirements**

- **5+ years** of experience building software products, with at least **1.5 years** focused on Generative AI.
- **The Python Expert:** You write clean, maintainable, and efficient Python code. You are comfortable with decorators, generators, and managing complex dependencies.
- **Product Intuition:** You understand that a model with 99% accuracy is useless if it takes 30 seconds to respond. You know when to trade off "smartness" for "speed."
- **Data Engineering Savvy:** Ability to navigate complex Big Data environments to find and clean the data needed to ground your AI models. You are comfortable writing complex SQL or Spark jobs to extract the context your AI agent needs.
- **Full-Stack Mindset:** While you focus on AI, you are comfortable touching the API layer and collaborating closely with Frontend engineers on streaming responses and UI feedback loops.
- **Privacy & Compliance**: Familiarity with handling sensitive prescriber data and adhering to GDPR/CCPA and internal compliance "walls" between Commercial and Medical data.
