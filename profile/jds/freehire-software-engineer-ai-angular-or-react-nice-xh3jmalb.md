**So, what's the role all about?**

You will join NiCE's CX engineering organization and help build cloud-native products. You will work in an agentic software development lifecycle, where engineers and AI agents together design, build, test, review and ship. You will not just implement tickets: you will use AI to move faster, raise quality, and rethink how software is built.

**How will you make an impact?**

- Use AI as part of every step of your work: turn requirements into specs, generate and refactor code, write tests, review changes, and troubleshoot issues
- Deliver small-to-medium features from idea to production with growing autonomy, in whichever stack the problem needs
- Build intuitive, polished user experiences, including interfaces for AI-driven features such as conversational assistants, insights and recommendations
- Ramp up quickly on unfamiliar codebases and technologies, with AI as your accelerator
- Handle edge cases, error states, performance and accessibility before users find them
- Help shape our agentic development lifecycle as it evolves: explore new AI tools, agents and automations, bring what works into how the team plans, builds, tests and releases, and share it with the team
- Collaborate with UX, product and senior engineers, and push solutions forward independently

**Have you got what it takes?**

- B.Sc. / B.E. / B.Tech. in Computer Science or equivalent, or a comparable track record of building real software
- 1-3 years of hands-on software development experience
- **AI-first mindset (core requirement):** daily, hands-on use of AI coding tools and agents; ability to show concrete examples of results you delivered faster or better with AI; good judgment about when not to trust AI output; curiosity to keep experimenting
- Strong engineering fundamentals: OOP/OOD, data structures, testing, APIs and system thinking
- Proven ability to learn new languages and frameworks quickly, and to build real projects end to end
- Good communication, teamwork and fluent English

**You will have an advantage if you also have:**

- UI experience (for example React or Angular, TypeScript, HTML/CSS) and a strong sense of UX and attention to detail
- Experience building AI-powered interfaces (chat, streaming responses, AI assistants)
- Understanding of .NET framework or another backend stack, and REST APIs

- Public evidence of your AI-first work: demos, repos, side projects or a GitHub portfolio
