Role Overview

We are looking for a hands-on **AI Framework Consultant** to design and establish a reusable **AI-enabled software development framework** that can be adopted across existing and future projects.

The role will focus on integrating AI throughout the software development lifecycle, from requirements and architecture to development, testing, documentation, and deployment. The ideal candidate should have practical experience with **BMAD or similar AI-driven/spec-driven methodologies, Human Layer/Human-in-the-Loop approaches, agentic workflows, and AI-assisted development tools**.

The objective is to help our engineering teams **accelerate development, improve quality and consistency, and effectively use AI while maintaining appropriate human oversight and governance**.

Key Responsibilities

- Design a scalable and reusable **AI-enabled development framework** for adoption across multiple projects.
- Establish AI-assisted workflows across **requirements, architecture, development, code review, testing, documentation, and deployment**.
- Implement **BMAD or comparable spec-driven/AI-native development approaches**.
- Design **Human Layer/Human-in-the-Loop** checkpoints for architecture, code, security, production changes, and other critical decisions.
- Build and optimize **AI agents and agentic workflows** for engineering activities and automation.
- Integrate modern AI development tools such as **GitHub Copilot, Claude Code, Cursor, Codex, Azure AI, and MCP-based solutions**.
- Define standards for **context engineering, prompt management, AI-generated code, security, testing, governance, and responsible AI usage**.
- Develop reusable templates, prompts, agents, accelerators, playbooks, and engineering standards.
- Mentor development teams and drive the adoption of **AI-augmented software engineering practices**.
