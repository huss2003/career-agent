**Day to Day**

This person will own the **design, testing, and optimization of prompts for several internal GenAI applications**, working closely with AI engineers, data teams, and SMEs to improve accuracy and reduce hallucinations. They'll troubleshoot whether poor responses are caused by the prompt, RAG/retrieval process, vectorization, content, or underlying data and then partner with the appropriate team to fix the issue. The ultimate goal is to make the AI applications faster and more reliable for both technical and non-technical users.
