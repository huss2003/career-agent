**Role Overview**

As an Artificial Intelligence and Machine Learning Engineer Lead, you will design and develop generative AI and agent-based solutions that drive revenue growth and operational efficiency. Working alongside solution architects, data scientists, and machine learning engineers, you will build scalable applications powered by large language models (LLMs), integrate them into enterprise platforms and APIs, and evaluate these systems for accuracy, reliability, safety, and effective grounding.

**Key Responsibilities**

\* Generative AI Development: Design and build generative AI applications leveraging both proprietary and open-weight large language models.

\* Agentic AI Architecture: Develop agent-based systems and workflows supporting planning, memory, tool use, orchestration, human oversight, and autonomous task execution.

\* Enterprise Integration: Integrate AI solutions seamlessly with existing enterprise platforms, APIs, and core business workflows.

\* API & Service Deployment: Build and deploy scalable RESTful services using Python frameworks such as Flask or FastAPI.

\* Evaluation & AI Safety: Continuously evaluate model, agent, and end-to-end system performance across accuracy, reliability, grounding, and safety benchmarks.

**Required Qualifications**

\* Experience: 69 years of overall professional experience, including at least 4 years working directly with artificial intelligence, generative AI, or agent-based AI systems.

\* GenAI & LLM Engineering: Proven experience designing and building GenAI-powered applications using open-source and proprietary LLMs.

\* Agentic Workflows: Hands-on expertise building agent-based systems (planning, memory, tool use, orchestration) and implementing multi-agent, human-in-the-loop, and autonomous workflows.

\* Advanced LLM Techniques: Practical mastery of prompting techniques, structured outputs, model routing, context and memory management, and Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG).

\* Programming & ML Stack: Strong proficiency in Python along with machine learning frameworks and libraries such as scikit-learn, TensorFlow, PyTorch, Keras, or pandas.

\* Cloud, Data & APIs: Experience with vector databases, retrieval systems, REST services (Flask or FastAPI), and cloud platforms such as AWS, Google Cloud Platform (GCP), or Microsoft Azure.

\* Education: Bachelors degree in engineering or a related technical field, or an equivalent combination of education, training, and work experience.

**Preferred Qualifications**

\* Model Fine-Tuning: Experience fine-tuning large language models, including parameter-efficient fine-tuning (PEFT) techniques such as LoRA and QLoRA.

\* Orchestration Frameworks: Familiarity with agent and LLM orchestration tools such as LangChain, LlamaIndex, AutoGen, or the OpenAI Assistants API.

\* Production MLOps & Governance: Knowledge of production machine learning operations (MLOps), CI/CD pipelines, monitoring, observability, security, compliance, and data governance.
