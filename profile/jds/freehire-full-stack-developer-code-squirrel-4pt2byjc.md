### The role

You’ll be a hands‑on Laravel developer working across client projects and internal products. You’ll take a feature from spec to production, covering the back end, front end, tests and deployment. Some of the work is new builds, some is extending and fixing existing codebases, and some is auditing and improving code other people wrote.

We expect you to work independently, own your tickets, and tell us early when something's off.

### What you’ll be doing

- Building and maintaining Laravel applications end to end: APIs, admin panels, background jobs, integrations and front ends
- Building modern front ends in React and Next.js
- Working on a large existing project that includes a significant jQuery front end, which you’ll be extending and maintaining alongside more modern parts of the stack
- Turning specs and tickets into working, tested code, and pushing back when a spec doesn't make sense
- Designing database schemas and writing efficient queries, and fixing the slow ones
- Integrating with third‑party systems such as payment gateways, CRMs, Microsoft 365/SharePoint, and client APIs
- Building AI‑powered features into client applications
- Using AI coding agents in your daily workflow, and reviewing their output as carefully as you’d review a colleague’s
- Writing automated tests and keeping CI pipelines green
- Reviewing teammates' merge requests and having your own reviewed
- Contributing to code audits of client and third‑party codebases
- Keeping your tickets updated in Jira and committing often with ticket references

### 4+ years of commercial software development

### How we work

### Communication.

Half the team is in the Philippines, so clear written communication matters a lot. You'll post daily standups in Mattermost, keep ticket comments detailed enough that someone else could pick up your work, and flag blockers early. If you don't know something, ask. Nobody here will think less of you for it, but they will if you sit on a problem for two days.

### Delivery.

Our projects run to set milestones, with parts of an application due on fixed dates. You'll need to estimate your own work honestly and hit what you commit to, or speak up early when you can't.

### Process.

Agile, ticket-driven work through Jira and GitLab with merge request reviews on everything and automated QA/UAT environments for client testing.

This role is Brisbane-based and mostly remote. You'll need to live locally and have the right to work in Australia.

After that, it's a minimum of one day a week in the office, and the rest of the week you can work from home. Wednesdays are our main in‑office day, when most of the team is in.

Hours are full-time, standard Brisbane business hours, with some flexibility on start and finish times.
