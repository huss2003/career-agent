### Accountabilities:

- Design, develop, and deploy production-ready AI systems, including LLMs, RAG pipelines, agentic workflows, and machine learning/deep learning solutions.
- Select appropriate models, architectures, and technologies while balancing accuracy, latency, cost, scalability, and complexity.
- Train, fine-tune, and optimize machine learning models using frameworks such as PyTorch or Keras, including data preparation and model evaluation.
- Research, implement, and benchmark emerging AI techniques before integrating them into production environments.
- Develop APIs, inference pipelines, backend integrations, and supporting infrastructure for AI-powered applications.
- Define and monitor performance metrics related to model quality, latency, cost, and reliability.
- Evaluate, debug, and optimize AI systems after deployment, ensuring production readiness and identifying potential regressions before they affect users.
- Collaborate with software engineers, backend developers, product teams, and engineering leadership to integrate AI capabilities into existing applications and workflows.
- Contribute to technical discussions, architecture decisions, and engineering best practices.
- Document system architecture, implementation strategies, technical decisions, and operational processes to support maintainability and scalability.
- Review machine learning research papers, reproduce relevant findings, and translate promising research into practical engineering solutions.
- Contribute to ongoing AI research, experimentation, and innovation initiatives.
- Work effectively with globally distributed engineering and research teams while adapting to evolving technical and business priorities.

- 3+ years of hands-on AI/ML engineering experience, including experience deploying at least one AI/ML system into production.
- Strong understanding of natural language processing fundamentals, including tokenization, embeddings, classification, named entity recognition (NER), and information extraction.
- Solid software engineering skills, including coding, Git, testing, debugging, API development, and system design.
- Practical experience training machine learning models using PyTorch or Keras.
- Understanding of generative model architectures, including Transformers and Variational Autoencoders (VAEs).
