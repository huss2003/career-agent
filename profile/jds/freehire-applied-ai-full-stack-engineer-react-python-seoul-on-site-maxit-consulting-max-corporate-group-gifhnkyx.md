# Full Stack Software Engineer – AI Products

**Seoul, South Korea (Gangnam) | On-site | Full-time**

MaxIT Consulting is looking for a **Full Stack Engineer, Applied AI** to join a seed-stage, AI-native company building the operating system for drug programs, HQ in the San Francisco Bay Area with a team in Seoul. The platform already serves leading biopharma companies.

## The Opportunity

This role spans the whole stack: backend services, applied AI workflows, RAG and search pipelines, APIs and the customer-facing product, working close to pilot customers. This is a production role, not research demos or prototypes.

## Key Responsibilities

- Design and build backend services on Python, FastAPI and Postgres.
- Build applied AI workflows with the OpenAI API, LangGraph, vector databases and search systems.
- Develop ingestion, indexing and search pipelines for scientific, clinical and regulatory documents.
- Build customer-facing features end to end, from data model and API to the React/TypeScript frontend.
- Ground AI outputs in source data with traceable, verifiable citations.
- Work directly with the leadership team, domain experts and early customers, owning outcomes rather than tickets.

## Required Qualifications

- 2+ years shipping production software end to end.
- Strong backend fundamentals: APIs, databases and services.
- Comfortable through the frontend with React and TypeScript.
- Hands-on production experience with LLMs, agents, RAG or vector databases.
- Fast, accountable builder who uses AI coding tools (Claude Code, Cursor) daily.
- Works comfortably in English with a US-based team.

## Preferred Qualifications

- LangGraph, agent frameworks or RAG systems designed and operated in production.
- Vector databases, semantic search and knowledge graphs.
- Enterprise SaaS, healthcare or life sciences products; understanding of scientific, clinical, regulatory or patent data.
- Seed or early-stage startup experience.

Life sciences experience is not required.

## Work Arrangement

On-site role in the Seoul office (Gangnam district); fully remote is not possible. One remote day per week may be possible for a strong profile. Candidates must be based in Seoul. You will work daily in English with the US-based leadership and engineering team; US visa sponsorship may be considered after six months.

## Hiring Process

Resume review, take-home assignment, two technical interviews and a cultural interview.
