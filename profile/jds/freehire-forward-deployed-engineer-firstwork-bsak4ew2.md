**About FirstWork**

FirstWork is an applied AI company building the next generation of workforce operations technology.

We work with companies across staffing, logistics, healthcare, and retail that manage large frontline workforces. These businesses deal with complex, highly manual processes involving hiring, onboarding, document verification, compliance, training, and ongoing workforce management.

We're using AI agents to automate these workflows, working alongside human teams and integrating with the systems businesses already use.

Having raised **$15M in our Series A**, led by Earlybird Venture Capital, bringing our total funding to $20M, we're entering an exciting phase of growth. With established enterprise customers and a growing product footprint, we're expanding beyond hiring and onboarding to build a comprehensive AI-powered workforce operations platform.

**About the Role**

We're looking for a **Forward Deployed Engineer (FDE)** to work at the intersection of product engineering, customer implementation, and applied AI.

In this role, you'll work directly with enterprise customers to understand their hiring, onboarding, and compliance workflows and translate those requirements into working solutions using FirstWork.

You'll own client implementations end to end, from discovery and solution design to integrations, automation, deployment, and ongoing support. You'll build integrations with external systems, configure AI-powered document verification workflows, develop automations, and work closely with our product and engineering teams to solve customer-specific challenges.

This is a hands-on engineering role where you'll write code, solve real-world technical problems, interact directly with customers, and see the impact of your work in live enterprise environments.
