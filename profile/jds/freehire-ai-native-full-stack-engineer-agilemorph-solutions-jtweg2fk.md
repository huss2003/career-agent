## About the Role

We're looking for an exceptionally AI-native Full-Stack Engineer who knows how to leverage AI to build, ship, and scale software significantly faster and more effectively than traditional engineering workflows.

This isn't a conventional engineering role where you pick up tickets, write code, submit PRs, and wait for the next assignment. We want someone who thinks like an engineer, operates like a product owner, and uses AI as a force multiplier across almost every aspect of their work.

You should be deeply comfortable working with AI-powered coding tools and autonomous agents such as **Claude Code, OpenAI Codex, Cursor, GitHub Copilot**, and other emerging AI development tools. You should know how to orchestrate agents, delegate complex engineering tasks, manage parallel workflows, review AI-generated code, debug issues, and take AI-assisted implementations all the way to production.

We value engineers who can take an ambiguous problem, understand the underlying requirements, make sound technical decisions, and independently deliver a complete solution without needing constant direction.

**Our ideal candidate doesn't just use AI to write code. They know how to use AI to engineer, think, investigate, debug, build, and deliver.**

## What You'll Do

### 1. AI-Native Engineering (Core Responsibility)

- Make AI coding agents a fundamental part of your everyday engineering workflow.
- Effectively leverage tools such as Claude Code, OpenAI Codex, Cursor, GitHub Copilot, and other AI development environments.
- Break down complex engineering problems into smaller tasks that can be executed by AI agents.
- Orchestrate multiple AI agents and parallel workflows to accelerate development.
- Build and maintain effective agent workflows, custom skills, rules, instructions, and reusable development processes.
- Use AI for code generation, architecture exploration, debugging, refactoring, testing, documentation, and code reviews.
- Understand how to provide context to AI tools, manage context windows, and get consistently high-quality results.
- Critically evaluate AI-generated code rather than blindly accepting its output.
- Identify opportunities to automate repetitive engineering tasks and improve development velocity.
- Continuously experiment with emerging AI tools and incorporate the ones that meaningfully improve engineering productivity.

### 2. Full-Stack Development

- Design, develop, and maintain end-to-end web applications.
- Build modern, responsive, and performant frontend applications.
- Develop robust backend services, REST APIs, integrations, and asynchronous workflows.
- Work across frontend and backend architectures and make independent technical decisions.
- Integrate third-party services, APIs, authentication systems, and external platforms.
- Write clean, maintainable, scalable code with appropriate testing and documentation.

### 3. Cloud Infrastructure & DevOps

- Deploy, manage, and maintain applications across cloud infrastructure.
- Work with platforms such as AWS, Google Cloud, Azure, or equivalent cloud providers.
- Set up and manage CI/CD pipelines, deployment workflows, and development environments.
- Work with Docker, containers, serverless architectures, and infrastructure automation.
- Monitor application performance, troubleshoot production issues, and improve system reliability.
- Understand infrastructure costs, scalability, security, and operational best practices.

### 4. Ownership & Independent Execution

- Take ownership of projects and features from initial idea through deployment and ongoing maintenance.
- Translate loosely defined business requirements into technical plans and working software.
- Proactively identify problems, bottlenecks, and opportunities for improvement.
- Make informed technical decisions without requiring constant supervision.
- Communicate progress, raise blockers early, and keep stakeholders informed.
- Take responsibility for the quality, reliability, and outcomes of what you build.
- Think beyond assigned tasks and contribute ideas that improve the product and engineering processes.
