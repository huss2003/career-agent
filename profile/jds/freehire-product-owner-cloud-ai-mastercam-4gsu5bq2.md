**Your Role At A Glance**

Mastercam is redefining the future of manufacturing software by evolving from a world-class desktop CAD/CAM system into an intelligent, connected, and cloud-powered platform. We are seeking a Product Owner, Cloud & AI to help drive this transformation, enabling the migration from traditional desktop software to a modern, service-based cloud architecture that integrates AI-powered workflows, automation, and data-driven insights. This role is critical to executing the cloud and AI transformation strategy. You will translate strategic goals into actionable product requirements and user stories, working closely with cross-functional teams to deliver cloud-native capabilities and embed AI assistance into every workflow.

**\*To be considered, applicants must reside in the Krakow, Poland area or Pune, India.**

**How You’ll Drive Success**

***Cloud Platform Ownership***

- Own and manage the product backlog for Mastercam’s cloud platform and connected services, including APIs, telemetry, and synchronization features.
- Define user stories and acceptance criteria for cloud-native capabilities such as collaboration, data sync, and connected toolpath computation.
- Partner with software architects and engineering managers to ensure scalability, performance, and security of new cloud modules.
- Ensure backlog priorities reflect the transformation roadmap set by the Product Manager.

***AI Product Enablement***

- Define and prioritize AI-powered use cases, including intelligent assistants, copilots, predictive machining, and automation insights.
- Collaborate with engineering to operationalize LLMs and generative AI features on secure, scalable cloud platforms.
- Ensure model feedback, telemetry, and user interaction data are leveraged for continuous improvement.
- Work with UX teams to design intuitive, context-aware AI interactions that elevate user experience.

***Cross-Functional Collaboration***

- Translate strategic goals from leadership and stakeholders into clear, actionable roadmaps for development teams.
- Act as the bridge between product development and design, ensuring clarity, alignment, and delivery against timelines.
- Collaborate with QA and DevOps teams to ensure cloud/AI release readiness.
- Participate in sprint planning, backlog grooming, and release reviews to ensure feature readiness and quality.
- Collaborate with Product Marketing and UX to ensure cohesive customer experiences as desktop and cloud modules converge.

***Measurement & Impact***

- Define and track KPIs for backlog velocity, cloud adoption, and AI feature engagement.
- Gather feedback from internal teams, customers, and beta users to refine requirements and improve outcomes.
- Maintain a strong focus on business value delivery, ensuring every cloud or AI feature contributes to user productivity and long-term scalability.

**The Talents We’re Seeking**

***Education***

- Bachelor’s degree in Computer Science, Engineering, related technical field, or equivalent professional experience required.
- Master’s degree preferred.

***Required Skills***

- 5+ years of experience as a Product Owner or Product Manager in software or platform development.
- Generalunderstanding of cloud architectures (Azure/AWS), APIs, and modern SaaS principles.
- Familiarity with AI/ML concepts, particularly LLM integration and AI service deployment.
- Demonstrated success translating high-level strategy into detailed product requirements and technical deliverables.
- Excellent communication and stakeholder management skills.
- Experience working in Agile/Scrum environments with distributed engineering teams.

***Preferred Skills***

- Certified Scrum Product Owner.
- Background in manufacturing software, CAD/CAM, or industrial automation.
- Understanding of telemetry, analytics pipelines, and cloud-to-desktop synchronization.
- Familiarity with PLM systems and hybrid deployment models for consistency with transformation objectives.
