**About the Role:**

We are seeking a highly skilled **AI Engineer with 6**+ years of experience to design, develop, and deploy enterprise-scale AI solutions that solve complex business problems across the organization. The ideal candidate will have strong hands-on experience in building intelligent applications end to end, including data preparation, model development, prompt design and optimization, orchestration of agentic workflows, deployment automation, infrastructure provisioning, monitoring, and production support.
