The Role The Web Development Team, part of Brand, owns beyondtrust.com end to end: a React and Next.js front end backed by an enterprise headless CMS, serving a global cybersecurity audience and the pipeline that runs through it. beyondtrust.com is BeyondTrust's largest go-to-market surface, and this role builds it — the campaign and launch experiences, the shared component system underneath them, and the front-end quality of the whole site. This is a hands-on, mid-level individual contributor role for an engineer who is strong in React, TypeScript and Next.js, has the visual judgment to make what they build look right without every state being specified, and uses AI tooling as an everyday part of how they work. Direction comes from the team; execution and craft are yours. The role reports to the Sr. Manager, UX/UI & Digital Experience. What You’ll Do Build the web experiences behind BeyondTrust's launches, campaigns and new product narratives, working from direction set with Brand, Demand Generation and Product Marketing, and turning it into something live quickly. Work hands-on in the shared component library every week: build new components, extend existing ones, and improve what is there through cleaner APIs, better typing and clearer documentation, and help shape where it goes next. Build for front-end performance across the site, including Core Web Vitals. Build and improve the conversion and demo-request journey and the form experience. Build WCAG 2.1 AA accessibility into components and pages from the start, including automated accessibility checks. Write component and end-to-end tests for your own work. When you see a better way to do something — a pattern, an interaction, a page that should work differently — prototype it far enough that the team can react to a working example. Use AI tooling, including Claude and Claude Code, as a core part of how you design, build, test and review, and help shape how the team uses it. Build AI-powered features into the site as the team takes them on, such as assistive and conversational search, and content recommendation and personalization. Follow secure development practices: handle secrets properly, keep dependencies current, validate input at boundaries, and take part in standing security review. What You’ll Bring 3+ years shipping production React and TypeScript, working in TypeScript strict mode. Solid working knowledge of Next.js App Router, including server versus client components, rendering and caching, and the interest to go deeper. Experience building against a headless CMS or API-driven content, including querying over GraphQL, and building components that hold up when content does something unexpected. Strong CSS, including a utility-first framework, and an eye for layouts that hold together at every breakpoint. Experience working in a shared component library, and opinions about what makes one good to build with. Experience measuring and improving front-end performance. Experience testing your own work with component and end-to-end tests, including accessibility checks. Working knowledge of accessibility implementation: semantics, focus management and keyboard interaction. Good visual judgment and comfort in Figma: able to take design direction and fill in the gaps — spacing, states, responsive behavior — without needing every case specified. Everyday, specific use of AI tooling in your own workflow, with examples you can walk through. Comfort taking a piece of work from brief to shipped in a small team.
