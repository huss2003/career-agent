**Overview**

At Allianz Partners we build AI products for travel, health and assistance, and the platform they run on. As an AI Engineer, you are a software engineer who builds AI systems: Python services and agents that read, check and prepare work such as a customer question or a claim document, extract data from documents and run AI steps inside business processes, reliably and at production scale. We are looking for a strong software engineer first, with production AI experience on top. You design for load, failure and cost, treat evaluation as engineering work and think critically: you question requirements and results and fix root causes. You use coding agents to automate much of your engineering work. You work closely with product owners and business experts, and you run what you ship.

**Key Responsibilities**

- Design, build and run AI systems in Python as production software: typed APIs and services, tests, code review and CI, from first design to operation, including your turn on call.
- Design for scale and failure: concurrency, provider rate limits, caching, durable long-running workflows and retries, with hand-off to a person where the AI should not decide.
- Design agent logic: task breakdown, tool calling, context and state, and prompts with structured output.
- Build evaluation for LLM systems on real cases and judge the results critically, so changes and releases are decided on evidence.
- Build observability into AI systems (traces, quality signals, latency, errors and cost), diagnose problems and fix their root cause.
- Protect data in AI systems: personal and health data, access control, safe logging and defences against prompt injection.
- Choose and upgrade models on measured evidence, balancing quality, latency and cost.
- Use and extend coding agents to automate engineering work such as testing, evaluation and releases

**Required Experience and Skills**

- 5+ years professional software engineering experience, mostly building and running Python systems in production, with hands-on delivery of production AI or LLM-based applications on top.
- Strong software engineering in Python: typed code, Pydantic, async programming, API design, testing and CI (for example GitHub Actions), with services deployed on containers and Kubernetes in Azure.
- System design for production: concurrency, throughput, caching, queues or durable workflows, idempotency and design for failure.
- LLM application experience: structured output, tool calling, retrieval-augmented generation and multi-step agents (for example with Azure OpenAI).
- Experience building evaluation for LLM systems and judging results critically.
- Production operations and data protection: logs, metrics and traces, incident response, and safe handling of personal data.
- Experience using and extending coding agents (for example Claude Code) to automate engineering work, and reviewing what they produce.
- Critical thinking: questions requirements and results, weighs options with measured trade-offs and says clearly what is not known

**Ways of Working**

- You build it, you run it: treats on-call and follow-up as part of the work.
- Thinks critically: looks at the outputs behind a score and fixes root causes instead of patching.
- Measures success by behaviour on real cases in production, not by feature count.
- Explains design choices, risks and open questions in plain words to business colleagues.

**Nice to Have**

- Experience with agent frameworks such as Pydantic AI, and durable workflow engines (for example Temporal).
- Experience with conversational, voice or multilingual AI, particularly in European languages.
- Insurance, assistance or health domain knowledge (claims, policy, medical cases).
- Experience in regulated environments where data handling and access controls are subject to compliance requirements
