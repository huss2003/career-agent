ADDMORE Services LLC is seeking technology-enabled Executive Assistants to provide administrative, organizational, and operational support to senior executives.

This is not a traditional Executive Assistant role. Candidates must combine strong executive support capabilities with practical experience using Artificial Intelligence (AI), automation tools, and modern business applications to improve productivity and streamline business operations.

We are looking for professionals who use AI as a productivity partner, not simply for writing prompts.

## Key Responsibilities

1. Executive Administration: Manage calendars, meetings, emails, correspondence, agendas, minutes, and executive documentation.
2. AI Applications: Use ChatGPT and other AI platforms for research, analysis, report writing, document preparation, and business communications.
3. Business Operations: Coordinate projects, monitor deadlines, maintain trackers, and prepare operational updates.
4. Digital Tools & Automation: Use Microsoft 365, Google Workspace, CRM systems, and project management applications; identify opportunities for workflow automation.
5. Research & Business Development: Conduct market and competitor research, prepare prospect profiles, support LinkedIn activities, and assist with proposals and presentations.
6. Reporting & Documentation: Prepare executive reports, presentations, SOPs, and management summaries.
7. Workflow Improvement: Develop reusable AI prompts and workflows, improve administrative processes, and identify repetitive tasks for automation.
8. Executive Support: Anticipate management requirements, exercise sound judgement, maintain confidentiality, and coordinate with international clients and teams.

## Required Technical Skills

ChatGPT, Microsoft Copilot, Claude / Gemini, Microsoft Office, PowerPoint, Google Workspace, Canva, CRM Platforms, Zapier / Make, Project Management Software.
