**Role Overview**:

BrightEdge is seeking a proactive, self-reliant DevOps Automation Engineer with a strong software development and tooling orientation to eliminate manual toil, repetitive scripting, and operational dependencies.

Your sole mission is to build the software, portals, integrations, and AI-driven workflows that enable our engineering teams to operate at scale. You will be responsible for creating self-service platforms, automated self-healing loops, and leading-edge AI-native workflows that ensure our production systems are fully optimised. This role is built for developers who love infrastructure, thrive on solving complex, unmapped technical challenges, and want to build the future of automated operations.

**What You’ll Do:**

- Build Elite Self-Service Tooling: Design and write custom internal software, APIs, and self-service portals (e.g., dynamic environment provisioning, automated access management) to completely remove human dependencies from the development cycle.
- Engineer AI-Native Operations: Develop and integrate custom AI-assisted automation (using Model Context Protocol / MCP, Bedrock, custom LLM agents) to automate alert triage, code-review gates, log parsing, and incident root-cause analysis (RCA).
- Architect Golden Pipelines: Build, standardise, and scale highly automated, reusable CI/CD templates and GitOps workflows across distributed engineering teams.
- Implement Self-Healing & Auto-Remediation: Write robust event-driven scripts (Python/Go/Bash) and infrastructure policies that automatically detect, quarantine, and remediate operational faults without human intervention.
- Maintain Strict IaC Standards: Ensure 100% of multi-cloud resources and configurations are deployed declaratively via modular Terraform or CloudFormation.
- Drive Cost & Performance Engineering: Continuously identify operational inefficiencies and automate cost-governance workflows (e.g., automated resource pruning, multi-region database replication, and scaling optimisations).

- Unmapped Problem-Solving: Genuinely motivated to step outside your comfort zone, take deep ownership, and aggressively tackle technical challenges you do not yet know how to solve.
