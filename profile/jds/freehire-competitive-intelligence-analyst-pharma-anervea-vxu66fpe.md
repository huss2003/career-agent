Job Title: Competitive Intelligence Analyst, Pharma (AI Enabled)

Location: Pune

Type: Full-time

About Anervea Anervea is the AI arm of GRG Health, a healthcare intelligence company. We build software that helps pharmaceutical teams understand their competitive landscape and make faster, better-informed decisions. Our flagship product, AlfaKinetic, is a competitive intelligence platform for pharma. It brings together more than 110 public data sources with GRG Health's own primary research, so commercial and medical teams can work from one place instead of stitching together scattered reports. We are a small, fast-moving team, and this role works closely with the people building and running the platform.

The Role

You will track the pharma competitive landscape and turn scattered signals into decision-ready insight. You will monitor pipelines, trials, and launches, and use the latest AI tools to research, analyze, and build faster. This is a hands-on role for someone who combines real pharma domain depth with genuine curiosity about AI, and who is comfortable not just using AI tools but building with them. You will work directly with our founders and the CI team, and help shape how a growing CI function operates.

What You'll Do

• Monitor pipelines, trials, regulatory milestones, approvals, launches, and label changes across assigned therapeutic areas.

• Track competitors through trial registries, regulatory filings, congresses, investor decks, earnings calls, and news.

• Turn findings into crisp deliverables: competitor profiles, landscape assessments, event readouts, and CI newsletters.

• Use AI tools to accelerate literature review, document analysis, and drafting, then validate everything with human judgment.

• Build and maintain CI assets such as pipeline trackers and competitor dashboards.

• Support brand, medical, and commercial teams with ad-hoc requests, war-gaming, and scenario analysis.

• Track emerging AI tools and bring practical uses into CI workflows.

What you will be responsible for

• Client output check – 100% of client-facing data checked on UAT and Live instance before it reaches the client

• Hand-audit – Manual audit of source data on Nexus datalake for 1 to 5% of data

• Ticket logging and resolution – Logging data issues in JIRA with reproducibility and priority. Providing solutioning approach to engineering team and closing the loop of for resolution

• Turn around – Data QC on QA to be completed in 24 hrs

• UAT approval – Formal UAT approval for all open bug fixes, data accuracy, coverage and relevance 1 day before Go Live

What You'll Need

• 3+ years in pharma or life-sciences competitive intelligence.

• Bachelor's or Master’s (M.Pharm, MSc, MBA, PharmD, or PhD (Good to have) degree in life sciences, pharmacy, biotech, or a related field. • Strong understanding of drug development, clinical trials, regulatory pathways, and commercial pharma.

• Fluency with core CI sources: ClinicalTrials.gov, regulatory databases, scientific literature, and company disclosures.

• Depth in one Therapy area like oncology, haematology, CNS and neuroscience, immunology, rare diseases, cardiometabolic (Good to have)

• Sharp analytical skills and clear, executive-ready communication.

• Hands-on use of AI tools to create deliverables, for example LLMs for synthesis, drafting, or data extraction, and AI-assisted trackers or workflows (Good to have)

• Basic experience of AI and agentic workflows, with vibe-coding familiarity (e.g. Claude Code) to prototype and ship working outputs (Good to have) • Prompt engineering and validation of AI-generated output (Good to have)

What Makes Someone Great at This

• Domain depth. You know where competitive dynamics matter most.

• Analytical rigor. You separate signal from noise.

• AI fluency. You work faster with AI without losing accuracy.

• Communication. You turn analysis into a clear story.

• Ownership. You are proactive and organized under tight timelines.

• Ethics. You operate within legal and ethical CI boundaries.

What We Offer
