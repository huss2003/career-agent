### What this role looks like at the junior level

You’ll join a team attached to one of our products and learn by shipping. Senior engineers will pair with you, review your work, and invest in your growth - but you’ll have real ownership from early on.

You’ll work across frontend, backend, and mobile: TypeScript, React, Node.js, Angular, MongoDB, and AWS. You don't need to know all of these, willingness to learn matters more than what you know today.

**Two things make engineering different here:**

- You’ll develop a product mindset. Instead of just working through tickets, you’ll start understanding why features matter to customers. Engineers here are closer to the problem than at most companies; your work has context and meaning from day one.
- You’ll build with AI from the start. We’re moving toward agentic engineering where engineers define what to build and orchestrate AI agents to construct it. As a junior here, you’ll develop AI-first habits that most engineers won't learn for years.
