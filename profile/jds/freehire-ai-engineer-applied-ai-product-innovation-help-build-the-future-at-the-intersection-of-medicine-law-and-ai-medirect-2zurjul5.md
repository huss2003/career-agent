You'll be involved in the full engineering lifecycle — from designing solutions and building prototypes through to production deployment, evaluation and continuous improvement.

**You'll report directly to Pri, our Head of Engineering**, working closely with our product and engineering teams to deliver new AI capabilities.

We're looking for someone with strong technical foundations, sound engineering judgement and the confidence to take ownership of meaningful work.
