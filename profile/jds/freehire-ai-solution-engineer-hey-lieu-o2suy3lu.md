## **About the Role**

We are looking for an AI Automation & Integration Engineer to maintain, optimize, and expand our existing automation ecosystem while developing new AI-powered features and intelligent workflows.

The company currently operates on an interconnected technology stack that includes Trello, a custom-built in-house CRM, Jotform, Zapier, and Make. The initial priority for this role will be to understand how these systems work together, troubleshoot existing integrations, identify inefficiencies, and improve the reliability of our current workflows.

Beyond maintenance, this person will help build the next generation of our internal operations by transforming manual, multi-step business processes into intelligent workflows powered by AI agents and automation. The goal is to create systems where agents can execute tasks, coordinate information, interact with external tools, and escalate decisions to humans when necessary.

This is a hands-on engineering role for someone who can work independently, understand inherited systems, make sound architectural decisions, and take AI-powered solutions from concept to production.

## **Key Responsibilities**

**1. Automation Debugging & System Maintenance**

- Take ownership of existing automations and integrations across Trello, the custom CRM, Jotform, Zapier, and Make.
- Diagnose workflow failures, identify root causes, and implement sustainable fixes rather than temporary workarounds.
- Evaluate existing automation architecture and identify opportunities to improve performance, reliability, and maintainability.
- Recommend whether existing workflows should be optimized, repaired, or rebuilt.

**2. Systems Integration & API Development**

- Build and maintain integrations between project management, CRM, form platforms, and external applications.
- Work with REST APIs, webhooks, authentication mechanisms, rate limits, retries, and data mapping.
- Analyze undocumented or custom-built systems by reviewing schemas, existing implementations, and available technical resources.
- Ensure accurate and consistent data synchronization across connected platforms.

**3. AI Agents & Context Engineering**

- Develop AI-powered agents capable of executing multi-step business processes with minimal human intervention.
- Implement context retrieval, memory management, conversation state, and relevant information injection.
- Optimize context windows and retrieval strategies to maintain accuracy and consistency across complex workflows.
- Design human-in-the-loop mechanisms for reviewing outputs, approving sensitive actions, and escalating exceptions.

**4. Workflow Automation & Orchestration**

- Audit, maintain, rebuild, and extend workflows using Zapier and Make.
- Leverage n8n or comparable orchestration platforms when they provide a more effective solution.
- Coordinate AI models, APIs, external tools, and business logic across multi-step workflows.
- Design task decomposition, execution sequences, dependencies, error handling, and recovery mechanisms.

**5. AI Feature Development**

- Build new internal tools, integrations, and automation features that support business operations.
- Develop maintainable solutions using Python, TypeScript, or comparable programming languages.
- Integrate LLMs and third-party APIs based on performance, reliability, complexity, and cost considerations.
- Translate operational requirements into practical technical solutions.

**6. Testing, Evaluation & Optimization**

- Test AI agents against representative business scenarios and measurable performance criteria.
- Identify failure patterns and improve accuracy, consistency, response times, and overall reliability.
- Validate structured outputs, tool calling, and automated decision-making.
- Continuously refine prompts, workflows, and agent behavior based on real-world performance.

**7. Deployment & Production Monitoring**

- Take solutions from prototype through testing, deployment, and ongoing optimization.
- Monitor production workflows, troubleshoot failures, and implement safeguards.
- Maintain version control, technical documentation, and code quality standards.
- Apply appropriate security practices when handling credentials, API keys, and sensitive business information.

**8. Continuous Technical Improvement**

- Stay current with emerging AI models, agent frameworks, orchestration tools, and context engineering practices.
- Evaluate new technologies and determine their practical value for the company's existing infrastructure.
- Identify opportunities to replace repetitive manual processes with reliable, AI-assisted workflows.
