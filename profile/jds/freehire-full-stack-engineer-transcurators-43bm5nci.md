**The Opportunity :**

Credit and asset-based finance still runs on manual document review, spreadsheets and judgment calls made under time pressure. Converge is rebuilding that workflow from the ground up: an AI-powered platform that combines automation, intelligent document processing and credit decisioning.

This is an early, high-ownership engineering seat. There is no legacy codebase to maintain and no layer of process between you and the product. You will help shape the architecture, and the decisions you make will show up in how real financial institutions operate.

**What You Will Own :**

\- Product surfaces : Design and ship the interfaces that underwriters and credit teams rely on in critical workflows.

\- Platform backbone : Architect backend services and APIs that stay fast and reliable as data volumes and customers grow.

\- Document intelligence : Build pipelines that turn unstructured financial documents into clean, trustworthy, decision-ready data.

\- Applied AI in production : Take LLM and automation capabilities from prototype to systems that hold up under real-world load.

\- Data at scale : Model, query and optimise large, complex datasets in PostgreSQL.

\- Engineering direction : Influence technical choices on performance, reliability and scalability from day one.

**Engineering Environment :**

\- Frontend : React

\- Backend : Node.js, Python

\- Data and APIs : PostgreSQL, GraphQL

\- Infrastructure : Cloud-native, with modern engineering practices as a core part of how we build
