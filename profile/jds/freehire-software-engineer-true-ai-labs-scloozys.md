**About True AI Labs**

True AI Labs builds custom enterprise AI SaaS, develops small and large language models for specialized use cases that can be deployed on the cloud, devices, and on-site, and offers a consumer AI assistant that handles calls, reservations, bookings, and daily tasks for businesses and consumers worldwide.

We build AI products and solutions that help businesses improve customer engagement, automate workflows, and leverage AI across their operations. Our work spans Voice AI agents, on-device Small Language Models (SLMs), intelligent automation, AI-powered applications, and SaaS products.
