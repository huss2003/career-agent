# **Job Title**

**Generative AI Developer / Engineer/Analyst**

## **Experience**

- **2–3 years of hands-on experience** in **Generative AI / LLM-based solutions**
- **2–3 years of experience** in **data analysis, business analysis, or analytics-driven roles**
- **1–3 years of experience** in **prompt engineering**

## **Role Summary**

We are looking for a **Generative AI Developer** who can build **end-to-end, production-grade applications** using Generative AI. The ideal candidate is a **strong technologist** with experience across **web applications, data analysis, and automation**, and who understands that GenAI development is as much about **language, data, and business context** as it is about code.

This role focuses on building solutions using **internal / private LLMs**, emphasizing **security, reliability, and enterprise integration** over public API experimentation.

## **Key Responsibilities**

### Generative AI & Prompt Engineering

- Design, develop, and optimize applications leveraging **internal LLM platforms**
- Create, test, and maintain **high-quality prompts** to drive accurate and predictable outputs
- Apply advanced prompting techniques including:
  
  - Few-shot prompting
  - Prompt chaining
  - RAG (Retrieval-Augmented Generation)
- Establish prompt evaluation and versioning practices

### End-to-End Application Development

- Build **full-stack, end-to-end GenAI applications**
- Design and implement backend services integrating LLMs with enterprise systems
- Develop or collaborate on **web-based user interfaces**
- Integrate GenAI capabilities into existing applications and workflows

### Web & Technology Stack

- Work with modern **web technologies**, such as:
  
  - REST APIs
  - Web frameworks (React, Angular, Vue, or equivalent)
  - Backend services (Node.js, Java, Python, .NET, etc.)
- Understand client-server architecture and web security fundamentals
- Build scalable, maintainable application architectures

### Data, Analysis & Automation

- Analyze structured and unstructured data for GenAI use cases
- Translate business requirements into **LLM-driven workflows**
- Perform data validation, transformation, and analysis to support AI pipelines
- Collaborate with automation teams; **experience with Blue Prism is a plus**

### Enterprise & Governance

- Build solutions aligned with **enterprise security, compliance, and governance standards**
- Work with private/internal LLMs, ensuring data privacy and controlled usage
- Document design decisions, prompts, and system behavior clearly

## **Required Skills & Qualifications**

### Core Requirements

- **2–3 years experience** building **GenAI / LLM-based applications**
- **1–3 years hands-on experience** with **prompt engineering**
- Strong **written English skills** (critical for prompt design and documentation)
- **Data analysis or business analysis experience** (2–3 years)
- Ability to code in **at least one modern programming language** (language-agnostic mindset)
- Experience building **end-to-end applications**, not just models or scripts

### Technical Skills

- Experience with:
  
  - Internal / private LLM platforms
  - RAG architectures and vector search
  - APIs and service-oriented architecture
- Solid understanding of:
  
  - LLM limitations (hallucinations, bias, drift)
  - Evaluation and validation of model outputs
  - Cost, latency, and performance trade-offs

## **Preferred / Nice-to-Have Skills**

- **Blue Prism RPA experience**
- Experience with **enterprise web applications**
- Familiarity with **LLMOps / MLOps** concepts
- Knowledge of **document-heavy domains** (contracts, agreements, reports)
- Experience working in **regulated or enterprise environments**

## **Mindset & Attributes**

- Strong analytical and problem-solving skills
- Comfortable working across **language, data, and code**
- Business-focused with an outcome-driven mindset
- Able to explain GenAI behavior to technical and non-technical audiences

## **Why Join Us**

- Build **real, production-grade GenAI applications**
- Work with **secure, internal LLM platforms**
- Influence how GenAI is adopted responsibly at scale
- Be part of an end-to-end engineering team, not a siloed AI lab
