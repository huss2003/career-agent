**Job Purpose**

The Power Platform Developer II designs, builds, and supports low-code business solutions on the Microsoft Power Platform — primarily Power Apps, Power Automate, and Copilot Studio — replacing manual and spreadsheet-driven processes with secure, scalable, supportable applications, automations, and AI agents. Partnering with business stakeholders and IT teammates, this developer translates requirements into working solutions and owns small to mid-sized efforts end to end with moderate guidance from senior developers and architects, working within established environment, data, and governance standards. The role balances new delivery with the enhancement and support of an existing solution portfolio, and contributes reusable components, patterns, and documentation to the broader maker community.
