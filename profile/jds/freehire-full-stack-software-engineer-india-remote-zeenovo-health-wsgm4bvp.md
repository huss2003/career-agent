1\. About ZeeNovo Health®

ZeeNovo Health® is a Canadian healthcare technology platform developing connected software solutions for community pharmacies.

Our software supports clinical assessments, appointment booking, medication safety, compounding, quality improvement, documentation, analytics, and pharmacy operations.

Our platform includes:

- **ZeeNovo Clinical:** Minor ailments, immunizations, medication injections, automated follow-ups, patient documentation and clinical analytics.
- **ZeeNovo Assure:** Medication incident reporting, continuous quality improvement, pharmacy assessments, corrective actions and compliance-related workflows.
- **ZeeNovo Magistral:** Pharmacy compounding management (Beta).
- **Enterprise & Partner Solutions:** Centralized booking, pharmacy group administration, permissions and consolidated analytics.
- **Medication Reviews and POCT:** Additional clinical services under development.

We are looking for developers capable of contributing to an existing production platform, improving reliability, and developing new features with minimal supervision.

Because ZeeNovo processes sensitive health information, candidates must understand secure software development.

Required capabilities:

- Authentication, MFA and secure session management.
- Role-based and resource-level authorization.
- Encryption in transit and at rest.
- Secure API access and input validation.
- Protection against SQL injection, XSS, CSRF and common OWASP vulnerabilities.
- Secure file uploads and document access.
- Audit logs for access, modifications and workflow activities.
- Tenant isolation and testing against unauthorized cross-pharmacy access.
- Secure handling of application credentials, secrets and tokens.
- Backup, recovery and incident investigation practices.
- Protection against automated registration abuse and API misuse.
- Preventing patient information from appearing in development logs, error reports or unapproved third-party tools.

Developers are expected to help ZeeNovo implement technical safeguards appropriate to applicable Canadian privacy requirements, including Ontario's PHIPA and PIPEDA where applicable.

**Production patient data must not be copied to personal computers, unmanaged development environments or unauthorized AI services.**

Access to production systems must follow company authorization and least-privilege policies.
