**ABOUT THE ROLE**

You will own the hub (Next.js, Supabase, Netlify), our supply chain app, and the scheduled jobs and integrations that connect them to Slack, ClickUp, Zendesk, Shopify and our 3PL. You report to the COO and work with every department head who needs something built. You inherit a large, working codebase written fast, so the job is to make it reliable and safe without stopping it.

**NOTE: This is a remote role under an independent contractor agreement.** 40 hours a week, Monday to Friday, 09:00 to 18:00 CET, with a one-hour break.

**Responsibilities:**

**Keeping it running:**

- Own the uptime and correctness of the hub, the supply chain app, the Slack bot and every scheduled job, so a break is found by you, not by the team.
- Add monitoring and alerts for the things that fail quietly: webhooks that stop arriving, syncs that skip rows, jobs that never ran.
- Fix what breaks the same working day, and write down what happened and what changed.

**Keeping it secure:**

- Own access: sign-in, who sees what, row-level security, and removing a leaver's access everywhere on their last day.
- Keep secrets out of code, rotate them, and keep dependencies patched.
- Protect the sensitive data the hub holds: pay, invoices, candidates and personal details.

**Building what comes next:**

- Run the request queue: agree the week's priorities with the COO, then ship them through staging to production.
- Tell people when a request is a bad idea, and offer something better.
- Make pages people can use without asking how: a clear layout, plain labels, and screens that work on a phone.
- Build integrations and AI tooling, including MCP servers so our AI agents can read internal data directly.
- Leave every system documented and tested well enough that someone else could take it over in a week.
