We are seeking an experienced Senior Software Engineer to contribute to the modernization, security, and development of scalable web applications. You will initially focus on maintaining and upgrading Drupal-based platforms, ensuring high standards of performance, reliability, and security. As modernization priorities progress, you will expand your responsibilities into C#/.NET backend development and full-stack engineering initiatives. ### Accountabilities:

- Lead the maintenance, modernization, and ongoing support of Drupal-based platforms, including version upgrades, security patches, troubleshooting, and performance improvements.
- Design, develop, and scale backend services using C#, .NET Core, RESTful APIs, and service-oriented or event-driven architectures.
- Build and enhance modern frontend applications using React, Redux, TypeScript, and JavaScript.
- Develop reliable, high-performance web applications capable of supporting high-traffic environments and large-scale data processing.
- Leverage AI-assisted development tools such as Cursor, Claude, and GitHub Copilot to improve coding, debugging, testing, and engineering efficiency.
- Develop AI agents using modern large language models (LLMs) and contribute to AI/ML-powered product features.
- Monitor, troubleshoot, and resolve production issues while contributing to incident response, system resilience, and continuous improvement.
- Collaborate with Product, Design, and Data teams throughout the product development lifecycle, from initial discovery to launch.
- Establish and maintain comprehensive unit, integration, and end-to-end testing practices to ensure software quality and reliability.
- Participate in technical and architectural reviews, share knowledge with engineering colleagues, and promote clean code and engineering best practices.

## Requirements:

- At least 6 years of professional web development experience, with strong expertise in Drupal, PHP, C#, .NET Core, REST APIs, and service-oriented or event-driven architectures.
- Minimum 2 years of hands-on Drupal experience, including platform maintenance, troubleshooting, database management, and version upgrades.
- At least 2 years of professional PHP development experience, preferably involving Drupal customization, module development, and maintenance.
- Practical experience using AI and LLM-powered tools for software development activities, including coding, debugging, testing, code interpretation, analysis, and documentation.
- Strong background in designing and developing complex web applications, high-volume systems, and solutions involving large-scale data processing.
- Solid understanding of software architecture patterns, scalable system design, clean coding principles, and maintainable web services.
- Strong problem-solving and analytical skills, combined with the ability to independently manage technical deliverables from investigation through implementation and testing.
- Excellent communication and collaboration skills, with the ability to work effectively across multidisciplinary and geographically distributed teams.
- Ability to manage competing priorities, take ownership of technical challenges, and adapt to evolving project requirements.

**Nice to have:**
