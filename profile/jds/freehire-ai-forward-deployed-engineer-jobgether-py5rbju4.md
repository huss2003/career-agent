### Accountabilities

As an AI Forward Deployed Engineer, you will take ownership of developing reliable AI infrastructure and agent capabilities, collaborating with cross-functional teams to turn innovative ideas into scalable production services. Your responsibilities will include:

- Design, build, deploy, and operate production-grade services that enable AI agents to perform tasks reliably.
- Develop internal tools and applications that improve employee productivity and streamline everyday workflows.
- Build external integrations, connectors, and tools that simplify how customers interact with and integrate services into AI-powered applications.
- Develop reusable agent primitives and capabilities, including meeting participation, web browsing, search, browser automation, email, and payment services.
- Establish reliable interfaces, workflows, and protocols for agent tool usage and orchestration.
- Collaborate closely with product and engineering teams to translate emerging AI agent use cases into practical, maintainable services.
- Strengthen platform safety, observability, testing, security, and operational reliability.
- Contribute to the overall architecture, technical strategy, and evolution of the agent enablement platform.
- Ensure that AI agents can interact with external systems securely, manage permissions appropriately, and handle actions with real-world consequences.
  
  **Requirements**
  
  The ideal candidate is a hands-on software engineer with strong production engineering experience, practical knowledge of applied AI, and the ability to turn ambiguous technical challenges into reliable products. You should bring:
  
  - Strong software engineering experience, including ownership and maintenance of production systems.
  - Practical experience working with Large Language Models (LLMs), AI agents, tool calling, and agent orchestration.
  - A solid understanding of designing reliable, scalable, and maintainable software services.
  - Good technical judgment regarding system reliability, security, access permissions, and external side effects.
  - The ability to navigate ambiguous problems, evaluate emerging technologies, and turn early-stage ideas into robust engineering solutions.
  - Strong problem-solving and analytical skills, with a practical and hands-on approach to development.
  - Clear communication and the ability to collaborate effectively with product and engineering teams.
  - Experience with agent frameworks and runtimes such as Hermes Agent or OpenClaw is considered a plus.
  - An interest in developing secure, dependable AI-powered products and shaping the future of agent-based applications.
