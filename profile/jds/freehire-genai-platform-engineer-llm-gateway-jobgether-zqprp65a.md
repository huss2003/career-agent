**This position is listed on behalf of a partner company, who manages all applications and next steps. Our partner is looking for a GenAI Platform Engineer – LLM Gateway based in India.**

We are seeking an experienced GenAI Platform Engineer to help build, operate, and continuously improve an enterprise-scale generative AI platform. In this role, you will contribute to the development of an LLM Gateway that provides secure, scalable, and governed access to multiple large language model providers. You will design and maintain backend services, model integrations, routing capabilities, and usage management features while supporting advanced agentic AI solutions. Working within a globally distributed engineering team, you will focus on platform reliability, security, performance, and automation. You will also contribute to open-source technologies, developer enablement, and innovative AI tooling. This is an exciting individual contributor opportunity to shape the future of enterprise GenAI infrastructure in a fast-evolving technological environment.

**Accountabilities**

- Design, develop, and operate enterprise LLM Gateway features, primarily using Python, enabling secure and scalable access to multiple large language model providers.
- Build and maintain multi-provider model integrations, intelligent routing, failover mechanisms, API key management, and access control capabilities.
- Develop platform features for usage metering, budget management, cost transparency, and chargeback reporting.
- Integrate agentic AI capabilities into the platform while ensuring appropriate security and governance controls.
- Develop and maintain Model Context Protocol (MCP) servers, cross-provider tool calling, and agent-to-agent connectivity.
- Maintain and enhance the platform's open-source foundations by assessing upstream releases, managing custom extensions, and delivering upgrades without introducing regressions.
- Contribute to administrative interfaces, self-service portals, developer experiences, and technical documentation.
- Build and maintain automated quality gates within CI/CD pipelines, including unit, integration, contract, and performance testing.
- Implement observability solutions using metrics, distributed tracing, and logging tools such as OpenTelemetry and Langfuse.
- Monitor platform performance and proactively identify and resolve latency, reliability, error-rate, and infrastructure cost issues.
- Participate in production support and on-call rotations, leading root-cause investigations and resolving complex incidents.
- Conduct blameless postmortems and translate incident findings into sustainable engineering improvements.
- Apply secure-by-default engineering practices, including Single Sign-On (SSO), OAuth 2.0/OIDC, Role-Based Access Control (RBAC), and secure secrets management.
- Maintain dependency security, version pinning, and software supply-chain hygiene.
- Evaluate emerging generative AI technologies, including model routing solutions, guardrails, agent registries, and agentic frameworks, and provide recommendations for adoption.
- Support internal engineering teams in onboarding and effectively using the platform through technical guides, runbooks, code samples, and reusable AI agent skills.
- Contribute to InnerSource initiatives, participate in code reviews, and collaborate with open-source communities and technology vendors.
- Identify opportunities to improve platform capabilities through upstream contributions, technical experimentation, and continuous innovation.
