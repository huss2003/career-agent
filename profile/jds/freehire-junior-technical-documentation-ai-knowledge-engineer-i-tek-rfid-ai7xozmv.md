**Experience:** Fresher / 0–1 Year

**Preferred Background:** Candidates with strong English-medium schooling ***\[preferably convent schools]***, excellent written and spoken English, and a flair for technical writing.

**Role Overview**

We are looking for **bright, enthusiastic engineering graduates** who have excellent English communication skills, a good understanding of technology, and a passion for writing, learning, and organizing information.

This is an excellent opportunity for freshers who want to build a long-term career in **Technical Documentation, Product Knowledge Management, and AI-powered Documentation Systems**.

The selected candidate will work closely with our software development and product teams to understand our products and convert technical information into clear, structured, and easy-to-understand documentation.

The role will also provide exposure to modern AI tools, knowledge management systems, and AI-assisted documentation practices.

**\** We are not looking for an experienced technical writer. We are looking for an engineering graduate with strong English skills, technical curiosity, attention to detail, and the willingness to learn and grow\*\***

**Key Responsibilities**

**1. Technical Documentation**

- Learn and understand our software products, features, workflows, and technical architecture.
- Prepare and maintain user manuals, product guides, technical documents, FAQs, and training materials.
- Convert technical discussions and developer inputs into simple, well-structured documentation.
- Ensure documentation is accurate, consistent, and easy to understand.

**2. Product Knowledge Management**

- Help build and maintain a centralized repository of product knowledge.
- Organize information related to product features, APIs, configurations, workflows, and implementations.
- Coordinate with developers, testers, and product teams to collect and document information.
- Keep documentation updated as products evolve.

**3. AI-Assisted Documentation**

- Learn and use AI tools such as ChatGPT, Copilot, and other AI-based documentation assistants.
- Explore how AI can help create user manuals, training guides, FAQs, and technical content.
- Assist in preparing structured product knowledge that can be used by AI-powered knowledge systems.
- Gradually gain exposure to LLMs, Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG), and AI knowledge management.

**4. Collaboration & Continuous Learning**

- Work closely with engineering and product teams to understand software functionality.
- Participate in product demonstrations, knowledge-transfer sessions, and technical discussions.
- Develop an understanding of software architecture, databases, APIs, and development processes.
- Continuously improve technical writing, documentation, and AI-related skills.
