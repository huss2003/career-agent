**Job Description: AI / Machine Learning Engineer:** **Graduate (Freshers) and Experienced Engineers**

**Location:** Remote / Hybrid (as applicable)

**Employment Type:** Full-Time

**Open to:** Fresh graduates through to experienced AI/ML engineers

**Why This Role**

AI is changing how every business works, and we want people who want to build it, not just read about it. As an **AI / Machine Learning Engineer** at Baseel, you will design, build, and ship real AI products LLM-powered applications, Generative AI workflows, and production ML systems — used by clients in more than 100 countries.

Whether you are a **fresh graduate** with strong Python skills and a few projects you are proud of, or an **experienced engineer** who has already put models into production, there is a clear place for you on this team. You will work on live projects from the start, learn from the people around you, and see your work used by real customers.

**Choose Your Track**

**Graduate Track (Freshers)**

- For final-year students and recent graduates (0–1 year of experience).
- Work on live AI projects from your first weeks — not dummy tasks.
- Learn alongside experienced engineers through pairing, code reviews, and regular feedback.
- Build hands-on skills in LLMs, RAG, prompt engineering, APIs, and deployment.
- Grow into a full AI/ML Engineer role as your skills and impact develop.

**Experienced Track**

- For engineers with professional experience in AI/ML or backend/software engineering.
- Own the design and delivery of AI features and services end to end.
- Shape architecture decisions for LLM, RAG, and MLOps solutions on enterprise projects.
- Mentor graduate engineers and help set engineering standards.
- Work directly with clients and product teams on high-impact problems.

**What You Will Do**

- Design, develop, and deploy **Machine Learning and Generative AI** models for production use.
- Build and optimise AI-powered backend services and REST APIs using **Django** and **FastAPI**.
- Integrate **Large Language Models (LLMs)**, NLP models, and other AI services into web applications and enterprise workflows.
- Build data preprocessing, feature engineering, training, validation, and evaluation pipelines.
- Apply **prompt engineering** and AI orchestration to build Generative AI applications.
- Test, benchmark, and improve model accuracy, inference latency, and scalability.
- Work with Product Managers, UI/UX Designers, Data Engineers, and Developers to turn business needs into working AI solutions.
- Document architecture, model designs, experiments, and APIs so others can build on your work.

Freshers will start with well-scoped tasks within these areas and take on more ownership over time; experienced engineers will lead them.

**What We Are Looking For**

**Everyone**

- Bachelor’s degree in Computer Science, Software Engineering, AI, Data Science, or a related field (final-year students are welcome to apply).
- Strong programming skills in **Python**.
- Curiosity about AI and a habit of learning new tools quickly.
- Good problem-solving, debugging, and communication skills.
- Familiarity with **Git** and basic software development practices.

**Freshers — we would love to see**

- Academic, personal, hackathon, or internship projects using ML or LLMs (a GitHub or portfolio link helps).
- Basic understanding of ML concepts and a framework such as **scikit-learn, TensorFlow, or PyTorch**.
- Some exposure to **Pandas, NumPy, SQL**, or building a simple API.
- No prior industry experience required — show us what you have built.

**Experienced engineers — you should bring**

- Professional experience in AI/ML engineering or a related software engineering role.
- Hands-on experience with **TensorFlow** or **PyTorch** and with backend development in **Django** and **FastAPI**.
- Practical knowledge of:
  
  - Natural Language Processing (NLP)
  - Large Language Models (LLMs) and prompt engineering
  - AI model integration using APIs and SDKs
- Experience with SQL databases, **Pandas**, and **NumPy**.
- Understanding of REST APIs, microservices, and backend architecture.

**Bonus Points (Any Track)**

- Retrieval-Augmented Generation (**RAG**) architectures.
- Vector databases such as **Pinecone, Weaviate, Milvus, or ChromaDB**.
- Orchestration frameworks such as **LangChain** or **LlamaIndex**.
- Deployment with **Docker** and **Kubernetes**.
- Cloud platforms: **AWS, Azure, or Google Cloud**.
- **CI/CD and MLOps** practices.
- AI security, responsible AI principles, and model monitoring.

Don’t tick every box? Apply anyway. We value potential and willingness to learn as much as a perfect match.
