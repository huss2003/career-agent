**About the Role**

Newsweek is looking for a Frontend Software Engineer to help build, maintain, and enhance the digital experiences that serve our readers across our publishing platforms.

In this role, you will focus primarily on **React, Next.js, TypeScript, and modern frontend engineering**, developing fast, responsive, accessible, and scalable web applications that deliver content to a large and diverse audience.

You will work on a modern, API-driven frontend architecture, building reusable components, integrating content and backend services, and optimizing the performance and reliability of our digital products.

We are looking for an engineer who combines strong frontend fundamentals with a modern, AI-assisted approach to software development. You should be comfortable leveraging AI tools to accelerate development, improve code quality, solve problems, and streamline engineering workflows while maintaining ownership of the solutions you deliver.

The ideal candidate has solid experience building production-grade applications with React and Next.js, understands modern web performance and frontend architecture, and can collaborate effectively with design, product, backend, and engineering teams.
