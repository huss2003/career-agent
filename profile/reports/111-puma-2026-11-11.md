Your mission

About the Organisation & Role:
At PUMA, technology plays a critical role in enabling exceptional consumer, digital, and business experiences across global markets. As a Software Engineer (Full Stack), you will build and maintain modern web applications and backend services that power business-critical and consumer-facing experiences. You will work closely with Product Managers, Designers, Architects, Data Engineers, and fellow engineers to deliver intuitive, scalable, secure, and high-performing solutions while contributing to engineering best practices across the organization.

What you will do:

- Design, develop, and maintain modern web applications using React, TypeScript, and modern frontend development practices.
- Build responsive, accessible, and user-friendly interfaces that deliver consistent experiences across devices and browsers.
- Develop reusable UI components and contribute to shared frontend libraries and design systems.
- Integrate frontend applications with REST APIs, backend services, and third-party platforms.
- Contribute to backend services and APIs using Java, Spring Boot, and microservices-based architectures.
- Collaborate with product managers, designers, and engineering teams to translate business requirements into high-quality software solutions.
- Participate in application performance optimization, troubleshooting, debugging, and incident resolution activities.
- Contribute to application security, quality assurance, automated testing, and code review practices.
- Work with cloud-native platforms, CI/CD pipelines, and modern software development tools to improve delivery efficiency.
- Leverage AI-assisted engineering tools and modern development practices to enhance productivity and code quality.
- Participate in Agile ceremonies and contribute to continuous improvement initiatives within the team.

- Experience building responsive and accessible web applications with a focus on usability and performance.
