We built an AI that runs the first round of a job interview out loud. It asks the questions, listens, follows up on what the candidate actually said, and shows the evidence behind every score.

InterviewSparrow builds each interview from the candidate's resume and the job description. The candidate speaks, not types, and shares their screen for the part where they show their work. About three minutes after the call ends, the report is ready: a verdict, seven scored dimensions, and under every score the exact sentence the candidate said. Candidates use it to practice. Hiring teams use the same interview as their AI first round.

The product is live. We're hiring a Software Developer to build it with us.

What you'll do
- Build features across the stack: React on the front end, Node and Express services, MongoDB.
- Work on the parts people touch every day: interview setup, the live session screen, the report pages.
- Keep pages fast and stable, and fix what breaks.
- Write tests and review your teammates' code.
- Pick up the LLM side with LangChain as you go, alongside our senior engineers.

What you bring
- You have built web apps with MongoDB, Express, React and Node, at work or in projects people actually use.
- You write clear code, and you read other people's code carefully.
- You can explain what you built and why, in plain words.
- Nice to have: LLM APIs or LangChain, TypeScript, tests with Jest or Vitest, AWS.

There's no years-of-experience bar. We care about what you've built, not how long it took.

What you'll get
- ₹15-25 LPA.
- Fully remote, anywhere in India.
- A live AI product, and a small team where your work ships the same week.
- Room to grow into the AI side of the product.

Benefits: Work from home
Work Location: Remote
