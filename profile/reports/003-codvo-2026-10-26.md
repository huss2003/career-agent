- Develop backend services using Node.js, Python, Java, or C#.

- Design and maintain REST APIs, business logic, authentication, and authorization.
- Design databases, optimize queries, and manage transactions.

- Azure/AWS, Docker, Redis, RabbitMQ, Kafka, and MongoDB.
