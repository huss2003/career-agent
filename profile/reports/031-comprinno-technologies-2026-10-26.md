# About Comprinno

Comprinno is a NASSCOM-incubated company headquartered in Bangalore, with offices in Pune, Coimbatore, and the United States. We specialize in cloud transformation, DevOps, infrastructure automation, and Artificial Intelligence, enabling organizations to build scalable, secure, and high-performing cloud environments on AWS.

Our flagship SaaS platform, **Tevico**, offers intelligent cloud governance and observability for AWS workloads. It helps enterprises improve uptime, reduce costs, and ensure compliance by proactively detecting anomalies and triggering automated remediation workflows.

As an AWS Advanced Consulting Partner, Comprinno helps organizations migrate, modernize, secure, and operate cloud environments while adopting emerging technologies such as Generative AI and Agentic AI.

# About the Role

We are seeking a motivated and technically strong **Data & AI Engineer** to contribute to data-driven and AI-powered solutions for enterprise customers and internal platforms.

In this role, you will work across data engineering, machine learning, Generative AI, and cloud-native analytics solutions while collaborating with architects, senior engineers, and customer teams. You will gain hands-on experience building production-grade data pipelines, AI applications, and cloud-native analytics solutions using AWS technologies.

This role is ideal for professionals who want to build a career in Data Engineering, Machine Learning, Applied AI, MLOps, and Generative AI.

# Key Responsibilities

## Data Engineering & Analytics

- Assist in building and maintaining data pipelines for customer and internal projects.
- Perform data preparation, cleansing, transformation, and feature engineering activities.

- Support development of scalable data processing workflows and analytics solutions.
- Ensure data quality, reliability, and consistency across projects.

- Explore emerging AI tools, frameworks, and orchestration platforms.
