About the role:

The Intelligent Automation Developer will serve as a primary hands-on technical resource supporting Boston Scientific's Robotic Process Automation (RPA) portfolio as part of the HR AI & Automation product team.

The role will focus primarily on the development, enhancement and technical support of Automation Anywhere A360 solutions across a portfolio of more than 100 production automations. Approximately 75% of the role will focus on hands-on development, with additional responsibility for production troubleshooting, defect resolution, enhancements and continued improvement of existing solutions.

This position requires demonstrated hands-on Automation Anywhere A360 development experience and the ability to enter a complex, established automation environment and contribute quickly. The developer must be comfortable understanding solutions developed by others, diagnosing technical issues, modifying existing automations and designing new solutions using scalable and maintainable development patterns.

While RPA is the primary focus, the developer will also support other intelligent automation capabilities, including low-code/no-code solutions, conversational AI/automation and configuration within the Moveworks.ai platform.

* Serve as a primary hands-on Automation Anywhere A360 developer supporting the delivery and ongoing operation of enterprise RPA solutions.
* Design, develop, test, deploy and maintain new Automation Anywhere A360 solutions based on documented business and technical requirements.
* Understand, troubleshoot and enhance existing automations developed by other resources across a mature portfolio of more than 100 production automated processes.
* Develop scalable and maintainable solutions using reusable components, configuration-driven design and established development standards.
* Design and implement integrations using REST APIs and other appropriate integration methods.
* Apply effective exception handling, logging, configuration management and troubleshooting practices to improve solution resiliency and supportability.
* Diagnose production defects, perform root-cause analysis and implement controlled fixes and enhancements.
* Partner closely with business systems analysts to translate business requirements, process maps, user stories and acceptance criteria into technical solutions.
* Provide technical input during requirements and solution design.
* Identify opportunities to refactor or enhance existing automations to improve reliability, scalability, performance and maintainability.
* Perform unit testing, support user acceptance testing and participate in peer reviews.
* Develop and maintain technical documentation, configuration documentation and operational support materials.
* Support configuration, troubleshooting and development activities within the Moveworks.ai platform and other low-code/no-code automation technologies as needed.
* Collaborate with developers, analysts and AI product resources across the HR AI & Automation product team.
* Follow established development standards, governance, security requirements and change-control practices.
* Contribute to the continued evolution of intelligent automation development standards, reusable assets and technical best practices.
